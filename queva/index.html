Queva, alpha 1.0

Queva, in my own words.

Queva is an assistant you text in iMessage or Telegram. It runs on Hermes, open-source agent software, with my setup on top: one private server per owner, a memory that keeps your prices, people and the way you talk, and rules that keep it to plain words and one question per text. It drafts texts, quotes and replies for you to send, keeps your lists, looks things up with sources, and builds small tools you open from a link. It is alpha: new, improving, and watched by me.

What it does.

Drafts

Texts, quotes, review replies and follow-ups in your voice. You check them and send them yourself.

Keeps your lists

Jobs, people, prices and to-dos, saved as plain files in your own projects folder.

Looks things up

Answers with the sources it used, so you can check them.

Builds small tools

Learns you slowly

Stays in plain words

One question per text, no tech terms. If it does, tell it, and I tune it.

Why Hermes.

Queva 1.0 runs on Hermes, open-source agent software. Ask it and it tells you the version.

I picked open source so the core is not a black box, and so your assistant does not depend on one company's app. My part is the setup on top: the server, the memory, the rules, the testing and the care.

What memory means.

Two kinds

Lists and notes are saved as plain files in your projects folder. A memory system on your server keeps facts like your prices and how you talk.

See it and fix it

Your private page has a Memory tab where you can read and erase notes. You can also text it to forget something. The deeper store is cleared when you ask.

What it will not keep

It does not guess or store your age, health, religion, politics, race, immigration status or money situation.

Where it lives

On your server, in your account. The AI provider processes the messages it needs to answer you.

What it will not do.

It never reads your email inbox. It has its own inbox for things you forward to it.

In normal use it does not send, pay or publish on its own. It drafts and you send.

It refuses payments, payroll, taxes, medical records, legal files, card numbers and bank logins.

By default it does not sign in to your accounts.

How I test it.

Before a change reaches you, I run it through pretend owners: a tile installer, a salon, a parent. They text Queva, and a separate AI grades whether the owner got what they wanted. Then I try the change on real phones.

In the first round on October 4, 2026 (100 chats) the owner got what they wanted 71% of the time. After fixes, the next three rounds (23 chats each) scored 83%, 83% and 78%.

Please read those numbers with care: the owners are simulated, the grader is an AI, the samples are small, and a single run can move a few points. They are not a promise about your results. They show me where to fix things, like plain words instead of tech terms and one question per text.

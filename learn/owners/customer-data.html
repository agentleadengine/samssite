Keep customer data safe

Track
Owners · lesson 07 of 16
Reading time
3 min
Last checked
28 Sep 2026
Marks
Checked My take

Before you paste customer information into any AI tool, know what kind of account you are using and what that vendor says about training. A privacy setting is useful, but it is not a reason to paste everything. Keep the sensitive material out unless your company has approved the tool and the use.

Consumer and business accounts are different

Consumer accounts are designed for an individual. Their data settings may let the user control whether conversations are used to improve a service. Business accounts are designed for organizational work and usually have different default data handling, administration, and agreements. Check the terms for the exact plan you are using, not just the brand name.

If your team uses a personal account for a quick trial, treat that work as public enough that it must be sanitized. Replace names with roles, remove addresses and identifying numbers, and use an invented example when you only need help with the shape of a message.

OpenAI says personal Free, Go, Plus, and Pro accounts have model-improvement sharing on by default unless the user turns off Improve the model for everyone in Data Controls. OpenAI says Business, Enterprise, Edu, and API data is not used for training by default.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 01

Change the setting you can change today

For a personal ChatGPT account, open Data Controls and turn off Improve the model for everyone if you do not want new conversations used for model improvement. For Gemini, review Keep Activity, which controls whether Gemini activity can be used to improve services. For Claude personal accounts, review the data-improvement choice in account settings. For consumer Copilot, review its privacy controls for training and personalization.

Those settings do not turn a personal account into an approved business system. They are one layer of care. Your office still needs a rule about which tools are approved and which information belongs only in the systems your company controls.

Anthropic says consumer accounts have a data-improvement choice, while commercial API, Team, and Enterprise data is not used to train models by default unless an organization opts in. Google says Keep Activity affects future Gemini activity use, and Microsoft documents separate consumer privacy controls.

Never paste these items

Keep out

  • Social Security numbers
  • Credit or debit card numbers
  • Passwords, API keys, and login codes
  • Medical or health information

Also pause on

  • Unpublished financial records
  • Full customer files
  • Employment or legal matters
  • Anything a customer expected only you to see

When in doubt, describe the situation instead. "A customer disputed a service charge" can be enough to draft a neutral reply. You can add the approved amount and name later inside the business system, after you have checked the wording.

Why business plans matter

A business plan can give an organization a clearer default than a collection of personal accounts. It may offer administration, commercial data terms, and a way to control access when a person leaves. That does not remove the need for an internal policy. It gives the policy a better place to live.

Make the safe route the easy route. Give the team an approved tool, a short list of allowed jobs, and a person to ask when a request falls outside the rule. If an employee feels they must use a personal account to finish normal work, that is a process problem worth fixing.

Data safety is also about what comes back out. A summary can repeat sensitive details even when it does not create them. Before sharing a draft, check who will receive it, whether the information belongs in that channel, and whether a simpler description would do the job.

Microsoft says commercial Microsoft 365 Copilot prompts, responses, and Microsoft Graph data are not used to train foundation models. Google says Workspace data is governed by Workspace terms rather than the consumer Gemini activity setting, and admins can control Gemini availability.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 04Source 05

Do this today

  1. Identify the exact account type your office is using.
  2. Review its training or activity setting and change it if appropriate.
  3. Write a four-item never-paste list where the team keeps prompts.
  4. Ask who approves an AI tool before anyone on the team signs up for a new one.

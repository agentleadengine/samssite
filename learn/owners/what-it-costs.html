What AI costs a small business in 2026

Track
Owners · lesson 03 of 16
Reading time
3 min
Last checked
28 Sep 2026
Marks
Checked My take

AI can be a small monthly office expense or a large custom software bill. Start with the simple version: a subscription per person, a small trial, and a budget you can explain without a spreadsheet full of guesses.

Know which kind of price you are seeing

Consumer plans are for an individual. Business plans are for a company that needs shared administration, commercial data handling, and a way to manage seats. Neither label makes an answer correct. The business choice matters because office work often includes customer information, shared documents, and people joining or leaving the team.

Some services also sell API usage. That is different from a chat subscription. An API is for builders who connect a model to software, and its bill depends on how much text goes in and out. If you are learning to use chat for office work, do not put API token pricing in your first budget. The builder track covers that path.

Individual plan prices today: Claude Pro at $20 monthly, ChatGPT Plus at $20 monthly, Google AI Pro at $19.99 monthly, and Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot at $23.50 per user per month when paid annually. Microsoft also lists a $28.20 monthly price for that bundle.

What business plans add

For a company, the subscription is not only about more messages or newer features. Look for who can add and remove people, whether the plan matches your office suite, what its data terms say, and whether you can set a simple policy for the team. If you are putting customer material into a tool, those questions are part of the price.

Do not buy a top tier because somebody might need it someday. Start with a small group that has repeat work. You can increase seats once the trial has shown a real use. You can also keep a personal plan for a limited, non-sensitive experiment while you decide whether the office needs a business setup.

Claude lists Team at $25 monthly or $20 per standard seat on annual billing. ChatGPT lists Business and Enterprise plans with plan or sales-dependent pricing. Google Workspace lists Business Starter at $7, Standard at $14, and Plus at $22 per user per month on annual commitment, with Gemini availability varying by edition.

A five-person budget you can show

Here is a plain example, using the checked Microsoft annual bundle price. If five people need Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot, the monthly software budget is:

5 people x $23.50 per person per month = $117.50 per month.

For a year, that is $117.50 x 12 = $1,410. That is not a promise of savings. It is simply the subscription cost before tax, promotions, changes in pricing, or optional software. Compare it with one specific kind of time saved, such as drafting routine replies or summarizing meetings, after the team has used it for a month.

A five-person ChatGPT Plus experiment, by contrast, would be 5 x $20 = $100 per month. A five-person Claude Pro experiment would also be 5 x $20 = $100 per month. Those are simple subscription examples, not business-plan recommendations.

Prices, included features, and promotional offers can change by region, tax, billing commitment, and date. Confirm the checkout price and the data terms before you buy seats for the office.

Budget for learning too

The hidden cost is not the card charge. It is paying for seats that nobody knows how to use. Give each person two or three safe, repeatable tasks. Put good prompts in one shared place. Spend fifteen minutes a week comparing what worked. This small habit makes the subscription easier to judge.

Also set a stop rule. If a trial has not produced a task people choose to repeat after four weeks, pause the rollout instead of adding more seats. That is useful information, not a failure. The work may not be ready, or a different tool may fit better.

Do this today

  1. List how many people truly need access in the first month.
  2. Pick one checked monthly price and multiply it by that number.
  3. Write down the two jobs that must improve for the subscription to be worth keeping.
  4. Put a 30-day review on the calendar before you buy more seats.

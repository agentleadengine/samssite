What changed, and when.

AI facts go stale. When I re-check a page, the date and what changed go here, newest first. If you spot something out of date, tell me.

28 Sep 2026

Business libraries fact-checked. SEO, paid ads, conversion (CRO), cold email, email marketing, growth, direct response, and running a business: 347 pages re-checked against official sources. Outdated platform facts were corrected (for example, GA4 attribution models removed in November 2023, current CAN-SPAM penalties, the FTC rule on fake reviews), unsupported statistics were removed or replaced with ways to measure your own results, and legal or tax statements now say what to ask a professional.

Checked28 Sep 2026

New design and a new owner track. The whole library moved to one design built around dates and sources. Ten plain-English lessons for owners were added (sixteen by the end of the day), from what AI can and cannot do to when a task is ready to run on its own.

Checked28 Sep 2026

Six more owner lessons. Based on what small-business owners say they need most in 2025 and 2026 surveys (Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses, Intuit QuickBooks, U.S. Chamber, NFIB): AI inside Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, marketing that does not sound like AI, phones and missed calls, meeting notes you can trust, spotting AI scams and fake invoices, and review replies.

Checked28 Sep 2026

Current model lineups and prices. Claude pages now cover Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5, and Haiku 4.5. Claude vs GPT now compares the Claude 5 family with GPT-6 Astra, Sol, and Luna. Plan prices for Claude, ChatGPT, Google AI, and Microsoft 365 Copilot were re-checked on the vendors' pricing pages.

Checked28 Sep 2026

Six new builder guides. Write your first skill, Subagents, Build an MCP server, The Claude Agent SDK, Context engineering, and Local and open models. Every command in them was checked against the official docs.

Checked28 Sep 2026

Claude Code and MCP pages refreshed. Corrected outdated details: skills now use the SKILL.md format, headless runs use claude -p, scheduling covers routines, desktop scheduled tasks, and /loop, and MCP transports are stdio and Streamable HTTP (SSE is legacy).

Checked28 Sep 2026

Nine new glossary terms. Subagent, skill, hooks, context engineering, Agent SDK, MCP registry, Streamable HTTP, open-weight model, and Ollama.

Checked28 Sep 2026

Not yet re-checked. The industry playbooks got the new design and their "Setting up Claude" modules were re-checked, but their other modules have not had a full fact check yet. Pages without a "Last checked" date should be read with that in mind.

B. Ads inside ChatGPT

  1. 1

    Can you buy them?

    Check whether you can run ads in the towns where you work.

    Details

    An ad can appear below a ChatGPT answer and is labeled as sponsored. Eligible people on the Free and Go plans may see ads. Plus, Pro and business plans are ad-free. Paying for an ad does not change the answer or the companies ChatGPT recommends in it.[50] [51] [52]

    Yes. OpenAI opened its do-it-yourself Ads Manager in the United States on May 5, 2026, at ads.openai.com. Local services are eligible to apply. The standard U.S. minimum is $25 a day, although some fixed bids require more. At $25, a single day can spend up to $50, with a cap of $175 per budget week as long as the daily setting stays the same.[53] [54] [55] [56] [57]

    Choose where ads may appear. OpenAI lists Connecticut, the Hartford and New Haven market, and ZIP codes including Meriden 06450 and 06451. Then give it “context hints,” short descriptions of the needs you want to reach. The system looks for conversations that fit the need you describe, even when people use different words. You can give it a target cost per click or per completed action, such as an estimate request. The target guides what the system bids and actual costs can vary.[58] [59] [60] [57]

    Details

    Make sure search tools can reach your service and project pages.

    Details

    Use the same business information on your site and listings.

    Details

    Fix the two addresses first. Use the same real name and number, and the same address wherever it belongs. Keep the hours consistent too.[25] [24] [15] [17] [18] [22]

    Ask every customer after the job. Let them describe their experience in their own words. Reply in the language they used when you can.

  2. 10

    What doesn’t work

    Skip ranking promises and put the time into useful answers and real reviews.

    Details
    • I’d skip llms.txt, a suggested file for AI tools. In one study, 97% of the valid files looked at got zero requests in the month studied. A ranking benefit is unproven.[69] [75] [87]
    • Write helpful FAQs and show authentic reviews. Adding labels in the code to try to get FAQ results or your own review stars has no proven AI benefit. Google limits those special search displays.[69] [88] [89]
    • Paying for ads does not change ChatGPT’s answers. I’d also skip any offer of guaranteed AI rankings. Answers can vary between runs, and one study found AI tools often named different businesses than Google Maps.[50] [27]

Questions people ask

Do I need to be technical?

No. I walk you through setup on a call. You sign in to your own accounts, and you can use the assistant by text afterward.

What does it cost all-in?

Setup $400 one time. Then Care $99, Managed $199 or Full Service $349 a month. Provider costs are included. 30-day money back. Your server, account and data stay yours; providers process information needed to run them.

What if I cancel?

Your server and data remain in your accounts. Email me to cancel or change your plan. I will confirm the last day of your plan and walk you through the provider accounts. The server and AI accounts are yours to keep or close; if you keep them after the plan ends, they will need your own payment method.

Is my client data safe?

Your server and data are in accounts you own. Care has no server login and shares only health status; Managed uses limited settings access; Full Service can use owner-approved, expiring access. Providers process information needed to run the service. On Managed, I change settings only through a fixed menu, and each change is logged. If I need more, your assistant texts you why and for how long. Nothing happens until you reply YES; access ends on its own, and you can say STOP anytime. It never reads your email inbox. Use ordinary, nonsensitive information and review outputs. Do not upload medical records, legal case files, Social Security numbers, card details or bank logins.

Will it text my clients without asking?

No. It prepares drafts for you. You approve before anything is sent or published.

Can I use iMessage or Telegram?

Yes. You can choose either during setup. iMessage uses a free Photon account; Telegram is free too.

How long does setup take?

Start with a 15-minute conversation. If you move ahead, we schedule a setup call of about two and a half hours to build it with you in your own accounts.

When can I reach you?

Every day from 9 AM to 9 PM Eastern. Support clocks resume when the daily window opens. Response times are targets, not repair guarantees. If something ever looks wrong, you can switch your assistant off yourself in your server provider's app.

What would you text it?

Start with the work already on your phone. These are examples of requests, not automatic messages to clients.

“Draft a follow-up for the couple I showed 12 Oak St to.”

“Write the listing description from these notes.”

“Summarize this inspection report and flag questions for my client.”

“Turn these open-house notes into follow-ups I can review.”

“Remind me Friday at 9 to call the Hendersons.”

“Make my Monday plan from these showings and deadlines.”

For documents and deal terms, check every draft yourself and ask a qualified professional when you need legal advice. Agent Care does not answer your phone or send client messages on its own.

Questions agents ask

Do I need to be technical?

No. Sam walks you through setup on a call. You sign in to your own accounts, and you can use the assistant by text afterward.

What does it cost all-in?

Care is about $133 to $148/month, Managed about $233 to $248/month, or Full Service about $383 to $398/month. Each estimate includes the plan, a DigitalOcean server at about $24 to $29/month, and an AI account at $10 to $20/month. Setup is free with a monthly plan; iMessage via Photon is free.

What if I cancel?

Your server and data remain in your accounts. Contact Sam to stop the Agent Care plan. You control whether to keep or close the separate server and AI accounts.

Is my client data safe?

Your server and data are in accounts you own. We keep no standing access after setup and never read your email inbox. Share only the client information needed for a task, review outputs, and follow your brokerage's data rules.

Will it text my clients without asking?

No. It prepares drafts for you. You approve before anything is sent or published.

Can I use iMessage or Telegram?

Yes. You can choose either during setup. iMessage uses a free Photon account; Telegram is free too.

How long does setup take?

Start with a 15-minute call. If you move ahead, we schedule a one-hour setup call to build it with you in your own accounts.

Website and engagement terms

Clear scope before paid work.

These pages describe the available services. A form, free call or calendar booking does not create a client relationship. Paid work begins only when both sides agree scope, inputs, permissions, price, deadline and payment in writing. Any agreed differences from the published service description must be explicit before payment.

Audit scope and price

The standard audit is $1,500 flat: one business or agency location, one priority journey, up to 50 recent records in an agreed sample window, an evidence table, native-feature check, ranked fix specification, recorded walkthrough and findings call. A $750 founding rate applies only if availability is confirmed and agreed in writing; no positive review or testimonial is required.

The five-business-day delivery period starts when agreed inputs and permissions are ready and we confirm the start date. About 75 minutes of owner meeting time is planned; staff preparation may take another 30 to 60 minutes. Sample window, preparation burden and any exclusions are agreed before payment.

Audit delivery promise

If I do not deliver the agreed audit checklist by the confirmed deadline after agreed inputs are ready, you can choose a revised delivery date or a full audit-fee refund. Any mutually agreed deadline change is recorded in writing. The promise is about delivering the agreed report, not finding a financial loss. You keep the delivered audit and documentation, including after a delivery-promise refund.

Contact Sam through the original engagement thread to request the remedy. Payment and refund handling are recorded in the written engagement.

No financial-results guarantee

No revenue, profit, savings, policies-sold, ranking, conversion or compliance outcome is guaranteed. Measured observations, estimated financial scenarios and unknowns are distinguished. A completed audit may recommend no paid automation. A process gap is not itself proof of an attributable lost sale.

Optional implementation and audit credit

The $2,500 to $5,000 range is for planning. A separate fixed quote depends on the agreed one-workflow scope, permissions and platform capability. No build deadline applies until agreed in writing. The full audit fee actually paid credits toward one implementation agreed within 90 days of audit delivery, capped at that build's price. No cash value, no stacking and no credit for a refunded fee. No obligation to purchase a build.

Software licenses, usage, messages and third-party fees are separate and disclosed before work starts. Accounts remain yours; vendor subscriptions remain subject to the provider's terms.

Optional Guard

$297 to $497 per calendar month for one agreed workflow. Both include a monthly test, exception review and evidence report. $297 includes up to one maintenance hour; $497 includes up to two. Maintenance hours do not roll over. New integrations, additional workflows and third-party incidents are separately scoped; excess work is not billed without agreement.

Coverage is during agreed business hours, not 24/7 monitoring, an uptime SLA or emergency response. There is no annual commitment; cancel before the next monthly renewal. The billing date and cancellation method are stated in the engagement. The monthly report distinguishes observed activity, estimates and unknown outcomes; it is not a guaranteed money-recovered report.

Data, permissions and professional boundaries

No customer, policy, claims or payment data belongs in a public inquiry form. Before paid access, agree secure transfer, least-privilege permissions, approved providers, retention and deletion dates, and an incident contact. No shared passwords or unrestricted account access. Any live probe or customer-facing automation requires separate written authorization and appropriate channel-specific consent and suppression checks.

This service is not legal advice, an insurance-advice service, underwriting, claims handling, binding authority, a cybersecurity certification or a regulatory-compliance guarantee. The business remains responsible for its obligations and obtains qualified advice where needed.

Readiness, delivery and corrections

Before the five-day clock starts, we confirm in writing that the agreed usable sample, decision-maker, workflow owner and approved-tool list are ready. Delivery means sending the agreed six-item report checklist and walkthrough by the confirmed date. The findings call may be scheduled separately. One factual-correction round requested within seven days is included; new journeys and changed requirements are new scope. Deadlines change only by mutual written agreement.

Guard maintenance boundaries

The monthly test and standard report are separate from the one or two included maintenance hours. Unused maintenance time does not roll over. Requests are acknowledged within two business days during agreed business hours, with no guaranteed resolution time. Incident repair beyond the allowance, client-caused breakage, new integrations and expanded scope are quoted before work. There is no 24/7 monitoring or emergency-response commitment.

Data and professional-services agreement

No customer records should be sent through this website. Before any records are shared, the signed engagement must specify permitted data, secure transfer, access, approved tools, confidentiality, retention and deletion, incident handling, deliverable ownership and liability terms. The agency confirms its authority to share records. No work begins with customer data until those terms and permissions are agreed.

Queva, alpha 1.0

What Queva is.

Queva is an assistant you text in iMessage or Telegram. It runs on Hermes, open-source agent software, with my setup on top: one private server per owner, a memory that keeps your prices, people and the way you talk, and rules that keep it to plain words and one question per text. It drafts texts, quotes and replies for you to send, keeps your lists, looks things up with sources, and builds small tools you open from a link. It is alpha: new, improving, and watched by me. More about Queva →

What you can text it.

A few everyday asks. It drafts; you check and send.

Follow up on a quote

“Draft a friendly follow-up for the Garcia quote I sent Tuesday.”

Who owes me

“Who still owes me from last month? Draft polite reminders.”

Reply to a review

“Write a short reply to this Google review.”

Summarize a PDF

“Summarize this PDF and give me the three things I need to do.”

Plan my Monday

“Plan my Monday from these jobs and errands.”

Make me a tool

“Make me a simple job tracker I can open on my phone.”

Why I built it on open source.

Hermes is open-source agent software, so anyone can read how it works. I add the setup, the memory, the rules and the care on top.

Each owner gets their own server in their own account. If you stop working with me, the server and what is on it stay yours.

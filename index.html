02

No new app. You just text it.

The usual way

Most AI tools are one more app to open, type into and copy out of. They forget your business by tomorrow, so you explain it all over again.

My way

Your assistant lives in your text messages, so there's nothing to install. Text it like a coworker, from the job site, the office or the parking lot. It remembers your business, your people and how you like things done. Answers stay short, and anything long goes on a page with a link.

03

Your assistant. Your server. Your data.

The usual way

Most AI tools run on somebody else's platform, on a machine you don't control, under rules they can change any time.

My way

Yours runs on one small cloud server in your own account, backed up every day. Only your number can text it; texts from anyone else get ignored. Everything it does goes to an activity log you can read, and you can switch it off.

04

You do the job. It does the paperwork.

What eats your day

The same questions, a quote to chase, notes to type into the CRM, a pile of forwarded email. Every hour of it comes out of selling, installing and serving your customers.

What it takes off your plate

Text it, forward it, ask it:

  • Forward it an email and it texts you a short summary with the deadline.
  • Checklists and reminders, by text.
  • Follow up drafts for you to look over and send.
  • Lookups and research with its own web browser.
  • Questions about your own business, answered from what it remembers.
05

Need a tracker? Text it. It builds one.

The usual way

Small businesses pay every month for software they barely use, and still keep half of it in a notebook.

My way

Ask by text for a tracker, a form, a list or a simple CRM, in plain words, like “a list of jobs that still owe a deposit.” It builds it as a private page on your server. Want a change? Text it. It's yours, not another subscription. It doesn't replace your CRM or your accounting software.

06

I set it up. Then I teach you to run it.

The usual way

Most setups end the day the installer leaves. Nobody knows how it works, so it slowly stops getting used.

My way

I set it up for you, then I teach you in plain words until you can run it, correct it and ask it for new things on your own. You won't need me for every little change. When you do need me, support is in every plan.

Out of the box it asks before a message, a payment, a post or a delete goes out; you reply yes and it happens. You can loosen that later.

07

What it won't do.

The usual way

Sites that promise everything, and a product that does less.

Straight answer

It never sends email and only reads what you forward it. It doesn't text your customers; it works for you. It stays off bank and payment sites. It will get things wrong sometimes; tell it by text and it remembers. If something I set up breaks, I fix it.

Evidence rules

A process gap is not recovered revenue.

Measured

What records show

Counts, timestamps, documented next steps and directly observed handling time. Interview recollections are estimates, not measurements.

Estimated

What might change

Potential incremental revenue and staff capacity, with assumptions and costs shown. Insurance scenarios use agency commission, not total policy premium. Outcomes can be zero.

Unknown / benchmark

What is not established

Missing records remain unknown. External benchmarks are context, never your results. Overlapping contacts are not counted twice.

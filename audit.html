What I look for

Five places revenue goes quiet.

I measure what I can, estimate what needs a reasonable assumption, and mark industry benchmarks clearly. I do not turn a benchmark into your number.

01

Missed first contact

After-hours calls hit voicemail, and a prospect may call the next business before anyone can help.

I measureWhat a real caller reaches after hours and what next step is available.

02

Quote follow-up gap

A quote goes out, follow-up lives in memory, and a ready buyer goes quiet.

I measureQuotes without a documented next step, owner, or follow-up date.

03

Speed to lead

A web lead waits in an inbox while another business answers first.

I measureThe time from form submission to a useful human response.

04

Late renewal outreach

Renewal and repeat-business outreach starts at the last minute, turning a relationship conversation into a price conversation.

I measureWhen the first outreach starts compared with the renewal date.

05

Re-keyed data

The same lead is typed across systems, adding staff time and chances for a missed task or wrong detail.

I measureWhere records are retyped, how long it takes, and what errors or missed follow-ups result.

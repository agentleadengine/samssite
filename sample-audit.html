Deliverable 1 of 3, Leak Map

Dollar figures are estimates built from intake answers and conservative industry benchmarks. They show scale, not an audit to the dollar. Commission math uses $1,800 average annual premium × 12% commission × 4-year average retention ≈ $860 lifetime commission per bound account. Each figure is tagged MEASURED from records or my probe, ESTIMATED from volume and a conservative rate, or BENCHMARKED as directional only.

Leak 1: After-hours calls go to voicemail and die there

  • What happens today: “If it rings after 5:30 it goes to the machine. Maria checks messages first thing, but half of them don't leave one.” I called at 6:15pm on a Tuesday, four rings, generic voicemail, no text-back, no callback by noon the next day.
  • The math: ~5 after-hours calls/week × 52 × 40% who never reconnect × 25% close rate × $860 = ~$22,400/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $22,000
  • Confidence: ESTIMATED call volume from their phone logs; reconnect and close rates are conservative industry figures. Probe evidence: MEASURED.

Leak 2: Quoted-not-sold follow-up lives in the producer's head

  • What happens today: “Joe sends the quote and follows up if he remembers. There's no list.” No second touch is scheduled for roughly half of all quotes.
  • The math: 25 quotes/mo × 12 × 50% with no 2nd touch × 10-point close-rate lift from one follow-up × $860 = ~$12,900/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $12,000
  • Confidence: ESTIMATED quote volume from their register; lift from published follow-up studies, rounded down.

Leak 3: Web leads answered in hours, not minutes

  • What happens today: Website form sends an email to the office inbox. First response averages 3+ hours during the day; Saturday submissions wait until Monday.
  • The math: 12 web leads/mo × 12 × ~10% of leads lost outright to slow response × 25% close × $860 ≈ ~$3,100/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $3,000
  • Confidence: BENCHMARKED no web-lead outcome records exist, worth instrumenting; excluded from guarantee.

Leak 4: Renewal outreach starts when the notice arrives

  • What happens today: First client touch is the carrier's renewal notice, which is also the client's cue to shop. No 90/60/30-day cadence exists.
  • The math: 650 households × 4% lost at renewal that proactive outreach retains × $860 = ~$22,400/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $22,000
  • Confidence: ESTIMATED book size from AMS export; retention lift is an industry benchmark, low end.

Leak 5: Every inbound lead is typed three times

  • What happens today: Website email → spreadsheet → AMS, re-keyed by hand. Staff estimate 6 hours/week across the office, plus the occasional wrong phone number that kills a lead.
  • The math: 6 hrs/week × 52 × $22/hr including taxes and benefits = ~$6,900/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $6,900
  • Confidence: MEASURED hours from staff interviews; loaded cost from their payroll figure.

Fix 2: AI receptionist with missed-call text-back, maps to Leak 1

  • What it does: Answers calls around the clock, books the appointment or takes a structured message, and texts the caller right away so the conversation keeps moving. You get a summary by text and email.
  • ICE: Impact 9 × Confidence 8 × Ease 7 = 504
  • Build cost: $3,500 one-time + $297/mo
  • Recovers: ~$22,000/yr
  • Payback period: ~1.9 months
  • Time to live: 7 days

Fix 3: Quoted-not-sold follow-up sequence, maps to Leak 2

  • What it does: Every quote automatically gets a 5-touch follow-up over 14 days (email + text), stops when the client responds, and hands warm replies to the producer. Nobody has to remember anything.
  • ICE: Impact 7 × Confidence 7 × Ease 8 = 392
  • Build cost: $2,500 one-time + $97/mo

Fix 4: Website-to-AMS lead pipe, maps to Leaks 3 & 5

  • What it does: Web form submissions instantly create the AMS/CRM record, text the lead within 60 seconds with a booking link, and notify the office. No inbox, no spreadsheet, no re-typing.

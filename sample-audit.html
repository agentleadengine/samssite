A quote went out. What happens next?

Sample audit: Maple Grove Insurance Agency (fictional)

The decision: make quote ownership and next actions visible using the agency's existing task tools. Do not buy a custom build yet. Confirm the data quality and staff adoption first.

This example focuses on one journey: quoted-but-not-bound new business. The fictional agency has four staff. The sample contains 40 quote records from a fictional 30-day window. No customers were contacted and no agency records were used.

2. What is a workflow gap, and what is not?

Workflow gap: a quote cannot be found in the team's next-action list, and nobody can confirm who owns the follow-up. Check notes and the producer's actual activity before concluding the contact was missed.

Not necessarily a workflow gap: the carrier declined the risk, the premium was uncompetitive, the prospect was not eligible, or the prospect declined further contact. Record those reasons and do not keep chasing a closed or suppressed contact.

No double counting: a prospect who called, filled in a form and received a quote is one opportunity, not three revenue leaks.

3. The commission scenario, not a recovery claim

For arithmetic only, assume a $1,800 annual premium and a 12% agency commission. That is $216 first-year gross agency commission per additional bound account. These are invented inputs, not an industry benchmark. Actual commissions depend on the book and contracts.

The current information does not justify a $2,500 to $5,000 implementation. Start with an existing task view and a named owner. A credible audit can recommend spending less, not more.

5. Boundaries and permissions

  • No customer-facing message is part of this example. Templates and any automated communication need separate authorization and a channel-specific consent and suppression review.
  • No coverage advice, binding, cancellation, underwriting or claims decisions are delegated to software.
  • Use redacted samples or an agreed screen-share. Do not paste policyholder information into a website inquiry form.
  • Platform access and integration availability must be checked for the actual account. No universal AMS integration promise.

6. What the follow-up report would measure

  • Eligible open quotes with a documented owner and dated next action, divided by eligible open quotes reviewed.
  • Overdue items, their age, duplicate tasks and false exceptions.
  • Time spent maintaining the workflow, from a timed sample rather than recollection.
  • Bound, declined and pending outcomes, with unknowns preserved. Association alone is not causal revenue attribution.

Evidence, assumptions and unresolved questions stay in separate columns. No invented total dollar loss, no promised conversion lift, and no positive testimonial required.

Headlines

8 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Why the headline does the work

Prospects don't read ads. They scan. The headline is the gate: if it earns attention, they read. If it doesn't, they're gone. On any platform, newspaper, email subject line, YouTube thumbnail, Facebook ad, landing page, the same physics apply. Attention is the scarce resource; the headline is the currency.

The headline's four jobs

  1. Stop the right reader with a relevant subject or problem.
  2. State a benefit the page can support.
  3. Add specificity that clarifies the audience, mechanism, or constraint.

2. Benefit

Name the useful change and the audience it applies to. Keep the wording within the evidence available on the page.

4. Quickness / easiness

Time and effort claims need defined starting conditions and evidence. If those are unavailable, describe the process instead of promising speed.

The headline writing process

  1. Write the audience, promise, proof, and next step before drafting.
  2. Create variants that change one angle at a time.
  3. Reject any version whose claim cannot be substantiated on the page.
  4. Vague benefit. "Elevate your business." Elevate is a word people use when they don't know what they're selling.
  5. Clickbait without payoff. "You won't believe what happened next." Prospects now distrust this pattern on sight.
  6. Too long, with no structure. Long is fine, but must be readable in one pass.

The best headline is boring if the market is sophisticated

When readers have seen exaggerated claims, a precise audience, mechanism, and constraint can be more credible than another amplified promise.

Register the headline test

Pre-register audience, control, challenger, primary event, quality guardrail, allocation, sample plan, and stopping rule. Report the estimate and uncertainty without a lift promise.

Where it breaks: A curious headline can raise clicks while lowering qualified response or revenue.

Do this today

  1. Write one audience-specific promise.
  2. Verify every factual claim.
  3. Choose the qualified outcome and guardrail.
  4. Freeze the rest of the page.

Guarantees + risk reversal

7 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Why guarantees work

A guarantee explains which risk the seller accepts and how a buyer can make a claim. It only builds trust when the terms are visible, specific, affordable, and consistently honored.

The guarantee ladder

Refund guarantee

State the eligible purchase, claim window, request channel, exclusions, evidence required, refund method, and processing owner.

Conditional guarantee

Name every action the buyer must take and every record the seller will accept. Avoid conditions that are hidden, subjective, or impossible to verify.

Service-level remedy

For delivery promises, say what event triggers a credit, refund, rework, or cancellation right. Keep it separate from an unsupported promise of business results.

Design the promise around the real risk

A buyer may fear poor fit, missed delivery, difficult cancellation, or a slow refund. Choose the remedy that addresses that fear. Do not stack promises that conflict or create an obligation the business cannot fulfill.

Publish terms you can honor

The FTC expects a reasonable basis for objective express or implied advertising claims before an ad runs. A guarantee does not replace evidence for a promised result.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 01

State the eligible purchase, claim window, exclusions, request method, evidence, refund method, and owner. Show the terms before payment. Do not promise an outcome without evidence. Ask a lawyer to review result language and the rules where you sell.

Track orders, requests, approved refunds, refund dollars, support cost, and net revenue by purchase cohort. Compare like offers over the same window.

Where it breaks: Refund data from one offer does not predict another offer. Delayed fulfillment can also hide claims that have not matured yet.

Do this today

  1. Copy the live guarantee into a terms sheet.
  2. Check that checkout shows the same terms.
  3. Remove unsupported result claims.
  4. Ask counsel to review the promise.

Related: Value equation · Grand slam offers · Pricing psychology

Measure one guarantee honestly

Terms sheet: eligible purchase, claim deadline, buyer actions, exclusions, request channel, required evidence, response time, refund method, and escalation owner. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review and show the same terms before payment and at claim time.

For each purchase cohort, record orders, eligible claims, submitted claims, approvals, denials with reasons, refund dollars, processing time, support cost, conversion, and net revenue. Wait until the full claim window closes and compare only the same offer, price, audience, and fulfillment. No seller dataset was supplied, so this page deliberately does not invent refund or conversion results.

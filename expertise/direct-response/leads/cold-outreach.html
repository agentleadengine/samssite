Cold outreach

8 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Cold outreach is a measured contact process, not a universal volume formula. Start with a lawful list source, a relevant reason to write, a clear sender, and a working opt-out.

The four inputs that determine success

  1. List: record source, selection rule, jurisdiction, consent or legal basis, and exclusions.
  2. Message: connect one observed business condition to one relevant offer. Do not invent personalization.
  3. Delivery: authenticate the sender, identify the business, suppress opt-outs, and monitor complaints.
  4. Outcome: define delivered, reply, positive reply, qualified meeting, and customer before sending.

Platforms beyond email

LinkedIn DMs

Use the platform's current rules and the recipient's context. Record connection, reply, and qualified outcome separately from email.

Cold calls

For calls, define the list, permitted hours, disposition codes, opt-out handling, and qualified outcome. Ask a lawyer which calling rules apply.

Twitter / X DMs

Use direct messages only when the audience and platform rules fit. Track the channel as its own cohort rather than combining it with email replies.

Multi-channel sequences

A multi-channel sequence needs one suppression record. When a person opts out or replies, every connected step should stop according to the registered rule.

Run a measured, consent-aware cohort

US commercial email must provide a working opt-out, and senders must honor opt-out requests within 10 business days. A vendor does not remove the sender's responsibility.

Gmail requires authentication, valid DNS, TLS, low spam rates, and RFC 5322 formatting. Some senders have added requirements based on daily Gmail volume.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 02

Document list source, consent or legal basis, suppression, sender identity, and opt-out. Define reply and qualified meeting before launch. Report delivery, opt-outs, complaints, replies, qualified meetings, and customers by list and week. Ask a lawyer which outreach rules apply.

Where it breaks: A reply rate can hide complaints, bad targeting, or unqualified meetings. Small lists also make week-to-week rates unstable.

Do this today

  1. Test the opt-out.
  2. Confirm authentication and suppression.
  3. Write the event definitions.
  4. Set a complaint stop rule.

An anonymized cohort record

Save one row per recipient with list source, selection reason, consent or legal basis, send time, delivery state, opt-out, complaint, reply category, meeting qualification, and customer outcome.

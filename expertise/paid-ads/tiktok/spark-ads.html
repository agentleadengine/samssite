TikTok Spark Ads

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Spark Ads promote an existing post from an owned or authorized TikTok account. Test them against brand creative with matched spend and the same conversion definition.

Spark Ads require an owned or authorized account or post. Authorization windows and workflows vary, so check the contract and current Ads Manager setting.

Changing28 Sep 2026Source 01

The FTC requires a material-connection disclosure to be clear and conspicuous. In video, put it in the video, preferably in both the picture and audio. A clear term such as “Ad” or “Sponsored” can work, but a hashtag by itself is not the rule.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 03

How it works

  1. Find or create an organic TikTok (yours or a creator partner's)
  2. Creator gives you permission via Spark Ad code
  3. You boost it as an ad
  4. Engagement (likes, comments, shares) accumulates on the original post

What changes in a Spark comparison

  • The creator or owned account identity appears with the promoted post
  • Paid engagement can appear on the original post
  • Comments and existing engagement differ from a new brand ad
  • Identity, creative treatment, and social context can change together

The creator partnership

Creator posts organically, then grants paid-use authorization under the written agreement. Record the fee, term, territories, edit rights, and expiry.

  • Creator gets reach and fee
  • You get native creative with creator credibility

The creator marketplaces

  • TikTok Creator Marketplace (official)
  • Insense
  • Billo
  • Direct DM to creators

The contract essentials

  • Video usage rights for paid advertising
  • Spark Ad authorization period
  • Content guidelines (what they can/can't say)
  • Clear, conspicuous disclosure placed with the endorsement and inside the video

Ask your lawyer to review disclosure language, usage rights, and any state or country rules that apply to the campaign.

Testing with Spark

Compare an authorized Spark post with the same approved edit from the brand account. Hold the objective, audience, bid, landing page, spend window, and qualified outcome constant.

When Spark loses

  • When original post is too niche to scale
  • Ad requires specific claims creator can't make
  • When branded messaging is non-negotiable

The performance numbers in this illustration are not documented results. Use the matched test below instead.

Do this today

Authorization record:

  1. Creator approves the final post, disclosure, paid-use term, territories, edits, and fee in writing.
  2. Creator enables ad authorization and sends the code through the agreed secure channel.
  3. Advertiser records the TikTok account, post ID, authorization term, contract owner, and expiry date.
  4. Advertiser previews the ad identity, caption, landing page, disclosure, and comments policy.
  5. Owner removes the ad at expiry and records whether organic authorization remains.

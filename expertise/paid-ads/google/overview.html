Google Ads overview

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Google Ads includes Search, Shopping, Display, Video, Demand Gen, and Performance Max. Each campaign type reaches different inventory and intent.

The properties

  • Search: text ads on search results. Highest intent. The most valuable.
  • Shopping: product ads with image + price. For e-commerce.
  • Display: banner ads on 2M+ websites. Low intent, cheap CPMs.
  • Video: YouTube ads. Covered in YouTube section.
  • Demand Gen: visual campaigns across YouTube, Shorts, Discover, Gmail, and the Google Display Network.
  • Performance Max: a goal-based campaign that uses Google AI across available inventory.

Demand Gen is the current campaign family for visual discovery placements. Discover remains an inventory surface inside it.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 01

Performance Max is a goal-based campaign type that uses Google AI across Search, Maps, Shopping, YouTube, Discover, Gmail, and Display inventory.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 02

Where most money should go

For most B2B and service businesses: Search first. High intent, clear conversion path. For e-commerce: Shopping + Performance Max. For brand-building: Display and YouTube complement Search.

Account structure basics

  • Account → Campaign → Ad Group → Ad + Keywords
  • One campaign per major objective or product line
  • Ad groups group tightly-related keywords
  • Use enough approved assets to test a named message difference without fragmenting the decision

Where to start

  1. Set up conversion tracking (GA4 + Google Ads conversions)
  2. One Search campaign targeting your highest-intent keywords
  3. Set the spend cap from allowable acquisition cost, conversion lag, and the planned decision
  4. Measure search terms, qualified actions, and CRM-confirmed outcomes before expanding

Do this today

  1. Map the offer's intent, product feed, visual assets, geography, and conversion value to eligible campaign types.
  2. Campaign map: Search captures queries on Google Search. Shopping uses product data for retail placements. Performance Max can serve across Google inventory from one goal-based campaign. Demand Gen is for visual demand creation across YouTube, Discover, Gmail, and the Google Display Network where available. Video campaigns use YouTube video inventory for supported reach, view, or conversion goals.
  3. Hypothetical choice: A local emergency plumber starts with Search because the demand is expressed as a query. A new meal-kit brand with strong vertical video tests Demand Gen. Keep each campaign tied to one measurable business goal.
  4. Decision rule: Start with the campaign whose inventory matches current intent and measurable value.
  5. Limitation: One campaign type can serve across several surfaces, so labels do not guarantee placement.

Google Ads bidding strategies

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Google offers a dozen bidding strategies. They fall into two camps: manual (you control bids) and smart/automated (algorithm controls bids based on goals). Modern best practice is automated, but only when you have the right data.

Automated strategies

Maximize Conversions

Algorithm spends budget to get the most conversions. Good starting point when you're proving the channel.

Target CPA

Bid toward a target cost per acquisition only after the conversion action, lag, and allowable acquisition cost are trustworthy.

Target ROAS

Bid to hit a target return on ad spend. Best for e-commerce with revenue tracking.

Maximize Conversion Value

Algorithm maximizes total value, not number of conversions. Good when conversions have different values (high vs low ticket).

Maximize Clicks

For traffic-focused campaigns. Not usually what you want for direct response.

Manual strategies

Manual CPC

You set max CPC per keyword. Full control. Lower ceiling. Use when you don't have enough data for smart bidding.

Enhanced CPC (eCPC)

Manual with algorithmic adjustment. Half-and-half. Transitional.

When to use each

  • Brand new account: Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks initially, just to get spend
  • Getting conversion data: Maximize Conversions
  • About 30 conversions per month per ad group: consider Target CPA when performance is stable
  • E-commerce with revenue tracking: Target ROAS
  • Large brands with multiple objectives: portfolios of bidding strategies

The smart bidding reality

Google recommends about 30 conversions per month per ad group when consistent Target CPA performance matters. Other strategies and account histories differ. This is guidance, not an algorithm cutoff.

Changing28 Sep 2026Source 01

Bid adjustments

Adjust bids by device, location, time of day, audience. Useful for narrowing or scaling. Less needed with smart bidding (it does this automatically).

Do this today

  1. Record conversion volume, lag, value accuracy, budget constraint, current strategy, and recent target changes.
  2. Decision tree: If conversions are not measured, fix measurement before automated conversion bidding. If every conversion has similar verified value, test Maximize conversions, with an optional target CPA after stable volume. If values differ and reliable values return to Google Ads, test Maximize conversion value, with an optional target ROAS.
  3. Wait through the known conversion lag before judging changes. Make one target change at a time, record the date, and compare sales-accepted outcomes. Do not use a target to hide poor conversion definitions.
  4. Decision rule: Change one bid input at a time and wait through the measured conversion lag.
  5. Limitation: A stable platform CPA can hide falling lead quality when values return late or not at all.

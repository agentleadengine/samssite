Meta audiences and targeting

4 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Pre-2021, Meta targeting was surgical: obscure interests, narrow demographics, custom combinations. Post-iOS 14, signal loss has made broad targeting often win. The platform's AI now does most of the targeting - your creative does the rest.

Audience types

Broad

Country + age range. Let Meta's AI find the conversions. Post-iOS 14 best practice for most advertisers.

Interest targeting

Target people interested in specific pages, topics. Less precise than it once was. Still useful for niche products.

Lookalike audiences (LAL)

Build from a permitted, quality-controlled seed and record the selected country and audience-size setting. Compare it with broad targeting on incremental qualified outcomes, not similarity labels alone.

Custom audiences

Your own data uploaded. Website visitors, customer list, video viewers, engaged IG/FB users.

Saved audiences

Combinations of interests + demographics you save for reuse.

The broad vs narrow debate

  • Broad: use as one hypothesis when the objective and event quality are reliable.
  • Narrow interest: use as a separate hypothesis when the category or eligibility requirement is specific.
  • Decision: hold the offer and creative family constant, then compare qualified outcomes and reach.

Exclusions

  • Exclude current customers from prospecting
  • Exclude purchasers for the measured repurchase cycle, unless the campaign is explicitly for replenishment or cross-sell
  • Exclude email subscribers from newsletter-signup campaigns

Geography

Default to the country you ship to. Narrow further (state, city, radius) only if your offer is local or you've proven a geographic pattern.

Demographics

Age range, gender. Only narrow if you have real data that a demographic doesn't convert. Most advertisers over-narrow here and miss buyers.

Placements

Treat automatic and manual placements as competing hypotheses. Hold audience, creative, objective, and spend constant. Compare qualified conversion rate and incremental outcomes.

Do this today

  1. List broad, customer-seed, interest, and exclusion cells with the same objective, creative, and conversion event.
  2. Hypothetical matrix: Broad eligible geography versus customer-list lookalike versus site retargeting. Use the same offer and creative. Exclude customers and staff from prospecting. Keep retargeting separate because its prior intent differs.
  3. For prospecting, decide on sales-accepted leads per assigned impression or a supported lift test. For retargeting, require incremental evidence or a holdout before increasing budget. Do not pick an audience from click-through rate alone.
  4. Decision rule: Choose from qualified conversion cost or measured lift after checking audience overlap.
  5. Limitation: Overlap and delivery optimization can blur the audience contrast.

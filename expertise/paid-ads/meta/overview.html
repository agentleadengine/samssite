Meta Ads overview

4 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Meta Ads serves Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and Audience Network from one system. It's the dominant paid channel for DTC, consumer products, local services, and many B2B plays. Post-iOS 14 it's harder but not dead.

The hierarchy

  • Account: your top-level ad account
  • Campaign: defines the objective (traffic, conversions, etc.)
  • Ad Set: audience, placement, budget, schedule
  • Ad: the creative

Key objectives

  • Sales: optimizing for purchases - most DTC uses this
  • Leads: for form submits, appointments
  • Awareness: reach-based; rarely worth it for direct response
  • Traffic: sends clicks, cheaper CPMs but lower conversion
  • Engagement: likes/comments - mostly for social proof building

What changed post-iOS 14

A May 2023 industry report said Meta removed the requirement to prioritize only eight web conversion events per domain. It also reported that Aggregated Event Measurement still affects how some events are processed.

Reported28 Sep 2026Source 03
  • Pixel, Conversions API, modeled results, and CRM outcomes can report different totals
  • Audience performance depends on the seed, consent, objective, and account history
  • Broad and defined audiences should be compared with the same objective and qualified outcome
  • Event setup should reflect the selected objective and the business outcome returned from the CRM
  • Conversions API can supplement browser events; deduplicate matching browser and server events

Where to start

  1. Set up conversion tracking (Pixel + CAPI)
  2. One campaign with the minimum structure needed for the chosen objective and comparison
  3. Use a broad eligible audience as a documented hypothesis, subject to location and legal limits
  4. Daily test budget = allowable CPA times desired conversions, divided by the learning window in days
  5. Optimize for purchase or lead conversion event
  6. Replace or expand creative when the prewritten performance and capacity rule is met

Don't start with

  • 10 campaigns at once
  • Interest-based narrow targeting
  • Image-only static creative
  • Conversion events you haven't implemented yet

Do this today

Hypothetical launch plan: A local roof inspector wants qualified inspection requests, not cheap form fills.

InputPlan
Objective and eventLeads campaign, optimized to a CRM-returned qualified-lead event
Allowable cost$80 per qualified lead, based on margin and close rate
Funded learning15 qualified leads × $80 = $1,200 planning budget
WindowDaily budget = $1,200 divided by the chosen test days
CreativeRoof-damage walk-through, inspection checklist, and owner explanation
TrackingPixel plus Conversions API, deduplicated event ID, CRM lead status
Stop rulePause for broken tracking, wrong geography, unsafe comments, or spend beyond the funded limit

Expected conversion rate is a planning input, not a Meta promise. Reconcile form fills with accepted leads before changing the audience or creative.

Limitation: A small local audience may not produce the planned event count inside the funded window.

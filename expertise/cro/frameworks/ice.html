ICE scoring

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

ICE ranks experiment ideas by three factors, each 1-10. Product is the score.

Impact

How big if it works?

Confidence

How sure are you it'll work?

Ease

How fast can you ship and test?

Output

Rank backlog by ICE score. Run top-scored first.

Critique

Subjective scoring. Use for relative ordering, not absolute decisions.

Do this today

Hypothetical scored backlog: Each person scores impact, confidence, and ease from 1 to 10. The total is the product of all three scores.

HypothesisProductDesignAnalyticsAverage total
Remove optional company field8 × 8 × 9 = 5767 × 9 × 9 = 5676 × 7 × 8 = 336493
Add delivery date near CTA7 × 6 × 8 = 3368 × 8 × 7 = 4487 × 5 × 8 = 280355
Rewrite the hero9 × 7 × 6 = 3788 × 8 × 7 = 4484 × 3 × 7 = 84303

The hero has the widest disagreement. Analytics scored impact and confidence lower because no search, survey, or behavior evidence names a message problem.

Do not average that disagreement away. Add a comprehension test and query-to-page review, then rescore. Test the field removal first if event validation confirms the field causes exits.

Limitation: ICE orders beliefs. It does not turn weak evidence into a reliable forecast.

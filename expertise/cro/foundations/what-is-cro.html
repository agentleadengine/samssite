What is CRO?

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) is the discipline of improving the percentage of visitors who take a desired action. It combines qualitative research with quantitative testing.

The CRO loop

  • Research: find the problems
  • Hypothesize: what to change
  • Test: A/B the change
  • Measure: did it move the metric?
  • Learn and iterate

What counts as conversion

  • Purchase
  • Signup
  • Demo booking
  • Add-to-cart
  • Email capture

Forecast without a lift promise

Start with observed visitors, conversion rate, order value, margin, and guardrails. Apply a hypothetical rate only as a scenario, then replace it with the measured interval after the test.

Not just for e-commerce

SaaS signup conversion, B2B lead form completion, and content subscriptions can all be measured with downstream quality.

Hypothetical worked funnel: This example shows the arithmetic. It does not report a client result.

Visitors1,0001,000Eligible sessions after bot and staff exclusions
Purchases4045Conversion rate: 4.0% versus 4.5%
Revenue$4,000$4,275Revenue per visitor: $4.00 versus $4.28
Gross-margin revenue$2,600$2,56565% versus 60% after product mix

The variant converts more visitors and earns more revenue per visitor. It produces less gross-margin revenue, so it should not ship on conversion rate alone.

Guardrails: Compare refunds, support contacts, fulfillment time, and repeat purchase quality for the same observation window.

Decision rule: Keep the change only when the primary value metric improves and every predeclared guardrail stays acceptable.

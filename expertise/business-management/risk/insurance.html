Business insurance

6 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Insurance transfers specified risks under a written policy. Start with the events your business could not comfortably fund itself. Then compare forms, exclusions, limits, and duties with a licensed broker.

The essential policies

General Liability (GL)

Ask how the form handles third-party bodily injury, property damage, products, locations, subcontractors, and contractual requirements. The policy wording controls.

Professional Liability / E&O (Errors & Omissions)

Map the services you promise against covered professional acts, retroactive dates, claim-reporting rules, and exclusions. A customer contract may require different terms.

Cyber / Data Breach

List the systems and personal data you hold. Compare incident response, forensic work, notification, business interruption, ransomware, and vendor-event terms.

Directors & Officers (D&O)

Review who qualifies as an insured, which entities are covered, and how the form treats investors, regulators, insolvency, and prior acts.

Employment Practices Liability (EPLI)

Compare covered employment claims, defense arrangements, deductibles, exclusions, and notice duties. Ask an employment lawyer about prevention and contract questions.

Workers' Comp

Check duties in every state where people work. Use the state regulator and a licensed broker rather than a universal employee count or limit.

Employee Benefits (Health, Dental, 401k Fiduciary)

Separate benefit-plan administration from fiduciary exposure. Ask the broker and benefits counsel which policies and bonds apply to the plans you actually offer.

Claims process

  • Save the incident time, people involved, damage, photos, contracts, and witness details.
  • Notify the broker or carrier through the policy's stated channel.
  • Do not promise coverage or fault before the carrier and counsel review the record.
  • Track claim number, adjuster, requested documents, deadlines, reserve, and decision.

Build coverage from exposures, not universal limits

Workers compensation programs and duties vary by jurisdiction. The US Department of Labor administers specific federal programs and links employers to state officials.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 01

Objective statements about policy coverage need a reasonable basis. Read the current form, endorsements, exclusions, and definitions before describing protection.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 02

Give a licensed broker your state, payroll, revenue, contracts, property, data handled, and prior claims. Ask for comparable written quotes. Check workers compensation duties with the state regulator. Ask an insurance lawyer about contract language or disputed exclusions.

Where it breaks: A coverage name is not a coverage promise. Definitions, exclusions, endorsements, state rules, and late notice can change the result.

Do this today

  1. Export insurance terms from customer contracts.
  2. List locations, payroll states, data, and equipment.
  3. Request comparable written quotes.
  4. Record why each limit and exclusion was accepted.

Sender reputation

3 min readUpdated 2026-04-19

Every sending domain and IP has a reputation score with each email provider. Higher = better inbox placement. Built and lost over time.

Signals that help

  • Low bounce rate
  • High engagement
  • Low complaints
  • Consistent volume
  • Good content

Signals that hurt

  • Spike in volume
  • High bounce from bad addresses
  • Complaint rates above 0.1%
  • Sending to unengaged addresses

Monitoring

  • Google Postmaster Tools (Gmail)
  • Microsoft SNDS (Outlook)
  • Sender Score (general)

What to do with this

  • Check Gmail Postmaster Tools + Microsoft SNDS weekly, they are the direct view into what Gmail and Outlook think of your domain
  • Keep spam complaint rate under 0.1%, above that reputation starts degrading and recovery takes weeks
  • Never buy email lists, reputation damage from bad lists takes months to recover and sometimes requires new domains
  • Maintain steady daily volume, reputation algorithms prefer consistency and treat volume spikes as suspicious
  • When reputation drops, pause sending to that domain, continuing through degradation compounds the damage

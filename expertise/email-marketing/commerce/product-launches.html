Product launches

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Pre-launch

  • Waitlist signup
  • Behind-the-scenes content

Launch day

  • Morning announcement
  • Afternoon follow-up (most opens happen later)

Post-launch

  • Social proof emails (reviews come in)
  • Restock / last-chance emails
  • Post-mortem offer (if not selling through)

Where this breaks and how to measure it

Separate the launch list by prior purchase behavior and product interest. Use a holdout to estimate incremental orders and margin across the complete launch window.

Do this today

  1. Segment the launch list, VIPs get early access + a different send cadence than cold traffic
  2. Run the last-chance email with genuine scarcity (closing date, limited inventory), manufactured urgency is detected and unsubscribes spike
  3. Send a post-launch recap + secondary offer if sell-through was weak, catches late movers

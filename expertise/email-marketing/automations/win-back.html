Win-back

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

Typical sequence

Don't over-invest

Most win-back attempts fail. That's fine. Cleaning the list is the other goal.

Where this breaks and how to measure it

Define inactivity from the product's normal purchase cycle. Hold out part of the eligible cohort and compare incremental margin after discounts and returns.

Do this today

  1. Offer a real reason to return (new feature, exclusive content, discount), generic "we miss you" emails rarely work
  2. End with an explicit breakup, "reply to stay or we'll remove you" catches late engagement and cleans the list
  3. Remove non-responders from active campaigns, the deliverability benefit of a clean list outweighs the lost potential
  4. Segment win-back by past behavior, lapsed customers need a different message than never-purchased subscribers

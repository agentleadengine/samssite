Abandoned cart

3 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

When a shopper adds items to cart and leaves without buying, automated emails nudge them back. The highest-ROI email automation for DTC.

Typical sequence

SMS vs email

SMS has higher open rates but lower per-message revenue. Use together for max lift.

Don't over-discount

Training customers to abandon for a discount. Use sparingly.

Where this breaks and how to measure it

Randomly hold out eligible carts by product category. Count incremental completed orders and margin after discounts, returns, and message costs.

Do this today

  1. Use started-checkout as the input and confirm that order-completed events arrive with the same customer identifier.
  2. Build a purchaser exclusion, consent check, delay, and one test branch by cart value. The expected output is a flow diagram with every trigger and suppression rule.
  3. Place test orders and confirm that buyers exit before any reminder. Event-sync delay and cross-device identity are limitations, so leave a safety buffer.
  4. Hold out a comparable group and report recovered orders, gross margin, unsubscribes, and complaints after one fixed window. Keep the branch only when incremental margin is positive.

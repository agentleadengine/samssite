Where this breaks and how to measure it

Map every automation from trigger to eligibility, suppression, message, conversion, and holdout. Report incremental revenue rather than attributed revenue alone.

Map the flow before writing messages

FieldExample decision
TriggerCart created or product event completed
EligibilityConsent and customer state allow this message
SuppressionPurchase, opt-out, support issue, or conflicting flow
HoldoutRandom eligible group receives no automation
OutcomeIncremental margin or product activation

Draw the branch that exits the flow before the branch that sends the next message. That is where duplicate and badly timed messages usually begin.

  1. Choose one automation.
  2. Write its trigger, eligibility, and suppression in plain language.
  3. Add a holdout and one outcome metric.

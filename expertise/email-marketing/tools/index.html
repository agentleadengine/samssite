Picking an email tool

The right tool depends on use case. Here's how to pick.

Kit's checked pricing page counts unique active subscribers, lists unlimited broadcasts on Free, and lists unlimited Visual Automations, sequences, and app integrations on the displayed Creator tier. Its subscriber CSV includes status, tags, attribution, and custom fields.

Klaviyo's checked pricing page uses active profiles for email plans, shows 250 active profiles and 500 monthly email sends on Free, includes automations there, and lists more than 350 built-in integrations. Its CSV export omits event data and received-message history.

Mailchimp pricing varies by plan, contact tier, and sends. Contacts in multiple audiences can increase the total count. Automation supports documented trigger types, integrations vary by app, and account export has documented inclusions and exclusions.

Switching cost is the work to preserve consent, suppression, events, templates, integrations, and live automations. I would not choose from the feature matrix until one production-like flow and one complete export pass.

My takemigration check

Do this today

  1. Price the same active audience and monthly sends in all three tools.
  2. Build one trigger, branch, suppression rule, and test message in the leading option.
  3. Export subscribers, consent, suppressions, and one event sample. Record what is missing.
  4. Estimate the hours to rebuild templates, integrations, forms, and live automations.

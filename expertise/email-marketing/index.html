Email Marketing

Email marketing connects subscriber permission, delivery, lifecycle messages, and measurement. You can export an address, but you still depend on providers, authentication, privacy rules, and the reader's attention.

Match email to the reader's stage

AudienceMessage jobMeasure
New subscriberSet the promise and reach the first useful actionActivation and opt-outs
Active customerSupport the next relevant purchase or product actionIncremental conversion and margin
At-risk customerAddress a known barrier without over-messagingRetained use and complaints

Email still depends on providers, authentication, consent, list quality, and the reader's attention. Use a holdout when you need to know whether a message caused the outcome.

  1. Name one lifecycle stage.
  2. Choose one action the email should support.
  3. Define a holdout and a guardrail.

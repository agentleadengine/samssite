Jurisdiction map, checked 28 Sep 2026

RecipientDated factQuestion for counsel
United StatesCAN-SPAM covers commercial messages, including B2B. It requires accurate headers, a nondeceptive subject, a postal address, an opt-out, and prompt handling.Which state, sector, contract, and platform rules also apply to this sender and offer?
CanadaCASL requires consent, identification, and unsubscribe. Implied consent is limited and fact-specific.What evidence supports consent for each recipient, and when does that basis expire?
United KingdomPECR's electronic-mail rule does not apply to corporate subscribers. Sole traders and some partnerships need separate analysis.What is the subscriber's legal status, and what UK GDPR basis and notice are required?
AustraliaCommercial electronic messages require consent, accurate sender information, and a working unsubscribe.What evidence establishes consent, and is this message covered by the Spam Act?

CAN-SPAM does not require prior consent for every commercial email, but compliance with CAN-SPAM does not settle other rules.

My takeoperator boundary

Do this today

  1. Export ten proposed recipients with country, legal status, data source, and consent evidence.
  2. Attach the exact message, sender identity, privacy notice, and opt-out flow.
  3. Ask your lawyer which laws apply and what evidence, notice, and suppression process are required.

From ICP to a usable row

Start with role, company type, location, and a reason the problem is plausible. A usable record then needs a source URL, email status, last verification date, exclusion result, and campaign segment.

Worked record: Operations manager, commercial roofing company, Mid-Atlantic region, hiring signal confirmed on the company site, business email verified, no suppression match, segment set to seasonal staffing.

Audit checks

  • Open a random sample and confirm the person still holds the role.
  • Reject records with no traceable source or an ambiguous employer.
  • Keep exclusion reasons so the same bad record does not return later.

Do this today

  1. Add source, verification, and exclusion columns.
  2. Review a random sample manually.
  3. Remove the segment whose message would not change.

The pitch

One buyer problem, one promise, one proof element, and one response path.

Before and after

Before

We help companies improve efficiency with our innovative platform. Can I get time on your calendar?

After

You are hiring two dispatchers while the service area expands. I can show you a routing worksheet that exposes duplicate drive time. Worth a reply if that is on your list this quarter?

The second draft names an observable situation, a useful artifact, and a reply-sized question. It still needs factual research. Delete the line if the hiring signal is not verified.

Test the message, not your memory

Send one version to a randomized slice of the same audience. Keep the offer and sender constant. Judge the result on qualified positive replies, with complaints and opt-outs as guardrails.

Do this today

  1. Underline the claim that proves the email was researched.
  2. Replace a meeting request with a question the reader can answer in one sentence.
  3. Write the test metric before sending.

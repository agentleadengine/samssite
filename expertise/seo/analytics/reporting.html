SEO reporting

8 min readLast checked 2026-09-28

A useful monthly SEO report connects search visibility, site behavior, technical exceptions, and earned referrals to specific decisions. This page gives you six fields, an owner for each field, and a monthly review sequence.

Monthly SEO report template

FieldExample calculationOwnerDecision supported
Organic conversionsCompleted lead forms from organic sessionsAnalytics ownerKeep or change the page and offer
Conversion rateOrganic conversions divided by organic sessionsAnalytics ownerSeparate traffic growth from page performance
Query-class clicksSum clicks for local, comparison, and informational groupsSEO ownerChoose the next content update
Indexation exceptionCount priority URLs with a verified indexing issueTechnical ownerOpen or close an engineering ticket
Content change resultPost-change clicks minus baseline clicks for comparable periodsContent ownerKeep, revise, or revert the change
Qualified referral visitsVisits from earned links that reach a key pageOutreach ownerContinue or stop the tactic

Hypothetical example: a Raleigh roof repair page

Oak City Roofing is a hypothetical contractor. Its priority page is /raleigh-roof-repair/. The report covers one calendar month and the matching prior-year month. All outcome cells stay blank until the owner enters measured values from the linked Google reports.

FieldApplied setupDecision in this example
Organic conversionsFilter the GA4 landing-page report to /raleigh-roof-repair/ and Organic Search. Count only the generate_lead event fired after a completed estimate-request form. Enter the measured count or mark it unavailable when the event was not working.Keep the estimate form as the conversion event. Repair its tracking before judging the page if the event is unavailable.
Conversion rateDivide that page's measured generate_lead events by its organic sessions for the same month. Keep country, device, and consent settings unchanged in both periods.If organic sessions rise while the measured rate falls, review the form and offer before adding more traffic work.
Query-class clicksIn the Search Console Performance report, filter page to the exact priority URL, country to United States, device to mobile, and search type to Web. Export clicks for local queries containing raleigh or near me, service queries containing roof repair, and problem queries containing roof leak. Do not assign conversions to a query class.Choose the next copy update from the class losing measured clicks, after checking impressions and position for the same filters.
Indexation exceptionURL Inspection reports that the priority URL is not on Google because Google selected another canonical. Record both the declared and Google-selected canonical. This status is hypothetical, not a claim about a live page.Open a technical ticket to correct conflicting canonicals and internal links. Close it only after a live test and indexed result confirm the intended URL.
Content change resultRecord the publication date for one change: add the emergency roof-repair process, service area, and estimate-form call to action above the FAQ. Compare page clicks and impressions for matched periods before and after that date.Keep the change only when measured search performance improves without a weaker form conversion rate. Otherwise revise it and annotate the report.
Qualified referral visitsIn the GA4 Traffic acquisition report, isolate sessions from an earned local-chamber link. A visit qualifies only when it reaches /raleigh-roof-repair/ and fires generate_lead. Enter the measured count or unavailable when source or event data is missing.Continue outreach to similar local sources only after the qualification rule is met. A link or visit alone does not qualify.

Search Console supplies query and page clicks, impressions, and indexing evidence. GA4 supplies landing-page and traffic-source behavior when tagging and consent allow it. Google documents generate_lead for form-generated leads. Neither product can repair missing measurement history.

Monthly operating sequence

  1. Freeze the date range and filters.
  2. Update the six fields above.
  3. Explain the largest change with evidence.
  4. Assign one decision, one owner, and one due date.
  5. Carry unresolved exceptions into the next report.

Limitation: This template exposes decisions and exceptions, but it cannot correct missing consent, broken tagging, or an invalid comparison period.

Do this today

  1. Copy the six fields into a sheet.
  2. Name an owner for each row.
  3. Add the current baseline date range.
  4. Write the decision each row will support.

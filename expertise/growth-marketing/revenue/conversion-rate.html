Conversion rate

3 min readUpdated 2026-04-19

Conversion connects an eligible denominator with a defined business action. Improve it without sacrificing qualification, accessibility, or later retention.

Benchmarks

Do not import a benchmark from another product. Establish the current numerator and eligible denominator for the same offer, channel, segment, and observation window.

Levers

  • Offer and price clarity
  • Required information and error recovery
  • Evidence and terms needed for the decision
  • Page reliability and accessibility

Process

Register the hypothesis, minimum useful effect, sample, stopping rule, and quality guardrail before assignment. Reconcile the proxy with later business outcomes.

Avoid

  • Optimizing an easy proxy while qualified outcomes fall
  • Stopping when a favorable estimate first appears
  • Mixing ineligible traffic into the denominator

Worked conversion rate record

Denominator: eligible unique quote starts assigned before exposure. Numerator: server-recorded qualified quote submissions. Exclude staff, duplicates, and invalid regions before assignment; guardrail invalid submissions. Illustrative sample rule: for an 8% baseline, a 10% minimum useful rate, two-sided alpha 0.05, 80% power, and equal allocation, plan about 3,210 eligible starts per arm. Stop after the sample and two full weekly cycles, not when the estimate first looks favorable.

Where it breaks: The calculation does not repair bot starts, assignment leaks, or delayed qualification. A higher submit rate can still lower sales quality.

Do this today

  1. Export the baseline numerator and denominator.
  2. Set the minimum useful change.
  3. Calculate sample and duration.
  4. Lock the invalid and qualified-sale guardrails.

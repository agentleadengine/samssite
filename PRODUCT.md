## Platform

web

## Users

Two confirmed reader groups (Sam, 2026-09-28):

- **Business owners and office staff** at small businesses (1 to 25 people: insurance, real estate, law, nonprofits, trades). They want plain-English answers: which AI tool to use for a real task, what it costs, how to set it up, and what to check before trusting the output. Usually reading on a phone or a work laptop between tasks.
- **Builders**: people who want to make AI do work on its own (Claude, Claude Code, MCP, skills, plugins, agents, RAG). They arrive from search or YouTube looking for a specific concept and want a clear explanation plus working steps.

Secondary: people who finish learning and want help. They are sent to Agent Lead Engine (agentleadengine.com), Sam's business.

## Product Purpose

samuelochoa.com is an AI learning brand first (Sam, 2026-09-28). It is Samuel Ochoa's free library for learning to use AI at work: a handbook (the Framework), deep topic libraries (Expertise), industry playbooks, comparisons, a glossary, essays, and a daily AI brief email. Success = readers learn something they can use the same day, come back, and join the brief. Services are a quiet secondary path, not the lead.

## Positioning

Written by one person who sets these tools up for real small businesses, so it covers both ends: the owner deciding what to use and the builder wiring it up. Plain English, no hype, and honest about what AI gets wrong.

## Product Principles

1. Teach first. Every page should leave the reader able to do one thing they could not before.
2. Plain English, then depth. Owners get the short answer up top; builders can go deeper on the same page or the next one.
3. Dated and honest. Say when something was last checked, and say what the tool gets wrong.
4. One quiet path to help. Services never interrupt the lesson.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readers include non-technical owners on phones and Spanish speakers. Keep WCAG AA contrast, readable line lengths, real headings, and captions on all video.

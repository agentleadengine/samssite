Start with the quoted-but-not-bound queue.

The first candidate is not an AI receptionist or a new CRM. It is a visible list of open quotes: owner, last meaningful action, next action and due date. An internal exception queue lets a licensed team member decide what happens next.

Use commission, not premium, in the business case.

A $2,000 policy is not $2,000 of agency revenue. A missing follow-up is not a proven lost sale. The report separates measured records, estimated incremental commission and unknown outcomes. It accounts for costs and overlapping contacts. A zero-benefit outcome stays visible.

Questions before a call

Will this work with my AMS?

We confirm the actual platform, subscription and access route. If the required permissions or features are unavailable, I revise the scope or recommend no build. I do not claim universal AMS connectivity.

What if the report says not to automate?

That is a valid outcome. You still receive the agreed evidence and handoff. The audit fee buys the diagnosis, not a promised upsell or revenue number.

What if my agency has very few open quotes?

We may use a longer agreed window, choose a different process, or skip the paid audit. A thin sample does not justify a confident return-on-investment claim.

Can my team or another provider implement the plan?

Yes. You keep the delivered plan and documentation. If you ask me to build, scope and fixed price are agreed separately. Software licenses and usage remain separate.

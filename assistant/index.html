What would you text it?

Start with the work already on your phone. These are examples of requests, not automatic messages to clients.

“Draft a quote from these job notes for me to review.”

“Write a follow-up after my call with Jordan.”

“Summarize this document and list the decisions I need to make.”

“Remind me Friday at 9 to send the invoice.”

“Make my weekly plan from these meetings and deadlines.”

“Draft a reply to this customer review.”

Check every draft yourself, especially prices, dates, and promises. Agent Care does not answer your phone or send messages on its own.

Questions owners ask

Do I need to be technical?

No. I walk you through setup on a call. You sign in to your own accounts, and you can use the assistant by text afterward.

What does it cost all-in?

$400 one-time setup, then your plan plus server and AI.Free setup when you book from this page, through November 10, 2026. Then your plan plus server and AI. Care is about $133 to $148/month, Managed about $233 to $248/month, or Full Service about $383 to $398/month. Each estimate includes the plan, a DigitalOcean server at about $24 to $29/month, and an AI account at $10 to $20/month. iMessage via Photon is free.

What if I cancel?

Your server and data remain in your accounts. Contact me to stop the Agent Care plan. You control whether to keep or close the separate server and AI accounts.

Is my customer data safe?

Your server and data are in accounts you own. I never keep access to your chats, memory or files. On Managed, I change settings only through a fixed menu, and each change is logged. If I need more, your assistant texts you why and for how long. Nothing happens until you reply YES; access ends on its own, and you can say STOP anytime. It never reads your email inbox. Share only the customer information needed for a task, review outputs, and follow the data rules for your business.

Will it text my customers without asking?

No. It prepares drafts for you. You approve before anything is sent or published.

Can I use iMessage or Telegram?

Yes. You can choose either during setup. iMessage uses a free Photon account; Telegram is free too.

How long does setup take?

Start with a 15-minute call. If you move ahead, we schedule a one-hour setup call to build it with you in your own accounts.

When can I reach you?

Every day from 9 AM to 9 PM Eastern. Texts that come in later are answered the next morning. If something ever looks wrong, you can switch your assistant off yourself in the DigitalOcean app.

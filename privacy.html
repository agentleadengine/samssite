Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through Samuel Ochoa consulting pages is used.

Information collected

If you submit a form, I may collect your name, email, phone number, company or team name, website, and the workflow details you choose to share.

How information is used

I use this information to review your inquiry, respond to you, evaluate whether there is a fit for consulting, and improve the site and advertising performance.

Service providers

Form submissions and follow-up may be processed through third-party tools such as GoHighLevel, Google Ads, and related analytics or communication services. These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Communication

If you provide contact information, I may use it to respond to your inquiry. You can ask me to stop contacting you at any time.

Data retention

I keep inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for consulting, recordkeeping, and follow-up purposes.

Contact

Questions or removal requests can be sent to sam@samuelochoa.com.

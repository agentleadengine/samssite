Setting up Claude

Separate product facts, brand voice, and customer data before drafting.

An online store gets better copy when the product record is exact and the model never sees live customer or payment data.

Step 1: Choose a plan from live usage

Claude Free is $0. Pro is $20 monthly or $200 annually. Limits and features vary by plan and region. Confirm the live plan page before budgeting ecommerce work.

Changing28 Sep 2026Source 01

Start on Free and test a week of catalog and campaign drafting. Upgrade only when the live usage indicator shows that approved work is being blocked.

Step 2: Save the context that matters

  • Product example: a 24-ounce stainless steel bottle with a screw cap.
  • Audience: commuters who want a reusable bottle that fits a standard cup holder.
  • Voice samples: one approved product page, one shipping email, and one support reply.
  • Boundary: use only supplied materials, dimensions, care instructions, and return terms.

Step 3: Run a real prompt test

PromptWrite a 90-word product description for the 24-ounce Harbor Bottle. State stainless steel, screw cap, and standard cup-holder fit. Do not claim it is leakproof, dishwasher-safe, or insulated because those facts were not supplied. End with one care note: hand wash before first use.

Score the three supplied facts, zero invented features, correct care note, length, and brand tone. Compare the draft before and after the context file.

Step 4: Keep the example safe

Use invented or approved data

Use order DEMO-1042 with no real customer attached. Never paste card data, addresses, phone numbers, customer exports, fraud notes, or private supplier terms.

Free, Pro, and Max users control a model-improvement choice, while safety reviews and other opt-ins remain exceptions. Commercial Claude inputs and outputs are not used to train models by default, unless feedback or permission is supplied. Keep card data, delivery addresses, customer exports, fraud notes, and private supplier terms out of store prompts.

Checked28 Sep 2026Consumer data sourceCommercial data source

Step 5: Use Projects only when available

Claude Projects work across Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise, with five allowed on Free. Team and Enterprise support organization sharing. For a store, separate public catalog knowledge from internal support macros and control who can edit each.

Changing28 Sep 2026Source 03

Create "Product catalog drafts" with approved-products.csv, brand-voice.md, and product-claim-rules.md. Create "Customer service templates" with returns-policy.md and support-macros.md. Upload no order export, fraud case, or supplier contract.

Do this today

  1. Create one approved product-facts record.
  2. Collect three public brand samples.
  3. Run the Harbor Bottle prompt twice.
  4. Reject any draft that adds an unsupported feature.

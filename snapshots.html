The six workflows I am building

Planned

Missed-call response starter

I am preparing hours-based messages, a booking link, an owner alert, and a duplicate guard.

Workflow: Missed call → eligibility check → one acknowledgment → reply or human task → stop.

Planned

Appointment confirmation and rescheduling

I am putting confirmations, reminders, and cancellation branches in one workflow.

Workflow: Booking → confirmation → reminders → attended, cancelled, or no-show → appropriate staff follow-up.

Planned

AI FAQ and booking assistant

I am pairing an approved FAQ with a calendar action and a human fallback.

Workflow: Inquiry → approved answer → qualifying facts → booking or handoff → log.

Planned

Quote follow-up tracker

I am building pipeline stages, owner tasks, editable email drafts, and stop rules.

Workflow: Quote sent → due follow-up → reply, won, or lost check → next task or close.

Planned

After-hours AI intake

I am preparing fact-capture fields, owner routing, a fallback, and a test script.

Workflow: Incoming call → AI introduction → collect minimum facts → transfer or task → confirmation.

Planned

Review request and service recovery

I am building a neutral review request, an owner alert, and a frequency limit.

Workflow: Completed service → same review invitation for eligible customers → unhappy reply alerts staff.

I will include the setup requirements and a test checklist with each release. HighLevel software and message usage are separate from these free templates.

Portfolio reference, not a product offer

An insurance workflow prototype.

This page describes a CRM design concept explored in my work: make producer handoffs visible alongside an agency's systems. It is not a claim of production deployment, customer adoption, universal AMS integration or proven revenue recovery.

Not currently offered as a standalone CRM. The commercial work starts with a scoped audit of your existing workflow, not a replacement platform.

The design questions

  • Can a producer see who owns each open quote and what happens next?
  • Can an internal exception queue surface overdue work without creating duplicate tasks?
  • Can staff review and approve a proposed action before it reaches a customer?
  • Can the workflow preserve permissions, suppression rules and an audit trail?

These are design goals, not verified capabilities of a system in your environment. Platform permissions, licensed features and data-handling requirements must be checked for every engagement.

What is not automated here

Coverage recommendations, binding, cancellation, underwriting and claims decisions stay with authorized licensed people. A prototype does not establish regulatory compliance or authorization to access any carrier or agency system.

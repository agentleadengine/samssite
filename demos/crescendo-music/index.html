Practice that actually earns its half hour.

Private, one-on-one piano, guitar, violin and voice lessons on Long Island, NY. Weekly, at the same time, with a teacher who plans the whole term around a piece you actually want to play.

Thirty minutes a week, and something to play by spring

Every student gets a plan built around a piece they actually want to learn — the technique comes along for the ride. Anyone who wants to is welcome to play it at the studio's recital each June.

What we teach

All lessons are private and weekly. Beginners are welcome on every instrument, and adults starting over after a long gap are too.

Piano

Ages 6 and up

Reading, hand position and pedaling, built around real repertoire from method-book beginners through classical and pop arrangements.

Guitar

Acoustic and electric

Chords and strumming from day one, then fingerstyle, scales, barre chords, and the songs you bring in. Amps and picks are here for lessons.

Voice

Teens and adults

Breath support, range, pitch and diction, plus how to work a microphone, building toward a small set list.

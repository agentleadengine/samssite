What we teach

All lessons are private and weekly. Beginners are welcome on every instrument, and we work with adults who are starting over after a long gap. Each section below covers what a lesson actually includes and who tends to need it.

Piano

Ages 6 and up

Reading, hand position and pedaling built around real repertoire, from method-book beginners through classical and pop arrangements.

Students practice on their own instrument at home — we're glad to help you pick one out if you don't have it yet.

Guitar

Acoustic and electric

Chords and strumming from day one, so there's something to play by the second week, then fingerstyle, scales, barre chords and the songs you bring in.

Amps and picks are here for lessons; practicing at home just takes your own guitar.

Violin

Ages 5 and up

Posture, bow hold and intonation taught slowly and carefully, with sizing help for younger students.

Good preparation for a school orchestra audition, if that's the goal — plenty of students are just here to learn.

Voice

Teens and adults

Breath support, range, pitch and diction, plus how to work a microphone.

We build a small set list together, so there's always something ready to sing.

Music theory

Add-on or standalone

Key signatures, intervals, chord construction and ear training.

Useful for students preparing for a school placement test, or players who want to understand what their hands already do.

Adult beginners

No experience needed

A slower, no-pressure track for adults who always meant to learn.

No recital requirement, and no assigned homework unless you ask for it.

Weekly private lessons start at $45 for a half hour. Terms run September through June, with a shorter summer session for anyone who wants to keep going.

Not sure which instrument yet?

Tell us the student's age and what they're drawn to and we'll talk it through — no obligation, no pressure.

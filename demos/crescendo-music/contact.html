A few common questions

Do I need to own an instrument?

For piano and violin, yes — you'll need one at home to practice on, and we're glad to help you find one. Guitar amps and picks are here for lessons. Voice needs nothing but you.

Is there a recital requirement?

There's a low-key recital each June for anyone who wants to play. It's optional, not required — some students play every year, some never do.

What happens at the first lesson?

A full-length lesson so the teacher and student can get a feel for each other, and to hear where things stand. From there we find a weekly slot that fits.

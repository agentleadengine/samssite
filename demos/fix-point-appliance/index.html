How the visit actually goes

No dispatcher, no guessing on the phone. Just what happens once we're at your door.

1

You call with the model number

It's on a sticker inside the door, behind the crisper drawer, or along the door frame — it tells us what to load before we leave the shop.

2

We diagnose it in your kitchen

The diagnostic is $89. Approve the repair and it comes off the total. Don't, and that's all you owe.

We fix what we can, right there

Belts, pumps, igniters, thermostats, and door seals ride on the truck. Anything else gets ordered and scheduled the day it lands.

When it's not worth fixing

If the repair runs more than about half the cost of a comparable new unit, we say so before anything is ordered. That's your call to make, not ours.

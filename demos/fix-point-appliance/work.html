A few calls, and what we found

No client names, no addresses — just the symptom, what we found, and what it took to fix it. Six sample projects, one per appliance.

Sample project

Refrigerator — warm on the door side

SymptomWarm milk on the door shelf; everything else in the fridge stayed at temp.
FoundA soft spot in the door seal near the bottom hinge, letting warm air wash the top shelf.
FixedDoor seal replaced. The gasket was already on the truck.
Sample project

Washer — walks across the floor

SymptomA front-load unit inching away from the wall on every spin cycle.
FoundTwo of the four suspension springs had stretched out, so the drum swung instead of spinning true.
FixedSprings replaced and the unit re-leveled. No wobble left on re-test.
Sample project

Dryer — three cycles to dry one load

SymptomHeat felt fine, but towels stayed damp cycle after cycle.
FoundA long flexible section of the vent run was packed solid with lint, choking the airflow.
FixedVent cleared and a rigid section swapped in so it won't repack as fast.
Sample project

Dishwasher — standing water after every cycle

SymptomAbout an inch of cloudy water sitting in the tub at the end of the wash.
FoundA chip of glass wedged against the drain pump impeller, stalling it mid-spin.
FixedPump cleared and run through two full cycles before we left.
Sample project

Oven — burner clicks, won't light

SymptomIgniter clicking steadily, no flame, a faint gas smell after a few seconds.
FoundA weak igniter no longer drawing enough current to open the gas valve in time.
FixedIgniter swapped. The burner lit on the first click again.
Sample project

Microwave — dead after the door latch failed

SymptomUnit went completely dark — no light, no turntable — though the door still closed fine.
FoundThe interlock switch behind the latch had failed open, which shuts the whole unit down as a safety lock.
FixedInterlock switch replaced. Full function restored the same visit.

Yours probably has a name like one of these.

Call with the symptom and the model number and we'll tell you what to expect.

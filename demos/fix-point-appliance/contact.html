A few things people ask first

Q.What does the $89 diagnostic cover?

It covers the visit and the answer: what's wrong and what it costs to fix. Approve the repair and the $89 comes off the total. Don't, and that's all you owe.

Q.Do you carry parts on the truck?

The common ones, yes — belts, pumps, igniters, thermostats, door seals. If your repair needs something else, we order it and schedule the fix for the day it lands.

Q.What if it's not worth fixing?

We'll tell you before anything is ordered. If the repair would run more than about half the cost of a comparable new unit, that's worth knowing before you spend a dollar on the old one.

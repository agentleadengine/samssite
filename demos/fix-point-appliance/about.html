One van. One technician. No dispatcher in between.

Fix Point is a one-van operation. Dave answers the phone, drives the van, and does the diagnosis himself — there's no dispatcher guessing at what's wrong before anyone's seen the machine. If a fridge is warm or a washer won't drain, the person who shows up at the door is the person who fixes it.

The idea behind the $89 diagnostic is simple: you should know what's actually wrong and what it costs before anyone touches a screwdriver. If the fix is worth it, that fee comes off the bill. If it's not — if a repair would cost more than putting that money toward a new unit — that gets said out loud too, before anything is ordered.

Fix Point runs in-home calls across Long Island, NY — apartments, condos, and single-family homes, all on the same two-hour arrival window. Saturday mornings tend to fill first, so calling early in the week is the best way to lock one in.

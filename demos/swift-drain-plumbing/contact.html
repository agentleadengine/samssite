A few things people ask first

Is the $149 the final price?

No. That's the diagnostic and service-call fee. If you have us do the repair, it comes off your bill. If not, that's what the visit costs.

Do you work weekends?

Saturday, yes, 8am to 4pm. Sunday is emergencies only: an active leak, no water in the house, or sewage backing up. Everything else gets the next open slot.

What if you get here and it's not what I thought?

We look, explain what's actually wrong in plain words, and give you a flat number before opening anything. You decide whether to go ahead.

Still not sure? Just call.

Describe what's going on and we'll tell you what it sounds like, no charge for the conversation.

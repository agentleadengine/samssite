Residential plumbing, start to finish

Houses on Long Island run the gamut: 1920s galvanized, 1960s copper, new PEX. We work on all of it, and every call gets the same four steps.

  • Same-day slotsMost weeks. Call and we'll tell you straight if we can't.
  • Flat price, quoted at the doorIt doesn't move once we open the toolbag.
  • Sundays: emergencies onlyActive leak, no water, or sewage backing up.
Most calls start at $149.

That's the diagnostic and service-call fee. If you have us do the repair, it comes off your bill. If not, that's what the visit costs.

Drain & Sewer Cleaning

What's included: We cable or hydro-jet the line, then run a camera so you see whether it was grease, roots, or a belly in the pipe, not just hear it secondhand.

When you need it: kitchen sink backing up, tub draining slow, or the main line gurgles when the washer runs.

Water Heaters

What's included: Diagnosis and repair on tank units, from a thermocouple relight to a full swap, with a straight answer on whether it's worth fixing or replacing.

When you need it: no hot water, a pilot that won't stay lit, or rusty water at the tap.

Leaks & Pipe Repair

What's included: We find where it's actually coming from, cut in a proper repair on copper, PVC or PEX, and leave the opening ready for patching.

When you need it: pinholes in old copper, cracked PVC, or a supply line that let go behind the wall.

Fixtures, Faucets & Toilets

What's included: Running toilets, dripping valves, new fixture installs, and shutoff valves that haven't turned in years. Small jobs get scheduled, not brushed off.

When you need it: anything that drips, runs, or won't shut off the way it should.

Sump Pumps & Basements

What's included: Pump testing and replacement, battery backup systems, check valves and discharge lines, done before the season needs them.

When you need it: worth handling in late summer, not during the first big storm.

Repipes & Remodels

What's included: Galvanized swapped for copper or PEX, rough-in for a new bathroom or kitchen, gas lines for a range. Straight runs, proper supports, work that passes inspection.

When you need it: planning a remodel, or tired of low pressure from old galvanized pipe.

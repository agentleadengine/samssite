Six jobs, the way we'd actually explain them

These are drawn scenes, not photos of a specific house, since we're not putting a customer's home on the internet. Each one is the kind of call we get and how we handled it.

Sample Project

Root Intrusion on a Main Line

The washer drained fine on its own but backed up with anything else running. Camera found roots at a joint about 40 feet out. Jetted the line clear and confirmed with a second pass.

Sample Project

Pilot Light That Wouldn't Hold

No hot water for two days. The thermocouple had failed, not the whole tank. Swapped it the same visit and the pilot held steady.

Sample Project

Pinhole in a Kitchen Wall

A slow stain on the ceiling below turned out to be a pinhole in 1960s copper. Cut in a clean section, soldered, and tested under pressure before closing the wall.

Sample Project

Shutoff Valve, Frozen Solid

The under-sink valve hadn't turned in years, so a faucet swap became a bigger job than planned. Replaced the valve and both supply lines while we were in there.

Sample Project

Sump Pump Before Storm Season

The old pump still ran, but the check valve was failing and there was no backup. Installed a new pump with a battery backup ahead of the fall rains.

Sample Project

Galvanized Out, PEX In

Original 1950s galvanized pipe had narrowed enough to drop pressure on the second floor. Repiped the run through the basement ceiling in PEX.

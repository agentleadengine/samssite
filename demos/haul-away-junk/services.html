Six kinds of piles, one truck

From a single item at the curb to a full contractor load, it's the same crew, the same truck, and pricing you hear before anything gets carried out.

Garage & Basement Cleanouts

The corner that's been "get to it later" for a year, cleared in one visit.

What's included

  • Stacked boxes and totes
  • Broken shelving and old storage units
  • Exercise equipment nobody uses anymore
  • Half-empty bins from the last move

When you need it

The garage bay or the basement corner has become storage for things that are never coming back out.

Furniture & Mattresses

Couches, bed frames and mattresses, carried out, stairs and doorways included.

What's included

  • Sofas, sectionals and recliners
  • Bed frames, headboards and dressers
  • Mattresses and box springs

When you need it

A new piece is on the way and the old one has to leave first, including down a flight of stairs.

Appliance Removal

The old unit, disconnected and out the door.

What's included

  • Refrigerators and freezers
  • Washers and dryers
  • Water heaters
  • Window AC units

When you need it

The old appliance is already disconnected and pulled from the wall, ready for us to carry out.

Construction Debris

Demo debris and job-site leftovers, hauled on the crew's schedule.

What's included

  • Demo debris and old cabinets
  • Countertops and tile
  • Decking and fence sections
  • Contractor loads, working off a photo the night before

When you need it

A job wraps and renting a dumpster for the driveway isn't worth it for one truckload.

Estate & Rental Turnovers

A property cleared and swept, ready for the next showing.

What's included

  • Whole-house clear-outs
  • Between-tenant turnovers
  • Items marked to keep are set aside first

When you need it

A listing or a new tenant is waiting on an empty, broom-clean space.

Yard Waste & Storm Debris

Whatever the yard, or the last storm, left behind.

What's included

  • Branches and brush
  • Old lumber and fence sections
  • Pool liners
  • Bagged or loose, both work

When you need it

A storm just came through, or the cleanup made more than the town pickup will take.

You pay by how much space it takes

Send a photo and we give you a number that holds. Nothing gets added once the truck is loaded.

Heavy material like tile, concrete and roofing is priced by weight instead of volume, because that is what the transfer station charges us. We say so on the phone, not at the curb.

What we can't take

Paint and solvents, motor oil, propane tanks, car tires, asbestos, and anything from a medical waste bin. If you're not sure what a container holds, leave it sealed and ask us on the phone. We'll tell you where it can go instead.

Describe the pile on the phone and we'll tell you the tier before we ever see the driveway.

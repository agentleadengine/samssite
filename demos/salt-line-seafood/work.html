Orders like yours

A handful of the ways people order from the window. Each one's a stand-in built to show how it works, not an actual receipt.

Beach Day Cooler Run

Sample project

A dozen fried baskets called in for noon, packed to travel so the fries survive the walk to the sand. Ice goes in the same bag as the drinks, not the food.

Boat Slip Pickup

Sample project

Someone ties off at the dock, calls from the boat, and the order's bagged and waiting by the time they walk up. No need to shut the engine off for long.

Backyard Clambake

Sample project

Steamers by the pound plus a few boil bags, called in a day ahead so we can plan the buy. Picked up in coolers, ready for a folding table in someone's yard.

Little League Team Basket

Sample project

Fifteen kids' plates called in after a Saturday game, fish sticks for the ones who won't touch a clam yet. One ticket, one pickup time, no chaos at the window.

Sunday Steamer Bucket

Sample project

A couple of steamer buckets and two lobster rolls, cold, called in for a quiet dinner at home. The kind of order that comes in every week without fail.

Your order's next

Whatever the occasion, call ahead and we'll plan around it.

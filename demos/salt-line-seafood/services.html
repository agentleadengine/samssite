The full board

Six things we make well. Nothing sits under a lamp, everything's cooked when your ticket prints.

Fried Baskets

Starting at $16

Clams, shrimp, haddock or sea scallops in our light cornmeal batter, dropped when the ticket comes in. Served with fries, slaw and a lemon wedge.

When you need it: a quick lunch at the window, one basket each.

Lobster Rolls

Starting at $26

Split-top roll griddled in butter, packed full. Cold with a little mayo and celery, or warm with drawn butter on the side. Tell us which one when you call.

When you need it: the one order everyone at the table agrees on.

Steamers & Mussels

Starting at $18

Steamed by the pound with white wine, garlic and parsley. Comes with the broth for dipping and a cup of butter. Bread on request.

When you need it: a slow dinner at the picnic tables, not a rush.

The Boil Bag

Starting at $34

Shrimp, corn, red potatoes and smoked sausage tossed in garlic butter and sealed in the bag so it stays hot on the ride home. Built to feed two.

When you need it: feeding two without a single plate.

Off the Grill

Starting at $22

Swordfish, salmon or whatever whole fish came in that morning, cooked over the flame with lemon and olive oil. Comes with rice and grilled vegetables.

When you need it: the lighter order, still off the boat.

Sides & Kids' Plates

Starting at $5

Hush puppies, corn on the cob, extra slaw, fries and a cup of chowder. For the little ones: fish sticks or chicken tenders with fries.

When you need it: rounding out a table, or feeding a kid who won't touch a clam.

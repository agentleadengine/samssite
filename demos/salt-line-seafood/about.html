Behind the window

Salt Line started as a folding table at the end of a pier, a cooler of clams and a two-burner fryer plugged into an extension cord that ran back to Denny's truck. The idea was simple: keep the menu short, keep the oil hot, and don't make anyone wait longer than it takes to cook their food right.

That's still how the window runs. The board only has six things on it because six things done right beats twenty things done fast. Whatever whole fish comes in that morning gets broken down in the back, and whatever doesn't sell that day doesn't go on the board the next.

Most people walk up and order at the window. Regulars call ahead so their food's ready when they pull in. Either way works, the fryer doesn't know the difference.

Where we are

Salt Line is a walk-up shack serving Long Island, NY. Order at the window and eat at the picnic tables out front, or call ahead and take it with you.

We also pack larger orders for beach days, boat trips and backyard parties. Call the day before so we can plan the buy.

A Few Common Questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in anytime the shop's open. If you'd rather have a slot held, call ahead and we'll give you a real time, not a guess.

What if I don't know exactly what I want?

Say so in the chair. Thirty seconds of talking about guard numbers and length saves you three bad weeks of growing it out.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, any age. The booster seat's out, and parents can sit right next to the chair.

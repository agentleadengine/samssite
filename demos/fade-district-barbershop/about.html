Inside Fade District

Two chairs, one door, and a shop built around getting the line right instead of getting you out fast.

Fade District started with the same problem every good barbershop solves: you know what a clean fade looks like, and most of what you get isn't that. So the shop is built around one thing, getting the blend right, and everything else works around it.

There's no app to book through and no membership to sign up for. Call the shop or walk in, and whoever's free tells you the honest wait right then. If both chairs are going, we'll say so instead of making you guess and stand around.

We're set up for the neighborhoods around us on Long Island, from Nassau County through Suffolk, covering the North Shore and the South Shore both. If you're close enough to walk over or a short drive away, you're in the right place.

Walk-Ins Welcome

Most of the shop's chairs fill up with people who didn't call ahead. If you'd rather lock in a time, call and we'll give you a straight answer.

No Appointment Needed

Push the door open any hour the shop's listed as open. If it's busy, we'll tell you the real wait instead of a guess.

Prefer a Set Time?

Call the shop and ask for a slot. Someone picks up between cuts, and if it rings out we call back with the next open chair.

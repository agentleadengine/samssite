The Cut List

Straightforward pricing, said plainly. Every cut ends with a neck shave and a hot towel, no upcharge and no surprise at the register.

Skin Fade

Starting at $35
  • Clippers down to skin on the sides
  • Blended clean into whatever length you keep on top
  • Finished with a hot towel and neck shave

When you need itEvery three to four weeks if you want the line to stay sharp, longer if you're growing the top out.

Classic Taper

Starting at $30
  • Scissor work on top
  • Tight taper at the ears and neckline
  • No shaved line, so it grows out clean

When you need itGood for stretching to four weeks between visits, when you don't want to be back in the chair every month.

Beard Shape & Line

Starting at $22
  • Cheek line and neckline squared up
  • Edge-up with the straight razor
  • Shaped to your jaw, not to a number guard

When you need itEvery one to two weeks to keep the edges from creeping, or before a photo you can't redo.

Cut & Beard Combo

Starting at $50
  • The full haircut
  • Beard detail in the same seat
  • One visit, one seat, nothing split up

When you need itWhen you want to walk out finished. Budget about an hour if you want it done right.

Kids Cut (12 & under)

Starting at $25
  • Booster seat
  • As much time as a first cut takes
  • Parents can sit right next to the chair

When you need itFirst haircuts, school pictures, or a kid who's just done with the last one.

Straight-Razor Shave

Starting at $40
  • Hot lather
  • Two passes with the straight razor
  • Cold towel and balm to finish

When you need itBefore an interview, a wedding, or any day you want the closest shave you can get.

Ready for a Seat?

Call the shop and tell us which cut, or just walk in and we'll figure it out in the chair.

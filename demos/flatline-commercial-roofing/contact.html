Common questions

How do I know if my flat roof needs replacement or just repair?

The inspection answers that. If the membrane is blistering widely, has multiple failed seams, or is past its expected service life, replacement usually costs less over time than repeated repairs. If it's a single seam failure or a flashing issue, repair is the right call. We tell you both options and the reasoning.

Can you inspect my roof without me being there?

Yes, as long as we can access the roof safely. Building access can be arranged with a property manager or tenant coordinator. We'll need someone available by phone on the day of inspection in case we find something that changes the scope.

What's the difference between TPO and EPDM?

TPO is a white, heat-welded thermoplastic membrane — reflective, energy-efficient, and common on newer commercial roofs. EPDM is a black rubber membrane — durable, flexible, and proven in cold climates. We help you choose based on your building's usage, foot traffic, energy goals, and budget. Both are proven systems when installed correctly.

Services

Five flat-roof services for commercial and industrial buildings on Long Island. Each one is a specific scope with a specific deliverable — not a vague "we do everything" list.

SCOPE 01

Full Roof Replacement

When you need it: Membrane is past service life, multiple repairs are failing, or the roof has widespread blistering and ponding.

Tear-off of the existing membrane down to the deck, inspection of the substrate, and installation of a new flat roof assembly — membrane, insulation, cover board, and flashings.

  • Full tear-off and deck inspection
  • New insulation board installation
  • Membrane selection (TPO, EPDM, or modified bitumen based on building needs)
  • New edge metal and flashing details
  • Drain installation or re-routing as needed
  • Written condition report of the deck beneath
SCOPE 02

Leak Repair

When you need it: Active interior leaks, water stains on ceiling tiles, or pooled water finding its way inside after rain.

We trace the leak to its source — which is rarely directly above the interior stain. Water travels along decking and beams before it shows up inside. Every repair targets the actual entry point, not the visible symptom.

  • Full surface inspection to trace leak source
  • Seam repair and re-welding (TPO/EPDM)
  • Flashing repair at penetrations, curbs, and edges
  • Drain clearing and collar repair
  • Patch installation with compatible membrane
  • Written repair summary with photos
SCOPE 03

Storm Damage Inspection

When you need it: After high winds, hail, or heavy rain events — before minor damage becomes a major leak.

A documented post-storm condition survey of your flat roof. We walk the full surface, check every seam and flashing, photograph any damage, and hand you a written report with findings and recommended next steps.

  • Full surface walk and condition survey
  • Seam and flashing inspection
  • Membrane puncture and blister check
  • Drain and scupper clearing assessment
  • Photo-documented written report
  • Priority scheduling if repair is needed
SCOPE 04

Gutter Integration

When you need it: Flat roof drainage relies on scuppers, interior drains, or edge gutters — if any are undersized or poorly integrated, water backs up onto the membrane.

We install and repair the drainage components that make a flat roof actually shed water: edge gutters, scuppers, downspouts, and interior drain assemblies. Proper integration with the membrane is the difference between drainage that lasts and drainage that pulls away at the first heavy rain.

  • Edge gutter installation and replacement
  • Scupper box fabrication and repair
  • Downspout routing and securing
  • Interior drain collar integration
  • Drainage flow testing
  • Membrane-to-gutter flashing detail
SCOPE 05

Flat Roof Systems

When you need it: New construction, building addition, or converting a pitched roof to a low-slope design.

Full flat roof system design and installation for new commercial construction and retrofits. We work with the three primary flat-roof membrane types and help you choose based on your building's usage, foot traffic, energy goals, and budget.

  • TPO (thermoplastic polyolefin) — heat-welded seams, reflective surface
  • EPDM (rubber) — durable, flexible, proven in cold climates
  • Modified bitumen — multi-ply, tough, for high-traffic roofs
  • Tapered insulation for positive drainage
  • Vapor barrier and air barrier integration
  • Roof asset documentation package

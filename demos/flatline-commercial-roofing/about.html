About

Why a flat-roof-only company

Most roofing companies on Long Island do a little of everything — pitched roofs, shingles, gutters, siding, and maybe some flat roofs when they come up. That's fine for homeowners, but flat roofs are a different trade. The materials are different, the failure modes are different, and the skills to install and repair them are different.

Flatline Flat Roof Co. was built around a simple idea: do one thing and know it cold. Every person on the crew installs and repairs flat roofs every working day. That means faster diagnoses, cleaner installations, and repairs that hold — because we're not figuring it out as we go.

How the business works

Every job starts with an inspection. Not a "free estimate" where someone walks the roof for ten minutes and hands you a number — a real inspection with a written condition report. You get photos, identified issues, and a plain-English explanation of what's happening and what your options are.

If a repair will solve the problem, we say so. If the membrane is at end of life and replacement makes more sense, we say that too. The recommendation comes before the quote, not after.

From there, the scope is defined, the schedule is set, and the crew shows up on the agreed date. You get a project summary when the work is done — what was found, what was done, and what to watch for going forward.

Service area

We work on commercial and industrial flat roofs across Long Island, NY — from Nassau through Suffolk. Flat roof buildings are common in warehouse districts, retail corridors, medical office parks, and older commercial buildings throughout the region.

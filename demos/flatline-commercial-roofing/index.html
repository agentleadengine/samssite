We read the roof before we touch it.

A flat roof fails at its seams, its drains, and its flashings — not in the middle of a sheet. Every inspection starts with a full surface walk, drainage mapping, and seam check. You get a written condition report with photos and a clear recommendation, not a guess and a quote.

If the membrane can be repaired, we say so. If it's at end of life, we tell you that too — with the reasoning, not a sales pitch.

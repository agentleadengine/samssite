Work

Sample projects showing how we approach flat roof replacement, repair, and new installation. Each one is a composite of the type of work we do — labeled as samples, not real client accounts.

Sample project

Warehouse Roof Replacement

Phased tear-off and TPO installation across a 12,000 sq ft warehouse roof — completed in sections to keep the building operational. New tapered insulation corrected a long-standing ponding issue at the northeast corner.

Sample project

Retail Building Seam Repair

A failed seam weld along the main membrane lap was allowing water intrusion into a retail space. Traced the entry point 40 feet from the interior stain, re-welded the seam, and installed a reinforced patch over the affected zone.

Sample project

Post-Storm Roof Survey

Following a wind and hail event, a full condition survey of an office building's flat roof identified four impact points and one lifted flashing. Written report with photos allowed the building owner to plan repairs before the next storm season.

Sample project

Scupper and Gutter Integration

A medical office building had recurring overflow at its edge scuppers. Resized the scupper openings, installed new edge gutters with proper membrane-to-gutter flashing, and routed downspouts clear of the building foundation.

Sample project

New Flat Roof System

Full assembly installation on a new commercial build: metal deck, vapor barrier, tapered polyiso insulation for positive drainage, cover board, and 60-mil TPO membrane. The tapered insulation design eliminated the ponding water issues common to dead-flat roofs.

Sample project

Drainage Correction

A flat roof with chronic ponding water was re-sloped using tapered insulation during membrane replacement. The new design directs water to two existing drains instead of pooling in low spots, extending the service life of the new membrane.

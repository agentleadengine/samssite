A few questions people ask first

Is a broken spring dangerous to deal with myself?

Torsion springs are under real tension, and the wrong move with one can hurt you. Leave the door closed, don't force it by hand or with the opener, and call. It's a same-visit fix either way.

Do you carry parts on the truck, or do I need a second appointment?

Most jobs, yes. The van's stocked for the repairs we see most, springs, cables, rollers, common opener parts, so the fix usually happens in one visit.

How fast can you actually get here?

Depends on when you call and where you are on Long Island. Call and we'll give you a real window, not “sometime today,” and you'll know before you hang up whether that's this afternoon or first thing tomorrow.

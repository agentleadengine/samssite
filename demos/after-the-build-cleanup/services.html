Services

Five cleaning services for Long Island spaces — from raw post-construction sites to ongoing home maintenance.

Post-Construction Cleanup

The full site reset. We arrive after the contractors leave and remove every trace of construction — dust, debris, adhesive residue, and powder from every surface. This is our core service.

  • HEPA vacuum of all floors and horizontal surfaces
  • Drywall dust removal from walls, trim, and baseboards
  • Adhesive and sticker residue from glass and fixtures
  • Vent and register cleaning
  • Interior window and glass cleaning
  • Inside cabinet and drawer wipe-down
  • Fixture and hardware detail cleaning
  • Debris bagging and removal

When you need it: After new construction, renovations, additions, or any project that leaves dust and debris behind. Schedule us before move-in or before the final walkthrough with your builder.

Deep Cleaning

A thorough top-to-bottom clean for spaces that need more than routine maintenance. Every surface, every corner, every fixture — detailed by hand.

  • Full wipe-down of all hard surfaces
  • Baseboards, door frames, and switch plates
  • Bathroom scrub including grout and tile
  • Kitchen degrease and appliance exteriors
  • Interior glass and window sills
  • Floor vacuum and mop
  • Ceiling fan and light fixture dusting
  • Blind and vent cleaning

When you need it: Seasonal resets, neglected spaces, or before hosting. Not a replacement for routine cleaning — this is the comprehensive version.

Move-In & Move-Out Cleans

Empty-space cleaning for transitions. Whether you are handing over keys or picking up new ones, the space should be clean from top to bottom.

  • Full floor vacuum and mop (hardwood, tile, vinyl)
  • Inside cabinet and closet wipe-down
  • Appliance interior cleaning (if empty)
  • Bathroom deep clean
  • Baseboards and window sills
  • Light switch and outlet plate cleaning
  • Interior window cleaning
  • Vent and register dusting

When you need it: Before moving into a new home, after moving out of a rental, or before listing a property. The best time is when the space is empty.

Office Cleaning

Commercial office spaces, from single-suite to multi-room. We work around your hours so your team arrives to a clean environment.

  • Workstation surface wipe-down
  • Restroom cleaning and restocking
  • Breakroom and kitchen area
  • Floor vacuum and mop
  • Trash removal
  • High-touch surface disinfecting
  • Glass entry door cleaning
  • Flexible scheduling (after hours or weekends)

When you need it: One-time or recurring. Offices under 5,000 square feet are our sweet spot.

Recurring Home Cleaning

Weekly, biweekly, or monthly maintenance for homes that have already been through an initial clean. We keep things at the standard we set.

  • All floor vacuum and mop
  • Kitchen and bathroom surfaces
  • Dusting of horizontal surfaces
  • Trash removal
  • Bed making and linen changes (on request)
  • Mirror and glass spot cleaning
  • Baseboard wipe (rotating)
  • Same crew, consistent schedule

When you need it: After a deep clean or post-construction cleanup, to maintain the standard. We set up the schedule that fits your household.

Not sure which service you need?

Call us. We will ask about the space and point you to the right one.

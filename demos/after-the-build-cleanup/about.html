About

A cleaning company built around one specific moment: the gap between construction done and move-in ready.

How the company started

The idea came from watching builders finish good work and then hand over a dusty mess. The construction was solid, the finishes were clean, but nobody had handled the transition. Homeowners walked into drywall powder. Tenants arrived to sticker residue on appliances. The gap between "construction done" and "ready to live in" was real, and nobody was filling it well.

After The Build Cleanup Co. was created to fill that gap. The focus is narrow on purpose — post-construction cleaning is a different skill from general housekeeping. It requires HEPA filtration, specific protocols for construction dust, and an understanding of what builders, homeowners, and property managers each need from the handoff.

Over time, the service expanded to include deep cleaning, move-in and move-out cleans, office cleaning, and recurring home maintenance — because once a space is cleaned to that standard, the question becomes how to keep it there. But the core remains: we are the crew that turns a construction site into a place people can use.

How it works

1. Walk the site

We come out, see what was built, what dust and debris are present, and what the timeline is. Then we tell you what the clean involves.

2. Schedule the crew

We coordinate with your builder or your schedule. The clean happens after construction wraps and before move-in or walkthrough.

3. Clean to the checklist

HEPA vacuum, surface wipe-down, detail work, floor prep. Each surface type has its own protocol. We do not leave until the list is done.

4. Final walk-through

We walk the space with you or your builder. If anything needs attention, we handle it before we pack up.

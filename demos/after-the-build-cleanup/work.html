Work

Sample projects showing the range of post-construction and transition cleans we handle. Every project is authored in our visual grammar — these are illustrations, not photographs.

Sample project

New Construction Final Clean

A 2,400 sq ft single-family home in Suffolk County. Drywall dust on every surface, sawdust in corners, adhesive on glass. Full HEPA vacuum, detail wipe-down, and floor prep before the family moved in.

Sample project

Kitchen Renovation Reset

Post-remodel kitchen in a Nassau County home. Fine sanding dust inside every new cabinet, grout haze on tile, and sawdust behind appliances. Interior cabinet clean, grout detail, and floor vacuum.

Sample project

Office Suite Turnover

A 3,000 sq ft office space after tenant improvements. Construction dust on workstations, new flooring debris, and vent powder. Full surface wipe, floor vacuum, and restroom detail.

Sample project

Bathroom Remodel Detail

Newly tiled bathroom with grout haze on surfaces and tile, sanding dust in the vanity, and drywall powder in the vent. Grout film removal, fixture polish, and vent cleaning.

Sample project

Whole-House Renovation

A 3,800 sq ft home after a full gut renovation. Multiple trades, multiple dust types, every surface affected. Two-day clean with a full crew, HEPA filtration throughout, and final walk-through with the homeowner.

Sample project

Move-Out Deep Clean

An empty apartment after the last tenant moved out. Floors scuffed, cabinets dusty, bathrooms in need of a full scrub. Complete empty-space clean before the new lease started.

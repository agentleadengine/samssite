Questions we hear

How soon after construction should we schedule the clean?

Ideally, we come in the day after the last trade wraps up. That gives dust time to settle so we can remove it in one pass rather than chasing it. If you are on a tight timeline, call us and we will figure out the schedule together.

Do you bring your own equipment and supplies?

Yes. We bring HEPA-filtered vacuums, surface-specific cleaners, microfiber cloths, and everything else needed. You do not need to provide anything unless there is a product you specifically want used or avoided.

Can you clean while the builder is still finishing punch-list work?

We can, but it usually means a second pass. If possible, wait until punch-list items are done. If the timeline does not allow that, we will do the best we can and flag anything that gets re-dusted after we leave.

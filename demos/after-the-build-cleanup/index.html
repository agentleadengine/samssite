What we walk into

After the contractors leave, the dust settles — literally. Every horizontal surface has a film. Vents are clogged with drywall powder. Windows have adhesive residue. Here is what the transition looks like.

Before

Sheetrock dust on baseboards, sawdust in corners, sticker residue on glass, vent covers thick with powder, nails and debris on the floor.

After

Surfaces wiped down and detail-cleaned. Floors swept and vacuumed. Vents cleaned. Glass cleared. The space is ready for furniture, for people, for living.

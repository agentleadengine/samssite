Six things we do on a gutter visit, and when each one is yours

Most homes only need the first two. Everything else gets pointed out and quoted on the spot, and only done if you say go ahead.

Full hand cleaning

$149–$219starting at

Leaves, shingle grit, seed pods, and the packed sludge at the bottom come out by hand into a bag. Nothing gets blown across the yard or left buried in the beds below.

When: at least twice a year, or as soon as you can see leaf litter from the ground. Single-story starts at $149, two-story at $219.

Downspout flush & unclog

Includedevery visit

We run water into the top of every downspout and watch it come out clean at the bottom. If it backs up, we snake the elbow and run it again until the line is actually open.

Flushing happens on every visit, no extra charge. If a spout is genuinely blocked and needs snaking, that's a $45-per-spout add-on, starting at.

Gutter guard service

$79add-on, starting at

Screens and covers still fill in with pine needles and shingle grit on top. We lift the panels where they come off, clean underneath, and set them back down straight.

When: if the guards have never been lifted, or water still comes over the front despite having them installed.

Hanger & seam tune-up

Sagging sections get re-secured, pulled spikes go back in with screws, and small end-cap or seam leaks get sealed while the gutter is clean and dry.

When: if a section looks crooked or pulled away from the wall. We point it out during the visit and quote it separately before touching anything.

Roof & valley clearing

What collects in the roof valley, where two planes of the roof meet, is what fills the gutter back up after the next storm. We clear what we can safely reach from the ladder line.

When: homes with more than one roof plane, especially heading into fall and winter.

Spring & fall plan

One visit in spring and one in fall, on the calendar in advance, at the same per-visit pricing above. No separate plan fee. You get a heads-up text a few days out and a photo set after each one.

When: so you don't have to remember to call. Especially worth it if you have large trees near the roof.

Already know which one you need?

Tell us how many stories the house has and what you're seeing when it rains. We'll price it on the same call.

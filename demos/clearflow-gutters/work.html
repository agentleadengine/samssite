Six visits, six different ways a gutter gets clogged

These are illustrated scenes built to show the kind of work we do, not photos of real houses. Each sample project lines up with a service on the page before this one.

Single-story colonial, fall leaf pack

Sample project

The back run was packed with dry leaf litter and shingle grit. It came out by hand into bags, and both downspouts were water-tested before the ladder came down.

Downspout unclog after a storm

Sample project

Water was sheeting over the front after every downpour. The elbow got snaked clear and flushed until it ran without backing up.

Two-story home with gutter guards

Sample project

Guards had been on since the house was built and never once lifted. Years of packed pine needles were sitting underneath.

Sagging section, refastened

Sample project

One run had pulled away from the fascia under the weight of standing water. It went back up level, with screws in place of the spikes that had worked loose.

Valley clearing on a multi-gable roof

Sample project

The valley was holding a season's worth of grit that kept refilling the gutter below. Cleared what could be reached safely from the ladder line.

Spring maple-seed mat removal

Sample project

Maple seed and pollen had formed a dense mat at the bottom of the run. Caught in late spring, it was the quickest clean of the year.

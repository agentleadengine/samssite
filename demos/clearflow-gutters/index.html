Where the water goes when nobody reaches in

A packed gutter doesn't just overflow. It finds the fascia board, the siding behind it, and the soil at the foundation corner. This is the same run, before a hand-clean and after.

  • Water sheeting over the frontmeans something is blocking the run. That's the first thing we check from the ground.
  • Dark streaks under the eaveusually mean water is finding its way behind the gutter, not through it.
  • We run water through every downspout before the ladder comes down, to confirm it's actually clear.

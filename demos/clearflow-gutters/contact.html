Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be home for the visit?

No. If you are, we'll walk the roofline with you before we start. If not, we'll still text photos afterward and leave a note about anything we found.

What happens if it's raining or windy on my scheduled day?

We don't run ladders in high wind, lightning, or ice. If the weather turns, we call that morning and move the visit to the next dry day, so you're not left waiting on a crew that never shows.

How do I know it's actually time to clean the gutters?

Three signs to watch for: water sheeting over the front instead of heading for the downspout, dark streaks under the eave, or anything growing up there. See any of those and it's time. More detail on our services page.

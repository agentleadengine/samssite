Every job starts
the same way.

We look at it, we price it in writing, you decide. Six things we work on, what's actually included, and when you'd call us for each.

Diagnostics

A check engine code points at a system, not a part. We pull the codes, then test the circuit and the component instead of guessing. Diagnostic time is quoted up front and comes off the bill if you have us do the repair.

When you need it
Check engine light, a new noise or smell, or a feeling that something changed while driving.

Brakes

Pads, rotors, calipers, hoses and fluid flushes. We measure rotor thickness and pad life and show you the numbers, so you know whether it's now or two months from now.

When you need it
Grinding or squealing, a soft pedal, pulling to one side, or it's just been a while since anyone measured.

Oil & Maintenance

Conventional or full synthetic, filters, belts, fluids and a look-over of the whole car while it's up. We follow your factory interval, not a sticker on the windshield.

When you need it
Due by mileage, the oil-life light is on, or you picked up a car with no service history.

Suspension & Steering

Struts, shocks, control arms, tie rods, sway bar links and wheel bearings. If it clunks over expressway potholes or wanders on the parkway, we'll find which joint is doing it.

When you need it
Clunks over potholes, wandering at highway speed, or tire wear that's uneven across the tread.

A/C & Heat

Leak test first, recharge second. Blower motors, blend doors, cabin filters and compressors. Topping off a system that leaks just buys you one more hot week.

When you need it
Air that's warm when it should be cold, a musty smell, or a system that worked last month and doesn't now.

Tires & Alignment

Mount, balance, rotate and four-wheel alignment. Uneven wear is usually a symptom, so we check the suspension before we sell you rubber you'll chew through again.

When you need it
Uneven wear, a pull at speed, new tires going on, or it's been a while since the last alignment.

Not sure which one it is?

Describe what the car's doing and we'll point you at the right one, or just get it looked at.

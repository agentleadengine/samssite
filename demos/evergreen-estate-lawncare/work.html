Work

Sample projects showing how we approach each service. Every scene below is an authored illustration, not a photograph of a real property.

Sample project

Weekly Mowing with Striping

A half-acre property maintained on a weekly schedule through the full growing season. The stripes come from a roller-backed mower bending the grass in alternating directions — a visual standard that only holds if the height stays consistent week to week.

Sample project

Foundation Bed Redesign

A full-width front foundation bed redesigned in three layers: back-row evergreens for winter structure, mid-height perennials for seasonal color, and a low ground cover along the stone edge. The bed reads as a designed composition from the curb, not a row of plants.

Sample project

Bluestone Patio with Lawn Border

A 400-square-foot bluestone patio laid on a compacted base with polymeric sand joints. The lawn borders on two sides with a bed edge that keeps the stone separate from the grass. Built to drain away from the house foundation, with a subtle pitch that is invisible to the eye but functional in every storm.

Sample project

Fall Cleanup: Before and After

A property that had accumulated a full season of leaf litter across the beds and lawn. The cleanup clears every leaf, cuts back the perennials to ground level, and refreshes the bed edge so the property enters winter clean. The illustration shows the half-cleared line between the leaf-covered side and the finished side.

Sample project

Four-Zone Irrigation Install

A full-property irrigation system with four zones: front lawn, back lawn, shrub beds on drip, and a separate zone for the sunny south-facing bed that needs more frequent watering. The smart controller adjusts the schedule based on rainfall, so the system does not run during a Long Island thunderstorm and then water again the next morning.

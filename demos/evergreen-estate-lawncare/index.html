The difference is in the edges.

Anyone can run a mower across open grass. The standard that makes a property look estate-kept is in the details you notice walking up the path: a crisp edge where the lawn meets the bed, hedging that reads as a wall, a patio surface that drains properly after a Long Island August storm. That is the work we do, week after week, for every property on our route.

Why properties stay with us

We are a small, weekly-route service. That means the same crew, the same standard, and the same eye on your property every visit.

  • Same crew, every week

    The crew that learns your property is the crew that keeps it. No rotating subcontractors.

  • Weekly subscription standard

    Care is scheduled, not reactive. Your lawn is maintained before it needs maintaining.

  • Design and build in-house

    From the garden bed to the stone patio, the same team designs and installs — no handoff between companies.

  • Long Island plant knowledge

    We plant what thrives in Suffolk County clay and salt air, not what looks good in a catalog.

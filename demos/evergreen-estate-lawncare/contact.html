Questions

Do you offer one-time service, or only weekly care?

Both. Weekly care is the core of what we do, but we also handle one-time projects like seasonal cleanups, patio installs, and irrigation work. Call us and we will figure out what fits.

How do you price weekly lawn care?

Pricing depends on property size, terrain, and what is included beyond mowing. We walk the property first and give you a flat weekly rate before you commit. No surprise charges.

What happens if it rains on my scheduled day?

We shift the route. If your day is rained out, we come the next dry day and keep the schedule on track. The standard does not drop because the weather moved things around.

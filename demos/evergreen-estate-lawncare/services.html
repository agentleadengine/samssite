Services

Five services, each held to estate standard. Whether you need weekly maintenance or a full garden redesign, the same crew handles the work from first visit to final detail.

Weekly Lawn Care

Mowing at the right height for the season, edging along every bed and walkway, and blowing clippings off hard surfaces. Weekly visits keep the grass at a consistent height so it never looks overgrown or scalped.

What's included

  • Mowing at seasonally adjusted height (3.5" summer, 2.5" spring/fall)
  • Edging along garden beds, walkways, and driveways
  • Blowing of clippings from patios, walks, and driveways
  • Quick visual check of beds and borders for weeds or damage
When you need it: If your lawn grows fast enough to look uneven between mowings, weekly care is the standard that keeps it. Bi-weekly is available for smaller properties, but most Long Island lawns need weekly during the growing season.

Garden Design & Planting

Garden beds designed for four-season interest, installed with plants selected for Long Island's climate and soil. We design the bed, source the plants, and install them so the layout reads as deliberate from the first day.

What's included

  • On-site design consultation with a sketched bed layout
  • Plant selection for sun exposure, soil type, and deer pressure
  • Bed preparation: soil amendment, edging, and mulch
  • Planting and initial watering setup
  • Seasonal maintenance plan for the first year
When you need it: When a bed looks like a random collection of plants rather than a designed space, or when you are starting from bare lawn and want it to look intentional from the start.

Hardscape Patios & Walkways

Natural stone and concrete paver patios, walkways, and retaining walls. Every project starts with base preparation and drainage planning, because a patio that shifts or pools water was never built right to begin with.

What's included

  • Site assessment and drainage plan
  • Excavation, base compaction, and leveling
  • Stone or paver installation with polymeric sand joints
  • Edge restraint installation
  • Optional sealing for stain resistance
When you need it: When your patio is cracked, settling, or was never properly based, or when you are adding outdoor living space and want it built to last more than a few seasons.

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring wake-up and fall put-down services that clear every bed to bare soil, cut back perennials, remove leaf litter, and prepare the property for the season ahead. These are the two visits that set the tone for the entire growing season.

What's included

  • Leaf and debris removal from all beds and lawn areas
  • Perennial cutback (spring) or die-back cut (fall)
  • Bed edging refresh
  • Mulch application (optional, spring)
  • Gutter clearing of yard debris (not roof gutters)
When you need it: Spring cleanup runs March through May. Fall cleanup runs October through December. If your beds have accumulated a winter's worth of debris or fall leaves are sitting on the grass, it is time.

Irrigation Systems

Installation of underground irrigation zones with smart controllers, plus seasonal start-up, mid-season tuning, and winterization. Water should go to the root zone, not the sidewalk or the driveway.

What's included

  • Zone planning based on plant type and sun exposure
  • Installation of underground lines, heads, and drip emitters
  • Smart controller setup with seasonal scheduling
  • Spring start-up and pressure check
  • Fall winterization and blowout
When you need it: If you are watering by hand, seeing brown patches in summer, or paying for water that is hitting pavement instead of roots, an irrigation system with proper zoning solves all three.

Not sure which service you need?

Call us. We will walk the property, tell you what we see, and recommend only what makes sense for your lawn and budget.

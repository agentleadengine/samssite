About

The idea

Evergreen Estate started with a simple observation: most lawn services treat a property as a stop on a route. They show up, mow fast, blow the clippings, and leave. The lawn is cut, but it never looks kept — the edges are rough, the beds are an afterthought, and the hardscape is someone else's problem.

The idea behind this company is that a property looks its best when one crew holds the whole standard. Mowing, edging, bed care, hardscape, irrigation — the same eyes on the same property every week. Not because it is efficient for us, but because it is what makes the difference visible.

How it works

Weekly care is the core. We keep our route small enough that every property gets the same attention on the same day. If you call for a one-time service — a cleanup, a patio, an irrigation install — we handle that too, but the weekly properties are where the standard is maintained and where the work shows.

Before any new property joins the route, we walk it together. We look at the lawn, the beds, the drainage, and the hardscape. We tell you what we see and what we would recommend. If weekly care is the right fit, we set the schedule. If it is not — if the property needs work we do not do, or the timing does not make sense — we say so.

Service area

Where we work

We serve Long Island, New York — Suffolk County and eastern Nassau County. Weekly routes are concentrated so the crew is never driving more than working. If you are outside our current route area, call us and we will tell you honestly whether we can get to you.

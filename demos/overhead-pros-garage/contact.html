Quick answers

Is off-track service really same-day?

We work it into the schedule the same day when we can, especially if the door's stuck open or won't close all the way. It's not a 24-hour dispatch line, so if it's after hours, leave a message and we'll call first thing.

How do you decide what spring my door needs?

We weigh what the door actually is, count the cycles it's rated for, and match wire size and wind to that. A door that's had glass or insulation added since it was built usually needs a heavier spring than the one it came with.

Will you install an opener on a door someone else put in?

Yes, as long as the door and track are in good enough shape to carry it. We'll check the door's condition first and say so plainly if it needs work before an opener goes on.

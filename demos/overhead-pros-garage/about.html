The spring gets sized before the door gets ordered.

Most garage door calls get the same treatment: a tech shows up, grabs whatever spring's on the truck, and moves to the next job. That's fine if all that's wrong is a remote with a dead battery. It's not fine for a door that's actually going to get opened and closed a few times a day for years, where the spring, the track, and the opener all have to be sized to what the door really weighs. Overhead Pros exists for that second kind of job.

The work starts with a tape measure and a real look at the opening, not a phone quote. We check headroom, backroom, and sideroom, weigh what the door actually is, and spec the hardware to match, not the other way around. Springs get wound to the door's cycle rating. Track gets set true and checked with a level, not just eyeballed. Before anything's called finished, the door comes off the opener and gets tested by hand.

If all you need is a remote reprogrammed or a bulb changed, that's a five-minute fix and we'll say so on the phone. For everything that actually depends on the hardware being right, a spring, a track, a new install, an opener that's fighting the door instead of matching it, that's the work Overhead Pros is built around.

Dave Kessler, Overhead Pros Garage Doors

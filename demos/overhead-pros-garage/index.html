How an install actually goes

The parts that matter aren't the drill work. They're these three.

  1. 1

    Measured against the actual opening

    We check headroom, backroom, and sideroom before anything's quoted. A stock size doesn't always fit.

  2. 2

    Hardware matched to the door's real weight

    Spring wire, drum size, and track gauge get picked for what the door actually weighs, not a generic chart.

  3. 3

    Balance checked by hand

    Before we call a job finished, the door gets disconnected from the opener and tested for balance manually.

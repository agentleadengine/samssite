Five jobs, specced before they're worked

Tell us the door and the problem. If it's outside what we install well, we'll say so instead of taking the job anyway.

New door installation

What's included
  • Opening measured for headroom, backroom, and sideroom before anything's ordered
  • Track, springs, and hardware sized to the door's actual weight and cycle count
  • Old door and track removed and hauled off, not left in the driveway
When you need it

A door that's dented, drafty, or old enough that parts aren't holding up anymore, or a new opening being framed.

Spring and cable repair

What's included
  • Torsion or extension springs replaced in matched pairs, wound to the door's actual weight
  • Cables and drums checked together, since a worn cable usually means a worn drum
  • Safety cables added where the original install skipped them
When you need it

A door that won't budge, a loud bang from overhead, or a cable that's frayed or already snapped.

Opener install and repair

What's included
  • Belt, chain, or screw-drive units matched to your door's weight and your garage's headroom
  • Force and travel limits set by hand, not left on factory defaults
  • Old openers repaired first when the motor and rail are still sound
When you need it

An opener that's slow, noisy, reversing on its own, or not responding to the remote at all.

Annual tune-up

What's included
  • Every roller, hinge, and bolt checked and torqued, not just eyeballed
  • Track alignment and door balance tested by hand
  • Weatherstripping and lubrication done as part of the visit, not an upsell
When you need it

Once a year, or when the door's gotten louder or slower than you remember.

Emergency off-track service

What's included
  • The door checked for what actually threw it: a bent track, a snapped cable, or a broken spring
  • Panel reset on the track and rehung if that's the honest fix
  • A straight answer if the door or track is bent enough that replacement is the better call
When you need it

A door that's stuck partway, hanging crooked, or come off the track outright.

Job not on the list?

Describe it on the phone. If it's not something we do well, we'll point you to what is.

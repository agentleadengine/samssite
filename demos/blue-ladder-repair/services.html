The jobs most contractors won’t drive out for

If it takes under a day and doesn’t need a permit, it’s probably on this list. If it needs a licensed trade, we’ll say so on the call and point you to one instead of taking the job anyway.

Drywall & plaster patches

Doorknob holes, water stains after a roof fix, popped nails and hairline cracks. Patched, sanded, primed and feathered so the wall doesn’t show a square where we worked — you shouldn’t be able to find the repair once the paint’s dry.

Good to know

Paint match on request — bring the can or the color name.

When you need it

You’re selling, showing a rental, or just don’t want to look at the hole anymore.

Doors, locks & hardware

Doors that stick in summer, latches that don’t catch, sagging hinges, new deadbolts and handle sets. Interior doors planed and rehung, storm doors adjusted so they close without the shoulder-check.

Good to know

Most doors done in one visit.

When you need it

A door that won’t latch, or a lock you want changed after moving in.

Faucets, toilets & small plumbing

Faucet and shower-head swaps, running toilets, wax rings, shut-off valves and garbage disposals. Anything behind the wall — a real leak, a re-pipe — we hand off to a licensed plumber and tell you that up front.

Good to know

Bring us the fixture, or we’ll pick it up.

When you need it

The toilet that won’t stop running, or a faucet you bought and haven’t had time to install.

Mounting & shelving

TVs on the right anchors, floating shelves, closet systems, curtain rods, mirrors and heavy art. We find the studs and tell you honestly what a wall can and can’t hold before we hang anything on it.

Good to know

Cable tucked and hidden.

When you need it

The TV that’s been leaning against the wall since move-in.

Gutters, caulk & weather-proofing

Gutter clean-outs, downspout re-aims, window and tub re-caulking, door sweeps and weather stripping before the cold sets in. Cheap fixes now instead of a bigger repair once the weather turns.

Good to know

Seasonal — book early in fall.

When you need it

Leaves are already piling up, or you feel a draft standing near the door.

Move-in & move-out lists

Landlord punch lists, security-deposit repairs, furniture assembly and childproofing. Hand us the list and we’ll work down it in one visit instead of chasing five different people.

Good to know

Photos sent when the list is done.

When you need it

A lease deadline, or a move with forty small tasks and zero free weekends.

Not on the list above?

Call anyway. If it’s a small job we probably still do it — and if it’s not, we’ll tell you who to call instead.

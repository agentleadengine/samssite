A few questions people ask first

Do I need to be home for the whole visit?

No. If you can’t be there, leave a key in a lockbox or with a neighbor and mention it on the call. I’ll text photos when your list is done.

What if something on my list needs a licensed plumber or electrician?

I’ll tell you on the call, before anything gets booked. Anything behind the wall — a real leak, new wiring — gets handed to a licensed trade instead of me taking a swing at it.

How does billing work?

Hourly work is billed in half-hour blocks after the first hour, and materials are at cost. Jobs past a 40-minute drive get a trip charge, quoted on the phone before you commit.

About Storm Guard

A roofing company built around one idea: the storms here are real, and the roof needs to be ready for them.

Why This Business Exists

Storm Guard Roofers started because Long Island weather doesn't give warnings — it gives events. Nor'easters roll in off the Atlantic. Hurricanes track up the coast. The winter brings ice dams that force water under shingles. And in between, there's the steady wear of sun, wind, and rain on materials that were never meant to last forever.

The idea was simple: build a roofing company that thinks about storms first. Not as a marketing angle, but as the actual design principle. Every roof we touch gets evaluated against the question, "What happens when the next one hits?" That means we don't just patch the obvious damage — we look for the weak spots the storm will find next time.

We work on Long Island, for Long Island homeowners. The houses here have specific needs — from wind exposure on the coast to snow load inland — and we've spent enough time on these roofs to know what holds and what doesn't.

How We Work

Every job starts the same way: we get on the roof and look. We document what we find. We talk you through what's actually wrong, what needs immediate attention, and what can wait. Then we give you a written scope before any work begins — no surprises, no scope creep.

If it's an emergency, we move fast. Tarps go up, water stops, and we figure out the permanent fix from there. If it's a planned replacement, we take the time to do it right — full tear-off, deck inspection, proper underlayment, and materials installed to manufacturer specifications.

Roofing Questions

How fast can you respond to a storm emergency?

During and after major storms, we prioritize emergency calls. The first goal is always to stop active water intrusion with tarps or temporary patches, then schedule the permanent repair. Call us and we'll give you a realistic timeline based on current call volume.

Should I get a roof inspection even if I don't see any leaks?

Yes, especially after a significant storm or if your roof is over 15 years old. Not all damage is visible from the ground — lifted shingles, cracked flashing, and minor punctures can let water in slowly without showing up on your ceiling until the damage is extensive.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

We'll tell you honestly. If the damage is localized and the rest of the roof is sound, a repair makes sense. If the shingles are failing across the whole surface, the deck is soft, or there's been long-term water intrusion, replacement is usually more cost-effective than repeated repairs. We document our findings either way so you can make an informed decision.

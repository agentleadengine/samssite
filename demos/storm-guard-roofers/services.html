Roofing Services

Five services covering everything from emergency leak repair to full roof replacement — built for Long Island weather.

Full Roof Replacement

When repairs aren't enough, a full replacement gives you a new roofing system designed to handle Long Island's worst. We tear off the old roof, inspect the deck underneath, and install a complete system from underlayment to shingles.

  • Complete tear-off and disposal of existing roofing
  • Deck inspection and rot repair as needed
  • Ice and water shield at vulnerable edges
  • New underlayment and flashing installation
  • Shingle or membrane roofing installed per manufacturer spec

When You Need It

Shingles are curling or missing across large sections, the roof is over 20 years old, or repeated repairs aren't holding. If the deck is soft or water has been getting in for a while, replacement is usually the right call.

Leak Repair

Water on the ceiling means something failed above. We trace the leak back to its actual entry point — which is rarely where the stain appears — and repair it at the source.

  • Interior and exterior leak source tracing
  • Flashing repair and replacement around penetrations
  • Shingle replacement in damaged areas
  • Valley and seam sealing
  • Water-path verification after repair

When You Need It

Water stains on ceilings or walls, dripping during rain, or damp spots in the attic. Even a small leak can damage insulation and drywall over time, so it's worth fixing before the next storm makes it worse.

Storm Damage Inspection

After a storm, you need to know what happened up there before you make any decisions. We get on the roof, document the damage, and give you a written scope of what needs attention.

  • Full roof surface inspection
  • Shingle, flashing, and vent assessment
  • Gutter and drainage check
  • Interior attic check for water intrusion
  • Written findings and recommended next steps

When You Need It

After any significant storm — high winds, hail, heavy rain, or a named system passing through. Also smart before listing a home for sale, or if you've recently bought a home and want a baseline assessment.

Gutter Integration

A good roof sheds water. A good gutter system moves it away from your foundation. We install and repair gutters as part of a complete roofing system, not as an afterthought.

  • Seamless gutter installation
  • Downspout routing and extension
  • Gutter guard installation
  • Fascia and soffit repair
  • Integration with new or existing roof systems

When You Need It

Gutters sagging, pulling away, or overflowing during rain. Water pooling near the foundation. Or as part of a full roof replacement to make sure the entire water-management system works together.

Flat Roof Systems

Flat and low-slope roofs need different materials and different thinking. We install membrane systems designed for the standing water and UV exposure that flat roofs face on Long Island.

  • TPO and EPDM membrane installation
  • Modified bitumen systems
  • Drain and scupper integration
  • Flat roof leak detection and repair
  • Coating and restoration for aging membranes

When You Need It

Home additions, porches, garages, or commercial-style flat sections. If your flat roof is pooling water, bubbling, or approaching the end of its membrane life, it's time to evaluate replacement options.

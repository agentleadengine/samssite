Sample Projects

A look at the types of roofing work we do across Long Island. Every project is different, but the approach is the same: inspect, document, fix it right.

Sample Project

Emergency Tarp and Leak Repair

A homeowner called after a weekend nor'easter lifted shingles and water started dripping through the dining room ceiling. We tarped the damaged section that afternoon, traced the leak to a compromised valley, and completed the permanent repair the following week.

Sample Project

Full Roof Replacement

A 25-year-old asphalt shingle roof with widespread curling and granule loss. Full tear-off revealed soft decking near the chimney, which was replaced before installing new underlayment, ice and water shield, and architectural shingles.

Sample Project

Post-Storm Damage Inspection

After a hail event moved through the area, a homeowner wanted a full assessment before deciding on repairs. We documented impact spots across the roof surface, identified two compromised flashing areas, and provided a written scope for the insurance process.

Sample Project

Flat Roof Membrane Replacement

An aging tar-and-gravel flat roof on a home addition was pooling water and developing blisters. We removed the old system, repaired the substrate, and installed a new TPO membrane with properly sloped drainage and new scuppers.

Sample Project

Gutter Integration with Roof Replacement

As part of a full roof replacement, the old gutters were undersized and pulling away from the fascia. We installed new seamless gutters with proper downspout routing and gutter guards, integrated with the new drip edge and flashing.

Sample Project

Chimney Flashing Repair

A persistent leak turned out to be failing step flashing around a chimney, not the shingles themselves. We removed the old flashing, installed new step and counter flashing with proper sealant, and verified the repair with a water test.

The five jobs we run most

What's actually included, and when it's worth calling tonight instead of waiting. No invented prices here — call and we'll quote it off what you describe.

Drain Cleaning

A sink that won't drain, a tub that backs up, a main line that's stopped taking anything — we run the cable until we feel it break through, then run water behind it to prove the line's actually open, not just quiet for a day.

What's included
  • Kitchen, bath, and laundry line clearing
  • Main line and branch-line clogs
  • Camera check on lines that keep coming back
  • Cable run start to finish, same visit
When it's tonight, not next week

Water backing up instead of draining, gurgling from a drain you're not even running, or more than one fixture backing up at once. That last one usually means the main line — call.

Water Heater Repair & Install

No hot water, a pilot that won't stay lit, a tank rusting through the bottom — we diagnose it on the truck and fix what can be fixed. When a tank's actually done, we size and install the replacement the same visit, when the unit's already on the truck.

What's included
  • Gas and electric tank units
  • Pilot, thermocouple, and element repair
  • Full tank replacement and haul-away
  • Tankless diagnostics and repair
When it's tonight, not next week

Water that never gets hot, a tank leaking from the bottom seam, or one that's popping and rumbling when it heats. None of those get better on their own.

Leak Detection & Repair

A damp spot on the ceiling, a water bill that jumped for no reason, a drip you can hear behind a wall but can't find — we trace it to the actual source before we open anything up, so we're not guessing with a wall.

What's included
  • Slab and under-floor leak tracing
  • Wall and ceiling leak tracing
  • Pipe and fitting repair or replacement
  • Water bill spike investigation
When it's tonight, not next week

A meter that's still spinning with everything off, a warm spot on the floor, a mildew smell with no visible source. The sooner it's found, the less it costs to open up.

Fixture Install

New faucet, new toilet, a disposal that finally gave out, a shower valve you're upgrading — we pull the old one, set the new one square and sealed, and run water through it before we call it done.

What's included
  • Faucets, toilets, and sinks
  • Garbage disposals
  • Shower and tub valves
  • Fixture swaps during a remodel
When it's worth calling

Whenever you're ready. This is the one job on this page you can actually schedule, instead of calling us over it at midnight.

Sewer Line Service

Roots in the main, a line that's settled and bellied, a section that's collapsed under the yard — we camera the line first so you're looking at the actual problem, not a guess, before anything gets dug.

What's included
  • Camera inspection with a real look at the line
  • Root cutting and hydro jetting
  • Spot repair and full line replacement
  • Cleanout install where there isn't one
When it's tonight, not next week

Every drain in the house backing up at once, a sewage smell in the yard, a line that clears for a week and clogs again. That pattern means the main, not the sink.

Not sure which one this is?

Describe what's happening and we'll tell you straight which job it is and whether it can wait until morning.

Two brothers, one truck, no dispatcher between you and the guy who shows up

Rooter Brothers started because my brother and I got tired of the way emergency plumbing usually goes. You call, you get a machine or a maybe, and by the time somebody shows up the puddle's already found the baseboards. We run it the other way — someone answers, you get a real price before a truck leaves the lot, and the person who quoted it is the one standing in your kitchen fixing it.

Most of what we do isn't glamorous. Drains, water heaters, a leak somebody's been ignoring for a month. We keep the truck stocked for the calls we get most, so a repair doesn't turn into “we'll be back Thursday with the part.” If a job needs something we don't have on hand, you hear that on the spot — not after we've already opened up a wall.

We run calls across Long Island, day shift and the middle of the night both. A stopped drain doesn't wait for business hours, so we don't either. If you're not sure whether what's happening is an emergency, call anyway and describe it — we'll tell you straight whether it can wait till morning or whether we're already grabbing keys.

— Danny, Rooter Brothers

How a door actually gets designed and built

Three visits, and you’ll know the plan before anything is ordered.

First

We talk about the door

You describe the house, and we talk through panel layout, window style, stain or paint, and hardware finish. Nothing gets ordered on this call.

Then

We measure and build

We template the actual opening and build the face to match it, panel by panel, in the shop, not off a shelf.

Last

We install it right

The crew that built it hangs it, sets the spring and cable to the door’s real weight, and walks you through the hardware before we leave.

What doesn’t change from job to job

The face gets drawn before it gets built

You see the panel layout and hardware plan before anything is cut.

One crew, start to finish

The people who measure your opening are the people who build and hang the door.

Hardware is real, not applied

Straps, hinges and clavos are set into the panel and do the work. They are not a printed film.

We say the finish out loud before it’s ordered

Stain, paint or hardware color gets confirmed with you, not guessed at.

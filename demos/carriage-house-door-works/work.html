Five doors, drawn the way we’d sketch them for you

No stock photography and no real customers’ homes here. These are sample projects showing the kind of design range a conversation with us actually covers.

Sample project

The Fairview

Espresso stain, arched three-lite windows, iron hardware over full cross-buck bracing on both leaves.

  • Stain: Espresso
  • Windows: Arched, 3-lite
  • Hardware: Iron
Sample project

The Harborview

Painted cream panels, a flat six-lite window band, no cross-buck. Iron hardware keeps the carriage look without the wood tone.

  • Finish: Painted cream
  • Windows: Flat, 6-lite
  • Hardware: Iron
Sample project

The Oakmont

Mid-tone oak stain with cross-buck bracing running the full face and a brass medallion where the straps cross.

  • Stain: Oak
  • Windows: Flat, 4-lite
  • Hardware: Brass
Sample project

The Northgate

A dark, minimal face with a single narrow window band, flat panels, and brushed hardware instead of black iron.

  • Finish: Dark composite-look
  • Windows: Slit band
  • Hardware: Brushed
Sample project

The Preston

Two-tone: a painted cream window band over espresso-stained cross-buck panels, with a wider strap at the seam.

  • Finish: Two-tone
  • Windows: Arched, 3-lite
  • Hardware: Iron

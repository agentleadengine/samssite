A few questions people ask first

Do you build to my exact opening, or do doors come in standard sizes?

We template the actual opening and build the face to match it. Standard sizes are a starting point for the hardware, not the design.

Can I see finish and hardware options before I order?

Yes. We talk through stain or paint and hardware finish during the design conversation, before anything is built.

My door won’t open. Is that an emergency call?

Usually not dangerous, but stop forcing it, especially if a spring looks like it let go. Call and describe what you’re seeing. We’ll tell you what’s safe to do and how soon we can get to it.

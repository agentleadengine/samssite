One shop, doors drawn before they’re built.

I spent years building cabinetry and built-ins before I ever touched a garage door. What kept nagging at me was how the door never got designed on purpose. Everything else on the front of the house got picked out, and then the garage door just showed up as whatever size the truck had that week.

Carriage House Door Works started as an answer to that. I draw the face of the door first, panel proportions, window row, stain or paint, hardware finish, against a photo of the actual house. Then we build it to the opening, not the other way around.

One crew runs a job from the first phone call through the install. The door gets built once in the shop and set once on your house, not adjusted forever after.

Sample detail · the bench, mid panel

What a normal call sounds like

You tell us about the house, what’s on the door now, and what you’d want instead. We talk through panel style, window row, and finish right on the phone, and if it sounds like a good fit, we’ll set a time to measure the opening in person.

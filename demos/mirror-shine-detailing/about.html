About Mirror Shine

Plain version: we look before we polish, and we don't rush the order. Here's how that actually plays out.

Mirror Shine started the way most of these do — a driveway, a shop-vac, and a few cars for people who asked around. What kept it going wasn't the wash itself. It was cars that came back looking better than the owner expected, and told a neighbor.

The rule that stuck from those first driveway jobs is the one everything else got built around: look at the paint under real light before saying what it needs. A quote made in shade or under a garage tube light is a guess. A quote made under a hard work light is what the car is actually going to get.

Most jobs still happen at your driveway or a parking spot with room to work — we bring our own water and power. Anything bigger, coatings and multi-step correction, stays at the shop, where the car can sit overnight undisturbed while a coating cures.

How a detail actually goes

The order doesn't change, whatever the car needs.

We look at the paint first

Under light, before we quote. Some cars need a full correction, some just need decontamination — we tell you which one yours is.

Clean before correct

Foam, hand wash, clay, iron remover. Polishing dirty paint is how swirls get put in, so that step doesn't get skipped.

You see it in the light

We walk the finished car with you before you pay. If a panel needs another pass, it gets another pass.

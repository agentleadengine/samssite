Three ways in

The full menu of six packages, what's included, and what each one runs, lives on the services page.

Maintenance Wash

Two-bucket hand wash, wheels and wheel wells, bug and tar removal, tires dressed, glass cleaned in and out. About two hours.

See how it works

Paint Correction

Machine polishing to cut swirl marks, wash marks, and light scratches out of the clear coat. One-step or two-step, depending on what the paint needs after inspection.

See how it works

Ceramic Coating

Decontamination, correction, then a coating that makes washing easier and keeps the gloss deep. One-year and multi-year options. The car stays with us overnight to cure.

Why the order matters

Nothing dramatic. Just the sequence we won't skip, whatever the car needs.

Inspected under light first

Before we quote, not after. Some cars need a full correction, some just need decontamination — we tell you which.

Clean before correct

Foam, hand wash, clay, iron remover. Polishing dirty paint is how swirls get put in, so that step doesn't get skipped.

You watch it in the light

We walk the finished car with you before you pay. If a panel needs another pass, it gets another pass.

Mobile or in-shop

Driveway detail for most jobs, or drop it with us for coatings and multi-step correction.

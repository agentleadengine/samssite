A few things people ask

How long does a detail take?

A maintenance wash is about two hours. Full interior runs closer to half a day. Correction and coating take longer, and a coating stays overnight to cure untouched.

Mobile or in-shop — how do I know which I need?

Most jobs come to you: we need a driveway or parking spot with room to open the doors, and we bring our own water and power. Coatings and multi-step correction happen at the shop, where the car can sit undisturbed.

What should I do before you arrive?

Clear out anything you don't want handled, and let us know where to park or plug in if we're coming to you. Everything else is on us.

Still deciding what the car needs?

Call and describe it. We'll tell you straight, no guessing over the phone.

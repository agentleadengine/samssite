Detail packages

Six ways to get from dull to done right. Prices start honest — the paint tells us the rest once we've looked at it under light.

Maintenance Wash

What's included
Two-bucket hand wash, wheels and wheel wells scrubbed, bug and tar removal, spray sealant, tires dressed, glass cleaned inside and out. About two hours.
When you need it
Every few weeks if the car sits outside, monthly if it's mostly garaged. It's the one that keeps the other five from getting expensive.
Starting at$95

Headlight Restoration

What's included
Lenses sanded, polished, and sealed, so cloudy yellow plastic goes back to clear. About twenty minutes a light.
When you need it
Headlights gone hazy or amber. Small job, biggest difference you'll notice — especially driving at night.
Starting at$130

Full Interior

What's included
Everything out and vacuumed, hot water extraction on carpets and cloth seats, leather cleaned and conditioned, vents and crevices gone through by hand, odor treated at the source.
When you need it
Before a long trip with kids or a dog in the back, after pollen season, or whenever the inside stopped feeling like a car you'd want to sit in.
Starting at$220

Sell-Your-Car Prep

What's included
Exterior polish, engine bay wiped down, interior reset to base, door jambs and trunk edges gone over — the parts a buyer opens, not just the parts they see in photos.
When you need it
You're listing it this week. This is the detail that helps the price you're asking, not just the photo that gets the click.
Starting at$300

Paint Correction

What's included
Machine polishing to cut swirl marks, wash marks, and light scratches out of the clear coat. One-step or two-step, decided after we look at the paint under light, not before.
When you need it
The car looks fine from six feet and dull from three. That gap between the two is what correction closes.
Starting at$450

Ceramic Coating

What's included
Full decontamination and correction first, then a coating that beads water, makes washing easier, and holds the gloss for years instead of weeks. The car stays with us overnight so it cures untouched.
When you need it
You've already committed to the correction and don't want to redo it every season. One-year and multi-year options, priced by what the paint needed to get there.
Starting at$900

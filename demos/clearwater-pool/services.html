Pool service, start to finish

Residential in-ground and above-ground pools. If it's something we can't handle from the truck that day, you get the price before we order the part — not after.

Weekly maintenance

The full routine every week: brush, vacuum, skim, empty the baskets, and balance the water. We come the same day each week so you always know when the gate needs to be unlocked.

  • Brush walls and steps, skim the surface
  • Vacuum the floor, empty skimmer and pump baskets
  • Test and balance chlorine, pH, alkalinity, and stabilizer
  • Quick check of pump, filter pressure, and skimmer line
  • Text summary after every visit, photos if something's off
$89starting at · per visit
Any in-ground or above-ground pool running through the swim season, May through September.

Openings & closings

Spring opening: cover off and cleaned, equipment reassembled, water filled and balanced, and the system run until it clears. Fall closing: lines blown out, returns plugged, winter chemicals added, cover secured.

  • Spring: cover off, equipment reassembled, water balanced
  • Fall: lines blown out, returns plugged, cover secured
$325starting at
Spring openings start in April; fall closings run through October, weather permitting.

Pump & filter repair

Motors that hum but won't start, leaking seals, cracked unions, clogged impellers, and filters that won't hold pressure. We carry the common parts, so most repairs are done the same visit.

  • Motors, seals, unions, and clogged impellers
  • Filters that won't hold pressure
  • Common parts carried on the truck
$95diagnostic, starting at
Equipment acting up between visits, or something noted during a routine round.

Green to clean

Pool went green after a storm or a long vacation? We shock it, run and clean the filter daily, vacuum out the settled debris, and stay on it until you can see the main drain again.

  • Shock treatment and daily filter runs until it holds
  • Vacuum out the settled debris as it clears
  • Stay on it until the main drain is visible again
Priced after we see it
The water's gone cloudy or green, or you can't see the bottom step anymore.

Salt system service

  • Clean and inspect the cell
  • Check output and salt level
  • Flag a cell nearing the end of its life early

Salt pools, once or twice a season, or when the chlorine reading won't hold.

  • Find where the water's actually going
  • Install replacement pumps, filters, and heaters when it's time

What a week of neglect looks like — and what we do about it

We don't ask you to trust that the water's balanced. This is the same transformation on every green-to-clean call: cloudy on one side, tiled and visible on the other.

  • Green usually means algae, not a leakA hot week without a visit is often enough to tip a pool from clear to cloudy. It's a chemistry problem before it's anything else.
  • We shock it and run the filter dailyNot a once-a-week fix. We're back the next day and the day after, vacuuming what settles until it clears from the top down.
  • Done when you can see the main drainNot when the water looks blue-ish. We stay on it until the deep end floor is actually visible again.

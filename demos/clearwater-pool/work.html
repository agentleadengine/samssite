A season of routes and repairs

A sample of the visits that make up a normal week on the route — from a green-pool rescue to the quieter job of a filter that just needed a clean seal.

Sample project

Green pool recovery

A pool left untouched through three weeks of August heat had gone fully green, bottom step invisible. Four straight days of shock treatment, daily filter runs, and hand-vacuuming brought the main drain back into view before the next weekend.

Sample project

Spring opening, screened pool

Cover off and folded, equipment reassembled after winter storage, water topped off and run until it pulled clear — ready for the first warm weekend.

Sample project

Pump motor rebuild

A motor that hummed but wouldn't turn over turned out to be a seized seal, not a dead motor. New seal, new union gasket, back online the same visit.

Sample project

Leak detection, in-ground pool

Losing about an inch and a half a week with no visible crack. A dye test at the light niche found a hairline gap at the fitting, patched before it became a bigger repair.

Sample project

Salt cell cleaning

Scale buildup on the plates was choking the cell's output, so the chlorine reading kept slipping even with the system running. A soak and a plate-by-plate clean brought it back to normal.

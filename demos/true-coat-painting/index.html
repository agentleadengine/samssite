A straight line, where the tape used to be.

Painter's tape only earns its keep if it goes down straight and comes off before the paint underneath its edge fully cures. That is a two-minute step that decides whether a ceiling line looks cut with a straightedge or feathered by hand. We do not skip it to save the two minutes.

Interior, exterior and cabinets are where most quotes start. See the full list, with what is included in each.

  • Interior painting

    Walls · Ceilings · Closets

    Furniture wrapped and centered, floors covered edge to edge, two finish coats cut and rolled.

  • Exterior painting

    Siding · Soffits · Shutters

    Washed, scraped and feather-sanded first, then primed, caulked, and painted on a dry forecast.

  • Cabinet refinishing

    Kitchens · Vanities · Built-ins

    Doors off and labeled, degreased and sanded, sprayed with a coating that cures hard.

A few questions we hear a lot.

Do I need to move the furniture myself?

Small stuff, yes. Anything heavy, we shift to the center of the room and wrap it before we start.

How long does a full interior take?

Most houses run two to four days, depending on how many rooms and how much the walls need patched before paint.

Do I have to pick the paint before you come out?

No. We usually help narrow it down at the walkthrough, and the color goes on the written quote before anyone commits.

Five sample projects.

These are illustrated scenes, not photographs, standing in for the kind of jobs we run every week: an accent wall, a two-tone kitchen, a repainted elevation, a mitered corner, a deck mid-stain.

  • The corner line

    A single accent wall, cut in tight against the ceiling and the untouched sides, so the color reads as a decision, not an accident.

    Interior · Eggshell · Ultramarine accent wall

  • Two-tone kitchen

    Uppers refinished light to open the room up, lowers gone dark for contrast, new pulls set square.

    Cabinet refinish · Satin · Linen over Iron

  • Weather side

    The sun side of the house, gone chalky and grey, brought back to one even warm tone across the whole elevation.

    Exterior · Flat · Warm Greige

  • The mitered corner

    Crown trim, mitered and caulked so the joint disappears and the line keeps running straight around the room.

    Trim & millwork · Semi-gloss · Cloud White

  • Boards in progress

    Stain back-brushed board by board, working across the deck so every plank gets the same number of passes.

    Deck & fence · Semi-transparent stain · Cedar

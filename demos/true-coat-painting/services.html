Six things we paint, and do all week.

No flooring, no roofs, no side work. Painting and the prep that goes with it, on houses across Long Island. Each service below covers what is included and when homeowners usually call for it.

Interior painting

Walls · Ceilings · Closets

Furniture centered and wrapped, floors covered edge to edge. We patch nail holes and settling cracks, spot-prime the repairs, then cut and roll two finish coats. Switch plates come off; they do not get taped around.

When you need itBefore you list the house, after a wall gets patched from a repair, or when a room's color has never quite matched the rest.

Exterior painting

Siding · Soffits · Shutters

Wash first, then scrape and feather-sand the failing edges. Bare wood gets primed, gaps get caulked, and we watch the forecast, because paint that goes on a cold or damp surface never cures the way it should.

When you need itSiding that is peeling or chalking to the touch, faded trim, or before a sale, since fresh paint is the fastest way to change how a house reads from the curb.

Cabinet refinishing

Kitchens · Vanities · Built-ins

Doors and drawer fronts come off and get labeled. Everything is degreased, sanded, and sprayed with a cabinet coating that cures hard, so the doors are not tacky the first time someone opens them.

When you need itBoxes and hinges are solid but the finish is dated or worn at the edges, and a full remodel is more than the kitchen needs right now.

Trim, doors and millwork

Base · Casing · Crown

Caulk lines run straight, joints get filled and sanded flush, and we sand between coats so the trim reads smooth instead of showing brush texture under the hallway light.

When you need itBaseboards nicked from years of vacuums and moving boxes, doors with buildup from one too many coats, or right after new millwork goes in.

Decks, fences and railings

Stain · Seal · Repaint

Cleaned, brightened and dried before anything is applied. We back-brush the stain into the boards instead of only spraying it, which is what decides how the deck looks after a full summer.

When you need itWood has gone grey, water no longer beads on the boards, or you would rather stain in the spring than mid-cookout in July.

Drywall repair and skim coating

Patches · Texture · Water stains

Popped seams, corner-bead dings and old water stains get cut out, taped, floated and sanded flat. Stains are sealed with a stain-blocking primer so they do not ghost back through the new paint.

When you need itAfter wallpaper or a fixture comes down, a ceiling stain that keeps bleeding through paint, or a crack that keeps opening along the same seam.

Every job includes this, no matter the service.

  • Your paint, on paper

    Brand, line, sheen and color code are written on the quote and left with you, so a touch-up years out matches without guessing.

  • Low-odor products indoors

    Low-VOC interior paint and primer as the default. If a surface needs an oil-based sealer, you hear about it and the ventilation plan first.

  • End-of-day cleanup

    Tools out of the walkway, plastic folded back, and a clear path to the kitchen and bathroom every night the crew is in the house.

  • One crew, start to finish

    The people who quote the job are the people on it. No handoff partway through to a crew you have never met.

  • Straight talk on surfaces

    If a ceiling needs a stain blocker or the siding needs repair before paint, you hear it at the estimate, not as a change order on day two.

  • A labeled touch-up kit

    A quart of each color left behind, taped shut and marked with the room it belongs to.

Not sure which service fits the job?

Describe the rooms or the side of the house and we will point you to the right one, free and in writing.

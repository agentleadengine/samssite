A few quick answers

How do I know it's time to pump?

Slow drains, gurgling, a wet or extra-green patch near the tank, or it's simply been a few years since the last visit — any one of those is worth a call.

What do I do if it's backing up right now?

Stop running water in the house, don't flush, and keep everyone away from any wet spots outside. Call us and we'll walk you through the rest while we're on the way.

Do I need to be home for a pumping visit?

If your lids are at grade and accessible, not necessarily — just make sure we can get to them. For a first visit or anything unusual, it helps to have someone around.

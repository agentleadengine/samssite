Five things we do, and when you actually need each one.

No prices dressed up as promises — just what's included and the signs it's time to call.

Septic Tank Pumping

Most tanks need a full pump-out every three to five years, depending on size and how many people are using the system day to day. We track when you're due and reach out before it becomes a problem, not after.

  • Locating and opening access
  • Full pump-out of solids and scum
  • Level check on the way out
  • Baffle and lid condition check

When you need it: it's been three-plus years since the last pump, you're not sure it's ever been done, or drains are running slower than they used to.

Inspections

A full look at the system, tank to field — done for a reason (sale, refinance, permit) or just for peace of mind. We check tank condition, baffles, riser seals, and effluent depth, then give you a plain written rundown, not a sales pitch.

  • Tank opening and level check
  • Inlet and outlet baffle inspection
  • Riser and lid condition
  • Written summary you can hand off

When you need it: buying or selling a house on septic, refinancing, a permit renewal, or you just want to know where things stand.

Drain Field Service

A soggy yard or slow drains don't always mean the field has failed. Sometimes the tank just needs pumping and the field needs a rest. We check the distribution box and trace the laterals before recommending anything bigger.

  • Full field evaluation
  • Distribution box check
  • Tracing slow or dry laterals
  • Repair or restoration when it's genuinely needed

When you need it: standing water or soggy ground over the field, an odor outside, or slow drains that pumping alone didn't fix.

We check before we dig. Every time.

A soggy yard doesn't always mean a failed field, and a slow drain doesn't always mean a new tank. We test first and tell you what we actually find.

Riser Installs

A lot of older systems bury the tank lid a foot or two down. We install sealed risers at grade so every pump-out and inspection after this one is faster — and doesn't touch your lawn.

  • Locating existing lids
  • Risers and lids sized to your tank
  • Sealed, secure fit
  • Grading around the new lid

When you need it: every visit currently involves digging, you're prepping for an inspection deadline, or you're just tired of losing patches of lawn.

Emergency Backups

A backup doesn't check the calendar. When drains are backing up or the yard's suddenly wet near the tank, we get out to find the real cause — not just clear the symptom — and pump if that's what it needs.

  • Priority response
  • On-site diagnosis
  • Pump-out if needed
  • Plain explanation of what caused it

When you need it: sewage backing into the house, multiple drains going at once, or standing water and odor appearing suddenly near the tank or field.

Subjects and test prep

If your student is behind, we back up until we find the gap. If they're fine and want more, we push ahead. Subjects run as ongoing weekly sessions; SAT, ACT and Regents review run as short packages that start with a diagnostic.

Math

Grades 3–12

Fractions and long division through Algebra I and II, Geometry, Trigonometry and Pre-Calculus. Most math trouble is an older gap wearing a new name, so we test backward before we push forward.

Starting at $70 for a 60-minute session

Reading & Writing

Grades 3–12

Decoding and fluency for younger readers. Thesis statements, evidence and revision for older ones. We work from the assignments your teacher actually gave, not a workbook off a shelf.

Starting at $70 for a 60-minute session

Science

Middle & high school

Living Environment, Earth Science, Chemistry and Physics. Lab reports, unit problem sets, and the vocabulary load that trips up strong students in their first real science class.

Starting at $70 for a 60-minute session

SAT & ACT Prep

8–14 week plans

A full timed diagnostic first, then we drill whichever sections are costing the most points. Pacing, guessing strategy and calculator habits get as much time as the content does.

Packages quoted after the diagnostic

Regents Review

January & June

Focused review built from released exams: Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II, Living Environment, Earth Science, Chemistry and English. We start six weeks out, not six days.

Packages quoted after the diagnostic

Study Skills & Organization

Any grade

For the student whose grade problem is really a planner problem: tracking assignments, breaking a long project into steps, and studying more than the night before a test.

Starting at $70 for a 60-minute session

Tell us the class and the most recent grade. Ten minutes on the phone is usually enough to know where to start.

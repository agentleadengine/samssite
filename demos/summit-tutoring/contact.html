A few questions families ask

Do sessions happen at our house or online?

Both. Most families do in-home sessions during the school year and switch to online for breaks, travel, or a Sunday when nobody wants to leave the house. Online uses a shared whiteboard, so nobody's holding a notebook up to a webcam.

What if my kid isn't failing, just kind of stuck?

That's most of who we work with. If a student is doing fine and wants to go further, we push ahead instead of repeating what the class already covered.

How long before we see a difference?

It depends on the gap and the subject, which is exactly why the first session includes a check, not just a lesson. After that you'll get a short written plan with what to expect and by when.

Still deciding? A phone call costs nothing and usually settles it.

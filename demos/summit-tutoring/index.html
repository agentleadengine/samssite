What's different from a tutoring chain

You keep your tutor

The person who learns how your student thinks is the same person sitting there in March. That continuity is most of why tutoring works at all.

We work from the real class

Bring the syllabus, the graded quiz, the teacher's rubric. We tutor the course your student is actually taking, on that teacher's timeline.

Parents hear from us

A short note goes out after every session: what we covered, what went well, what to watch. No mystery about what you're paying for.

Honest about fit

If a student needs an evaluation, a reading specialist, or honestly just more sleep, we'll say so instead of selling more hours.

Ready when you are.

Call, text, or send a note. Ten minutes on the phone is usually enough to know where to start.

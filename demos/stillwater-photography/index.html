What makes a session work

We shoot in the light that’s already there

No studio, no flash rig to wait around for. Golden hour outside or window light at home, we work with what the day actually gives us.

Kids don’t pose, so we don’t ask them to

Ninety minutes of walking and talking gets you real expressions that no amount of direction could talk them into.

Your gallery arrives with no pitch attached

Every photo we keep is yours to download at full size. Prints are there if you want them, and never pushed if you don’t.

Let’s find an evening that works.

Tell me the ages of the kids and roughly when you’d like to shoot, and I’ll tell you what light we’ll have that week.

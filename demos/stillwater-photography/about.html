The person behind the camera

Stillwater is a one-person studio. No team, no studio address, just someone who shows up with a camera and gets out of the way.

Stillwater started because I got tired of family photos where you could tell nobody wanted to be there. I’m Kate, and I’ve spent enough afternoons on the other side of a camera to know the real picture usually shows up a few seconds after everyone stops holding still.

A session with me looks like an ordinary outing, not a photo shoot. We pick a spot with good late-day light, I give the kids something to do, and I stay quiet with the camera while the day happens around us. Ninety minutes is usually enough.

I shoot across Long Island, NY, mostly outdoors in the last couple of hours before sunset, or at home for newborn sessions. If the weather turns, we just pick the next clear evening. No fee for that, and no hassle either.

How booking works

Four steps, and nothing you haven’t already been told by the time it happens.

01

A short call

Ten minutes on the phone. Who’s coming, how old they are, and what you want to end up with.

02

Pick a place and time

I’ll suggest two or three spots that work with the light we’ll have that week. You pick whichever is closest to home.

03

The session

About ninety minutes. Come as you are. I’ll tell you where to stand, then mostly leave you alone.

04

Your gallery

A link within two weeks, full-size downloads included, with print ordering if you want it. Files stay up for a year.

About the office

Long Island, NY — Nassau & Suffolk counties

Ledger Lane started at a folding table in the back of a Hicksville strip mall office, the kind of space where the sign still said the name of the insurance agency that used to be there. The idea was simple: sit down with people who feel behind on their taxes, uncomfortable, or just tired of guessing, and get them to the other side of it with a clear number and no lecture.

Most of what happens at the desk is asking questions until the picture is complete. Did you sell the house? Did the kid start college? Did the freelance thing turn into a real business this year? The answers change the return more than any software update does, so the conversation happens before anything gets filed.

The office keeps going mostly by word of mouth — people who got tired of mailing documents to somewhere they’d never see, and wanted to sit across from the person doing the work instead.

Every return gets reviewed and signed off with you before it’s transmitted.

Service area

In-person appointments, secure drop-off, and pickup at the office. If you can’t make it in, most returns can be finished by phone once your documents are in hand.

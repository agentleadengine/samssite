What we prepare

Six kinds of returns come through the door most often. Call with your situation and we’ll tell you what to bring and roughly how long it takes — none of this needs to be figured out over the phone in advance.

Individual returns

Federal and New York State returns for W-2 households, retirees, and families with dependents.

What’s included
E-filing plus a printed copy of everything filed, and a check for the credits people usually miss doing it alone.
When you need it
You’ve got a W-2 or two, maybe a 1099-INT or a retirement statement, and want a second set of eyes on it.

Self-employed & 1099

Schedule C returns for contractors, drivers, stylists, and side businesses.

What’s included
Mileage and home-office worksheets done together at the desk, plus quarterly estimate numbers for the year ahead.
When you need it
You drive, cut hair, freelance, or run a side business, and a 1099 showed up where a W-2 used to.

Small business returns

Partnership, S-corp, and single-member LLC filings for shops with a handful of employees.

What’s included
The business return prepared from whatever bookkeeping you’ve got, spreadsheet or shoebox.
When you need it
You’ve got a shop or a small crew and need the business return filed separately from your own.

Rental & investment income

Schedule E for landlords with one unit or several, plus brokerage statements and property sales.

What’s included
Schedule E preparation, brokerage statement reconciliation, and property sale reporting.
When you need it
You collect rent, sold a property this year, or have dividends showing up on a 1099 you don’t recognize.

Prior-year & amended returns

Catching up on a year you skipped, or fixing a return that already went out wrong.

What’s included
A skipped year filed from scratch, or an amendment with a plain explanation of what changed and what to expect next.
When you need it
Life got in the way of a filing, or you caught a mistake on a return after it was already sent.

Quarterly estimates & bookkeeping

Estimated payment vouchers through the year and monthly bookkeeping for businesses that would rather not touch a spreadsheet.

What’s included
Vouchers on a schedule through the year, plus monthly bookkeeping handled off your desk.
When you need it
You’re self-employed and tired of a surprise bill every April, or the books haven’t been touched since last season.

Not sure which one fits?

Say what you’ve got when you call and we’ll sort out the rest — most people aren’t sure either.

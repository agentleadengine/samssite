A few common questions

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Last year’s return if you have it, your W-2s or 1099s, and anything else with a tax form printed on it. If you’re self-employed, a summary of income and expenses is enough — a shoebox of receipts is fine too, we’ll sort it together.

Can you still file if I’m missing a document?

Often, yes. We’ll tell you at the desk whether what’s missing holds up the filing or can be added later with an amendment.

Do you handle a return from a year I skipped?

Yes — prior-year and amended returns are routine. Bring what you have from that year and we’ll work out what’s still needed.

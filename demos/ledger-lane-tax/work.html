Sample projects

A few kinds of returns worked at the desk, described in general terms. No client names or documents shown — these are representative situations, not case files.

Sample project

A family’s first year filing together

Two W-2s, a new dependent, and a first-time question about the child tax credit. We walked through what qualifies and filed federal and state together.

Sample project

A driver’s first Schedule C

A rideshare driver’s first year off a W-2. We sorted mileage against the platform’s own numbers and set up quarterly estimates going forward.

Sample project

A two-person shop’s S-corp catch-up

A two-person S-corp that had fallen behind on separating payroll from owner draws. We reconstructed the year from bank statements and got the return current.

Sample project

A two-unit landlord’s Schedule E

A first-time landlord renting out half a two-family house. We split expenses between the owner-occupied unit and the rental and filed the Schedule E.

Sample project

Two skipped years, filed in one visit

A return that had gone unfiled for two years after a move out of state. We rebuilt both years from what was available and filed them in the same visit.

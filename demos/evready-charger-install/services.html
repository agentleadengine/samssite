What we install and fix

Five things cover most of it, the charger first. If your job isn't on this list, call anyway — you'll get a straight answer on whether it's ours.

Every job on this list starts with the same question: what can your panel actually carry. That answer shapes the rest.

LVL2 · 48A

EV charger installation

What's included. A Level 2 charger on its own dedicated 240-volt circuit, sized to what your panel can actually support and run cleanly to the garage or the driveway side of the house. The permit gets filed and closed out before we call it finished.

When you need it. You're charging off a regular outlet now and it's slow, or you're about to bring an EV home and want the circuit sized right from the start instead of retrofitted later.

MAIN · 200A

Panel upgrades & service changes

What's included. A full panel swap when the existing service doesn't have room for a charger on top of everything else already running through the house — sized for your current load plus the headroom a charger actually takes.

When you need it. The load calculation comes back tight, the panel predates central air and an EV both being part of the picture, or there's no open slot left for a new circuit.

CIRC · LED

Lighting design & install

What's included. Driveway and exterior lighting laid out so the car and the charger are actually visible after dark, plus interior fixtures and dimmers — planned as a circuit, not added fixture by fixture.

When you need it. The driveway goes dark the moment the sun's down, you're finishing a room, or fixtures are getting swapped as part of a bigger job anyway.

DX · FAULT

Troubleshooting & repair

What's included. Tracing a charger session that keeps stopping early, a breaker that trips only sometimes, or an outlet that went dead, back to the actual cause instead of swapping parts and hoping it holds.

When you need it. Anything that used to work and doesn't anymore — especially if it only acts up sometimes. Those are the ones worth calling in before they become a bigger repair.

GEN · XFER

Generator hookups

What's included. A transfer switch wired for the circuits you actually want covered in an outage — including keeping the charger running, if that matters to you.

When you need it. Planned in before the next outage, ideally alongside a panel upgrade or a charger install rather than tacked on afterward.

How we size it

Every quote starts with a load calculation run on your actual panel, not the size of the box on the wall. You'll have a number in writing, and know how we got there, before anything's ordered.

Not sure which one fits?

Describe it on the phone and we'll tell you honestly which of these it is, before anyone drives over.

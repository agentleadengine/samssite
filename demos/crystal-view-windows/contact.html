A few things people ask

Do you clean the inside, or just the outside?

Both, on a standard visit. If you only want the outside done, especially on a hard-to-reach upper floor, just say so when you call.

What happens if it rains on the day you’re booked?

If it’s actually raining when we’re supposed to show up, we call to reschedule, no charge for moving the date. Light rain after the glass is already clean and dry doesn’t undo the work.

Do I need to be home?

For inside work, yes, someone needs to let us in. For outside-only jobs with clear access to the property, you don’t have to be there.

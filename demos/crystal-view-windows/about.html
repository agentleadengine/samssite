A small crew, a short list.

How Crystal View actually runs, told plainly.

Crystal View started the plain way: a bucket, a squeegee, and a few neighbors who mentioned their windows needed help. I’m Ray Doyle, and it’s still a small operation on purpose. A crew that knows the route, not a call center that dispatches whoever’s free.

A visit runs the same way every time. You tell us roughly how many windows and how many floors, and we give you a number on the phone, no sales visit first. On the day, we lay a cloth down before we touch anything, work pane by pane, and don’t call it done until you’ve looked at the glass yourself. Anything you don’t like, we redo before we leave.

We’re a mobile crew, Nassau and Suffolk, houses, condos, and small storefronts. There’s no shop to visit, we come to you, water and ladders already in the van. If you’re near the edge of where we drive, ask straight and we’ll answer straight.

How a visit actually goes

No sales visit, no long forms. Most jobs are booked in one phone call.

  1. Count and quote

    Roughly how many windows, how many floors. We give you a price range on the call and confirm it once we’re standing in front of the house.

  2. Clean day

    Mats down inside, room by room, radio low. Most homes take two to four hours start to finish.

  3. Walk-through

    You look at the glass before we load the van. Anything you don’t like gets redone right then, not scheduled for later.

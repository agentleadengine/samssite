What we clean, plainly.

Priced by pane count and access. Tell us roughly how many windows and whether they open in, and we can usually give you a number on the phone.

Home windows, in and out

Both sides of the glass on every visit, not just the outside. We hand-detail the edges and corners where a cloth usually misses, then wipe the frame and sill before moving to the next room. Shoes off or shoe covers on, whichever you’d rather have in the house.

Good for a seasonal reset, or getting a house ready to show.

Screens and tracks

Screens come off the frame, get washed on both sides, and dry before they go back in. Tracks get vacuumed first, then wiped by hand until the sash slides the way it did when the window was new. This happens on every full-house visit, not as an add-on.

Good for houses where the tracks haven’t been touched in a while, or screens that have gone gray.

Second-story glass

Some upper windows take a ladder, planted somewhere it’s actually safe to plant one. Others take a water-fed pole and a longer reach from the ground. We decide which your house needs when we walk around it, not before.

Good for colonials, capes, and anything with a second floor that hasn’t seen a squeegee in years.

Storefront glass

Weekly, every other week, or monthly, on a schedule that holds. We come before opening so nobody has to step around a bucket or a ladder during business hours.

Good for shops, small offices, and storefronts that want the glass handled without thinking about it.

Skylights and sunrooms

Angled glass collects pollen and film faster than anything else in the house, especially in spring. We reach what a squeegee on a pole can safely reach, and say so plainly if a panel is out of range.

Good for sunrooms, skylights, and anything tilted enough that rain doesn’t rinse it clean on its own.

Post-construction cleanup

New glass after a remodel usually comes with paint flecks, stucco spray, and sticker glue still on it. That’s scraper work, not a squeegee pass, and we price it separately once we’ve seen the actual mess.

Good for right after a window install or a remodel, before the room gets used.

Not sure which one fits? Just call.

Tell us what the house or storefront looks like and we’ll sort the rest out on the phone.

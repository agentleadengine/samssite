Six calls we get, every single week

Tell us the door and the lock. If we can't help, we'll say so on the phone instead of charging you to find out.

Car lockouts

What's included
  • Door opened without prying the frame
  • Push-button and keyless models
  • Spare key cut on the spot if you want one
When you need it

Keys locked inside, whether the motor's running or not.

House & apartment lockouts

What's included
  • Front, back, or basement doors
  • ID check that matches the address
  • Lock tested before we leave
When you need it

Locked out of your own place, day or night.

Rekeying

What's included
  • Existing cylinders rekeyed, hardware kept
  • Old keys stop working same day
  • One key across multiple doors on request
When you need it

New place, old tenant, or a key that walked off.

Deadbolts & door hardware

What's included
  • Deadbolt installs
  • Strike plates anchored into the framing, not just the trim
  • Handle sets swapped without wrecking the door
When you need it

A door that won't latch, or hardware that's had enough.

Broken key extraction

What's included
  • Broken piece removed clean
  • Cylinder checked for damage
  • Fresh key cut on the spot if the lock's still good
When you need it

Half the key in your hand, half in the lock.

Storefront & commercial

What's included
  • Panic bars and aluminum storefront cylinders
  • Mortise locks
  • Master key sets for staff and managers
When you need it

A business that needs one key for the manager and different ones for everybody else.

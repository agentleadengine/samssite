Quick answers

Do you charge more at night?

Overnight and holiday calls carry an after-hours rate. We say the number on the phone before you agree to anything.

Can you open my car without damaging it?

Yes — we don't pry the frame or the weatherstripping. Newer push-button and keyless cars are fine too.

What if my key snapped off in the lock?

We pull the broken piece, check the cylinder for damage, and cut a fresh key on the spot if the lock's still good.

A few common questions

Do you really come to me, or do I still have to bring it in?

The truck comes to you for almost everything on the services list. The only time we'll ask you to bring something in is if the job needs equipment or space a truck-mounted rig can't carry, and you'll hear that on the phone, not after a trip out.

What if it turns out the piece needs to be replaced instead of welded?

Some steel is too far gone to weld back safely, and we'll say so honestly instead of forcing a fix that won't hold. You won't get charged for a repair that isn't a real repair.

How fast can you get here?

Most calls get a same-week visit, and plenty go same-day depending on where you are on Long Island and what's already on the schedule. Call and you'll get a real window, not a guess.

Still deciding?

Call anyway. Ten minutes won't cost you anything.

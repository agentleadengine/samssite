Five things the
truck is built for

Every job below gets the same rig: a generator, a welder, a grinder, a cutting torch, and enough stock steel to fabricate small pieces without a supply run. Pick the one that matches what's broken.

What's on the list

Mobile Welding

The rig shows up with everything it takes to cut, grind, and weld steel right where you're standing: a driveway, a job site, a back lot. If it's broken where it can't easily move, we fix it there instead of hauling it anywhere.

What's included

  • MIG and stick welding, on location
  • On-the-spot cutting and grinding
  • Small-parts fabrication from stock on the truck
  • Generator-powered, no shop outlet needed

When you need this: anything steel that's broken, cracked, or bent where you can't easily move it.

On-site

Gate & Railing Repair

Sagging gates, snapped hinges, bent railings, a post that's pulled loose. These usually don't need a whole new gate, just a welder who'll come look at the actual hinge.

What's included

  • Hinge and post repair
  • Railing straightening and re-welding
  • New hinge or latch fabrication to match what's there
  • Gate realignment so it swings and latches right

When you need this: a gate that won't swing right, a railing that's loose or bent, a post that's separated from its weld.

Same-visit, most jobs

Custom Fabrication

Sometimes nothing's broken, you just need a piece of steel that doesn't exist yet: a rail section, a mount, a small frame. Tell us what it needs to do and we'll build it to fit, on-site or from measurements.

What's included

  • One-off steel pieces built to your measurements
  • Rail, frame, and mount fabrication
  • Match-welding new steel into an existing structure

When you need this: you need something built, not fixed, and a stock part won't fit the job.

Built to your measurements

Trailer Repair

Cracked frame welds, a bent hitch, a tongue that's seen one too many potholes. Trailer steel takes a beating, and most of it can be welded back solid without a shop appointment.

What's included

  • Frame crack repair
  • Hitch and tongue welding
  • Axle mount and cross-member repair
  • Ramp and gate hinge fixes

When you need this: a trailer that's cracking, rattling, or sagging somewhere it shouldn't.

Same-visit, most jobs

Structural Brackets

Ledges, shelving, equipment mounts, deck supports. Brackets that carry real weight need a weld that's actually sound, not a hardware-store fix.

What's included

  • Load-rated bracket fabrication
  • Mounting to existing steel or masonry
  • Repair of brackets that have started to pull or crack

When you need this: anything hanging, resting, or mounted on steel that needs to hold weight safely.

Load-rated welds

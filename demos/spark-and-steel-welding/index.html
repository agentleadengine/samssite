Three calls we get a lot

These come up most, but the truck's built for anything steel that's stuck where it broke.

Gate & Railing Repair

A hinge that's pulled loose, a rail that's bent, a post that's come off its weld. Usually a repair, not a whole new gate.

Trailer Repair

Cracked frame welds, a bent hitch, a tongue that's seen one too many potholes. Most of it welds back solid in one visit.

Custom Fabrication

Nothing's broken, you just need a piece of steel that doesn't exist yet. Tell us what it needs to do and we'll build it to fit.

Why people call back

  • You watch the whole repair. No drop-off, no black-box shop. The torch comes out where you can see it, and it's welded and done before the truck leaves.

  • Priced before the torch starts. You'll hear a real number on-site before anything gets cut or welded, not an invoice after the fact.

  • Steel gets welded, not swapped. If a weld will hold, that's the fix. New parts only come up when a weld actually can't do the job, and you'll hear that straight.

Tell us what broke

A phone call is usually enough to know if this is a one-visit fix. Call during work hours and you'll typically get Deb, not a call center.

What a clean install looks like

The difference is in the details you don't see — hidden line sets, sealed wall sleeves, and condensate drains that actually drain.

Hidden line sets

Refrigerant and power lines run inside walls or trim covers — not stapled to your siding.

Sealed wall sleeves

Every penetration gets a proper sleeve and seal — no drafts, no pests, no water intrusion.

Drains that drain

Condensate lines pitched and routed to drain properly — no drips down your wall, no clogs in August.

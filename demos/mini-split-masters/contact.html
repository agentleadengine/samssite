Questions

How long does a mini-split installation take?

A single-zone install typically takes a full day. Multi-zone systems can take two days depending on how many indoor units and how much line-set routing is involved. We give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do I need ductwork for a mini-split?

No. That's the whole point. Ductless systems heat and cool individual rooms without any ductwork. Each indoor unit connects to one outdoor condenser through a small hole in the wall — about three inches across.

Can a mini-split heat my home in winter?

Modern inverter-driven mini-splits heat efficiently down to very low outdoor temperatures. For Long Island winters, they work well as a primary heat source for the rooms they're installed in, or as supplemental heat for spaces that run cold.

Sample Work

Illustrated examples of the kinds of installations we do. Every project is different — call to talk through yours.

Single-Zone Living Room Install

A 12,000 BTU indoor unit mounted high on a living room wall. Line set runs through the wall cavity to an outdoor condenser on the side of the house — no visible wiring, no surface-mounted tubing.

Sample project

Outdoor Condenser Placement

Condenser mounted on a level pad with proper clearance for airflow. Padded feet keep it off the ground, and the electrical disconnect is mounted within reach per code.

Sample project

Wall Sleeve Penetration

The line-set penetration through the exterior wall gets a proper sleeve, sealed inside and out. This keeps drafts, moisture, and insects out — and keeps your conditioned air in.

Sample project

Smart Thermostat Setup

Wireless remote and smart thermostat configured for each zone. Set different temperatures in different rooms, schedule setbacks for overnight, and control everything from your phone.

Sample project

Condensate Drain Routing

Drain line pitched and routed to a safe discharge point. We test the drain on every install — if water can't flow, we reroute before we leave. No dripping down walls, no clogs in August.

Sample project

Multi-Zone System

One outdoor condenser powering two indoor units — one in the master bedroom, one in the home office. Each zone controls its own temperature independently. No ductwork, no wasted energy heating empty rooms.

Services

Ductless is our specialty. We also handle furnaces, maintenance, and air quality — the full comfort picture for your home.

AC Installation

Whether you're replacing an old unit or adding cooling to a room that never had it, we size the system to your space and install it so it runs quiet from day one.

  • Load calculation and system sizing
  • Indoor unit mounting and outdoor condenser placement
  • Refrigerant line routing and connection
  • Electrical connection and breaker sizing
  • System testing and performance verification
When you need it: your current system failed, you're adding a room, or you want cooling without installing ductwork.

Furnace Repair

When the heat stops, you need someone who can find the problem fast and fix it honestly. We diagnose, explain what's wrong, and repair — no mystery charges, no upselling a new system unless you actually need one.

  • Full diagnostic and pressure testing
  • Igniter, flame sensor, and control board repair
  • Blower motor and capacitor replacement
  • Gas valve and thermocouple service
  • Safety inspection on every repair visit
When you need it: no heat, short cycling, unusual noises, or pilot light won't stay lit.

Ductless Mini-Splits

This is what we do best. Single-zone, multi-zone, inverter-driven systems that heat and cool — no ductwork required. Perfect for additions, converted garages, sunrooms, or any room that's never quite comfortable.

  • Single-zone and multi-zone system design
  • Indoor unit placement for optimal airflow
  • Line-set concealment through walls or trim covers
  • Condensate drainage routed and tested
  • Wireless remote and smart thermostat setup
When you need it: adding comfort to a room without ducts, replacing baseboard heaters, or zoning your home for independent temperature control.

Seasonal Maintenance Plans

A mini-split that's maintained runs quieter, lasts longer, and costs less to operate. We clean the coils, check the refrigerant, clear the drain, and verify performance — twice a year, before the seasons change.

  • Coil cleaning and filter replacement
  • Refrigerant level check and top-off
  • Condensate line flush and drain pan inspection
  • Electrical connection and safety verification
  • Performance report after each visit
When you need it: before cooling season and before heating season — or when your system hasn't been serviced in over a year.

Air Quality & Filtration

Ductless systems filter the air in each room individually. We help you upgrade filtration, add purification, and make sure your system is actually cleaning the air — not just moving it around.

  • HEPA and multi-stage filter upgrades
  • Ionic and UV purification options
  • Filter access and replacement setup
  • Airflow balancing for even distribution
  • Humidity assessment and recommendations
When you need it: allergies, dust, dry air, or you want cleaner air in specific rooms.

Not sure which service you need?

Call and describe what's going on. We'll tell you straight whether it's a repair, a service visit, or a new system — and what it involves.

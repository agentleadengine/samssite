About

We chose to do one thing really well instead of doing everything adequately.

Mini-Split Masters started with a simple observation: most HVAC companies treat ductless as an afterthought. They send a technician who installs two or three a year, follows the manual, and moves on. The results show — visible tubing, poorly pitched drains, units that hum louder than they should.

We decided to do it differently. Ductless is what we do. Every day, every job. We know which units run quietest, which mounts hold tight over time, and which line-set routes stay invisible. We know how to size a room without overloading it, and how to place an indoor unit where the airflow actually reaches the whole space.

The work speaks for itself. When we finish an install, the unit is level, the lines are hidden, the drain works, and your walls look the same as when we got there. That's the standard. If something isn't right, we come back and fix it.

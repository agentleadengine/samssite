What we do, and when to call us for it

Five services. Most projects use two or three of them, roughly in this order.

Sheet 01/05

Site Feasibility Studies

Know what your lot can support before you spend real money on design.

What's included
A zoning and setback read for your lot, a buildable-envelope sketch, and a plain-language list of what's possible, what needs a variance, and what's off the table.
When you need it
Before you buy a lot, before you commit to a big renovation, or when you just want a straight answer about your property.
Sheet 02/05

Custom Home Design

A house designed around your site, your budget, and how you actually live, not a plan pulled from a catalog.

What's included
A conversation about how you'll use the house, a site and zoning read, concept sketches in a few directions, schematic floor plans and elevations, material and system decisions, and coordination through construction drawings.
When you need it
You own a buildable lot, you're tearing down to rebuild, or you're starting from raw land.
Sheet 03/05

Additions & Renovations

Work that respects what your house already does well, and fixes the part that doesn't.

What's included
An as-built survey of the existing house, options for how new construction ties into the structure and roofline, and drawings coordinated with your contractor.
When you need it
You're adding a primary suite, expanding a kitchen, converting a space, or reworking a layout that's stopped working for how you live now.
Sheet 04/05

Permit Drawings

Documents built for what your specific building department actually asks for.

What's included
Construction documents, structural coordination, energy code compliance, and revisions through plan review comments.
When you need it
Your design is settled and you're ready to file, or your last set got kicked back and needs a redraw.
Sheet 05/05

3D Concept Visualization

See the design before it's built, pulled straight from the real drawings.

What's included
A 3D model built from the working drawings, not a separate rendering exercise, walkthroughs of key rooms, and massing studies for how new construction sits against the existing house or street.
When you need it
You're deciding between two directions and need to see them, not just read plan lines.

Not sure which one you need?

Most people aren't, at first. Call or text and tell us roughly where you're starting. We'll point you at the right one.

How the studio works

The short version: you're part of making the drawings, not waiting to see them.

Priya started Blueprint Collective after working at firms where clients found out what their house would look like at one big meeting, months into the project. That always felt backwards. The people paying for the design should be part of making it, not waiting for a reveal.

So the studio works differently. You're in the drawing file as it develops, not just at milestones. Redlines and sketches go up as they happen. Check-ins are short and frequent instead of one long meeting a month. And if a drawing note uses a word you don't recognize, that's on us to explain, not on you to look up.

That's the whole approach, really: a small studio that would rather show you a rough sketch today than a polished one in three weeks. It makes for more conversations along the way, and fewer surprises at the end.

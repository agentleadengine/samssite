Six sessions, honestly described

Every session starts with a few minutes of talking through what's bothering you and what you'd rather we leave alone. What's below is what actually happens after that, not a menu of upsells.

Swedish Massage

60 or 90 min · from $95

Long, even strokes at a pressure you set from the first few minutes. Good if you mostly need to slow down and get blood moving again after a long stretch of desk work, driving, or travel.

When you need it

You're tense but not injured, and mostly want to exhale.

Deep Tissue

60 or 90 min · from $115

Slower and more focused, working through the layers around the neck, shoulders and low back. Speak up as we go; pressure adjusts on the spot, not after the fact.

When you need it

A specific tight spot that Swedish strokes don't reach.

Sports & Recovery

60 min · from $110

Built around what you actually do: running, lifting, court sports, long days on your feet. Includes stretching and a couple of things worth keeping up at home.

When you need it

A training block, race week, or a body that's been standing all day.

Prenatal Massage

60 min · from $105

Side-lying with bolsters, gentle work on hips, low back and legs. Second and third trimester. Please bring your provider's okay to your first visit.

When you need it

Second or third trimester, with your provider's sign-off.

Hot Stone Add-On

Add to any 60 or 90 min · from $25

Warm basalt stones worked along the back and shoulders to soften tight areas before hands-on work. A good fit for cold months and stiff mornings.

When you need it

Winter, or a body that takes a while to loosen up.

Chair Massage On Site

Booked by the hour · from $130/hr

Ten- to fifteen-minute seated sessions at your office or event. No oil, no changing clothes. Two-hour minimum, scheduled a couple of weeks ahead.

When you need it

An office morale afternoon, not a single scheduled slot.

Not sure which one?

Say what's bothering you and we'll point you at the right session, or split the hour between two.

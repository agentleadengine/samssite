A quiet room, one client at a time

Not a chain, not a franchise. One therapist, one table, and a schedule built so the room is never rushed.

Kneaded Relief runs out of one room, one client at a time. That's not a limitation we're working around, it's the whole point. Rushing between two rooms rushes the work in both of them, and an hour you paid for shouldn't feel like someone's watching the clock for the next appointment.

Booking is by appointment, so please call ahead rather than walking in. Twenty-four hours' notice on cancellations keeps the schedule fair to whoever is waiting for that slot next, and it's the same courtesy we'd want if the roles were reversed.

Nothing here is priced to get upgraded at the counter. You might hear about a stretch or two worth doing between visits, and that's the extent of it. Rebooking is entirely up to you, and we'd rather earn the next appointment than ask for it.

How a visit works

1

You call and we pick a time

Appointments run back to back with a buffer, so the room is quiet and reset before you walk in.

2

A short intake, then the table

We go over injuries, pressure preference and any areas to skip. Five minutes, and it changes the whole hour.

3

Say what you need, and we adjust

Too much pressure or not enough, just say so partway through. Nobody gets a trophy for gritting through an hour they didn't enjoy.

4

Water, and out the door

We'll mention what we noticed, maybe a stretch worth doing, then you're free to go. No pitch on the way out.

Our Work

Every project starts with the air and ends with a system that runs quietly. These are sample projects — illustrated scenes showing how we approach the work, not photos of someone else's job.

Sample project

Whole-Home Filtration Upgrade

A Long Island ranch home with persistent dust and allergy complaints. We assessed the existing HVAC system, installed a MERV 13 media filter, and balanced the airflow to all three rooms. The homeowner noticed the difference within two days — less dust on surfaces, clearer morning air.

Sample project

Mini-Split for Home Addition

A homeowner added a sunroom but had no ductwork running to it. We installed a single-zone ductless mini-split — heating in winter, cooling in summer — without touching the rest of the house's system.

Sample project

Furnace No-Heat Repair

A January call — furnace blowing cold air. We traced the failure to a cracked igniter, replaced it, and ran a full safety check on the heat exchanger and venting before calling the job done.

Sample project

AC Replacement with Proper Sizing

An older system that couldn't keep up on 90-degree days. We ran a proper load calculation and found the old unit was undersized. The replacement dropped the homeowner's summer energy bills while reaching every room.

Sample project

Seasonal Maintenance Visit

A routine spring visit on a planned maintenance plan. Filter replaced, coils cleaned, refrigerant levels checked, and an air quality reading taken. The system was ready for the Long Island summer before it arrived.

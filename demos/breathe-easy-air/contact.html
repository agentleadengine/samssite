Questions we hear

How often should I change my air filter?

It depends on the filter type and your household. A standard 1-inch filter usually needs replacing every 1–3 months. If you have pets, allergies, or a higher-efficiency filter, you may need to check it monthly. We can help you figure out the right schedule during a maintenance visit.

What's the difference between a media filter and a standard filter?

A media filter is thicker — usually 4–5 inches — and captures more particles without restricting airflow. It also lasts longer (6–12 months instead of 1–3). If your HVAC system can accommodate one, it's a meaningful upgrade for air quality. We can tell you if your system is compatible.

Do you handle emergency no-heat calls?

Yes. If your furnace goes out in the middle of a Long Island winter, call us. We prioritize no-heat calls and will get to you as quickly as we can. Sunday hours say "emergency calls only" because that's exactly what we mean — if it's not an emergency, we'll see you Monday.

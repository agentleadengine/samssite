Services

Five things, done thoroughly. Every job starts with the air — what's in it and what shouldn't be — and ends with a system you can forget about because it just works.

AC Installation

A new air conditioner should be sized to your home, not just to the sales sheet. We calculate the load, match the equipment, and install it so the airflow reaches every room — not just the one closest to the unit.

What's included

  • Load calculation for your home's square footage and layout
  • Equipment selection and installation
  • Ductwork inspection and adjustments
  • Thermostat setup and calibration
  • Air quality baseline check before and after install
When you need it: Your current system struggles on hot days, your energy bills are climbing, or the unit is more than 12–15 years old.

Furnace Repair

When the heat goes out, you need someone who can find the problem — not just swap parts until something works. We diagnose the actual failure, explain what happened, and fix it right the first time.

What's included

  • Full system diagnostics — ignition, blower, heat exchanger, thermostat
  • Clear explanation of the failure and the fix
  • Repair or component replacement
  • Safety check on carbon monoxide and venting
  • Air filter inspection and replacement if needed
When you need it: No heat, short cycling, strange noises, or the pilot keeps going out. Don't wait — a failing furnace can be a safety issue.

Ductless Mini-Splits

No ducts, no problem. Mini-splits deliver heating and cooling to individual rooms — quiet, efficient, and perfect for additions, garages, or homes without existing ductwork. We size each indoor unit to the room it serves.

What's included

  • Room-by-room cooling and heating load calculation
  • Indoor unit placement and mounting
  • Outdoor condenser installation and line-set routing
  • Zoned temperature control setup
  • Filtration check on each indoor unit
When you need it: A room that's always too hot or too cold, a home addition, a converted garage, or you want independent temperature control in different rooms.

Seasonal Maintenance Plans

Your HVAC system runs all year — it needs attention twice. Our seasonal visits catch small problems before they become mid-summer or mid-winter emergencies. Every visit includes an air filter check, because a clogged filter is the #1 cause of preventable breakdowns.

What's included

  • Spring visit: AC tune-up, refrigerant check, coil cleaning, filter replacement
  • Fall visit: furnace inspection, safety check, ignition test, filter replacement
  • Priority scheduling for any repair calls
  • Air quality check at each visit — filter condition and airflow
  • System performance report after each visit
When you need it: If you want to stop replacing furnaces and AC units before their time, seasonal maintenance is the single best investment you can make.

Air Quality & Filtration

This is where we start. The air in your home carries dust, pollen, pet dander, and particles you can't see. A proper filtration system — matched to your HVAC, not bolted on as an afterthought — removes the vast majority of airborne contaminants before they reach your lungs.

What's included

  • Whole-home air quality assessment — particulates, humidity, airflow
  • Filtration system selection — MERV rating matched to your system
  • Media filter or electronic air cleaner installation
  • Humidity evaluation and balance recommendations
  • Ongoing filter replacement guidance
When you need it: Allergy symptoms at home, visible dust on surfaces soon after cleaning, dry or humid air, or a household member with respiratory sensitivities.

Not sure which service you need?

Call and describe what's going on — we'll point you to the right service or schedule a visit to find out.

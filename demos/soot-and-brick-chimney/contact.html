A few quick answers

How long does a sweep actually take?

Most flues take under an hour once we're set up. A full sweep and inspection together usually run somewhere between 60 and 90 minutes, depending on how the flue's been treated.

Do I need to be home?

Yes. Someone 18 or older needs to let us in and be able to tell us what the fireplace or insert has been doing, since that shapes what we check first.

What if you find something broken mid-sweep?

We stop, show you what we found, and give you a plain answer on the fix before we touch anything else. Nothing gets decided without you seeing it first.

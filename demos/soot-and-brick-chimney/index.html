How a visit actually goes

Four steps, same order every time, so there's no guessing what happens after you call.

1

Drop cloths down first

Before anything gets touched, the hearth and floor around it are covered. Soot stays in the flue and the bag, not your rug.

2

Full-length brush and rod

Every section of the flue gets swept, not just what's visible from the firebox. If something looks off, we stop and look closer.

3

You see what we found

A cracked crown, a loose cap, a liner joint that's opened up: it gets pointed out plainly, with a photo if you want one.

4

Repair first when it makes sense

Most chimneys need cleaning and a small fix, not a rebuild. We'll say so plainly, even when the bigger job would pay better.

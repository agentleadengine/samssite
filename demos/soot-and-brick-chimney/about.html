One name on the truck

No dispatcher, no franchise script. Here's who shows up and how the shop actually runs.

I got into this because a chimney is the one part of a house where the masonry, the fire, and the air all have to agree with each other, or something goes wrong that you can't just paint over. I like that it's real work: brush, rod, trowel, camera. Nothing about it is a shortcut.

Most of what I do is less dramatic than people expect. It's a brush pass, a crown that needs resealing, a liner joint that opened up. I'd rather tell you it's a small fix than talk you into a bigger one, and I'll show you the part that's actually wrong before I touch anything else.

I keep the truck local, Nassau and Suffolk, so if something feels off after a repair I can get back to it fast. Soot & Brick is one name on the invoice and one person who answers when you call.

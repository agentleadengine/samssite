A few jobs, start to finish

Five sample projects drawn up the way we'd sketch them on the tailgate: the house, what was wrong, what we did about it. Every scene here is illustrated, not photographed, and every project is a stand-in for the kind of work we actually do.

Sample project

Cape-style two-story · oil-to-gas conversion

The old clay tile liner had cracked in three spots once the flue temperature changed with the new appliance. We fit and insulated a stainless liner sized for the gas insert, then tested the draft before we packed up.

Liner installs
Sample project

1920s brick colonial · storm-cracked crown

Hairline cracks in the crown were letting water run behind the brick face every time it rained. We rebuilt the crown, sealed it, and set a new cap with a tight mesh screen so animals stay out.

Sample project

Ranch with a wood-burning insert · annual sweep

Heavy glaze from a season of burning whatever wood was on hand. A full brush and rod pass cleared the flue, firebox, and smoke shelf, with drop cloths keeping the room clean the whole time.

Sample project

Farmhouse rebuild · spalling brick face

Years of freeze-thaw had the face brick popping off in chunks and the joints gone soft. We repointed the stack top to bottom and reset six loose bricks so the wall sheds water again.

Sample project

Split-level · pre-sale inspection

The buyer asked for a full look before closing. A camera pass down the flue found a hairline crack in the tile liner, and it went into the write-up plainly enough to be priced into the deal, not discovered later.

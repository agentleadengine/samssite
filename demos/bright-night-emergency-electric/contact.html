A few things people ask

Is the 24/7 line actually real?

Yes, for real emergencies: no power, sparking, burning smells, a main breaker that won’t reset. Call any hour and a person picks up. If what you’re describing can safely wait for daylight, we’ll say that too, honestly, instead of sending a truck out at 3am for a job that didn’t need one.

What should I do before you get there?

If you smell burning or see sparking, shut off the main breaker if it’s safe to reach, get everyone clear of the area, and call from somewhere else in the house. If a single breaker tripped, you can try resetting it once — if it trips again immediately, leave it off and wait for us.

Can I get an EV charger without a full panel upgrade?

Often, yes. We check the load on your existing panel against what’s already running — the dryer, the range, central air — before we say either way. If there’s room, it’s one new circuit. If there isn’t, we’ll say so honestly before any work starts.

Still not sure? Call and ask.

Six calls, told straight

No client names, no addresses, no photos — just six jobs that look like what actually comes through the phone, drawn in the shop’s own style instead of staged for a camera.

Sample project

Main breaker failed, 1am, whole house dark

The call came in a little after midnight: no power anywhere, breaker wouldn’t reset, two kids asleep upstairs. We were in the panel within the hour, found a burned lug on the main breaker, replaced it, and had the house back on before the furnace ever went cold.

Sample project

Kitchen outlets that only died some mornings

Not an emergency, just maddening: two outlets that worked fine most days and went dead whenever the coffee maker ran early. Traced it to a GFCI outlet failing intermittently as it warmed up, swapped it during a normal weekday visit, and it hasn’t tripped since.

Sample project

100-amp fuse box to a 200-amp panel

An old round-fuse box with no spare capacity, feeding a house that had since added central air. We ran a new 200-amp service, moved every circuit over without a full day of downtime, and left a typed directory taped inside the door.

Sample project

Level 2 charger, garage wall

Checked the load against the dryer and range first — there was room. One dedicated 240-volt circuit run from the panel to the garage, on its own breaker, so charging overnight never competes with anything else in the house.

Sample project

Recessed lighting and dimmers, kitchen remodel

Eight cans across two dimmer zones, matched to the LED bulbs going in so they wouldn’t hum or flicker turned down low. The island fixture got a fan-rated box instead of the plastic one it used to hang from.

Sample project

Interlock kit before the next storm, not during it

A portable generator that used to run extension cords through a cracked window. We installed an interlock kit and a manual transfer switch so it feeds the fridge, the sump pump, and a few lights straight from the panel.

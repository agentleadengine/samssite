Five jobs, every one tuned for quiet

Tell us the job and the door: new install, a repair, or one that's just started sounding different. If it's outside what we do well, we'll say so straight instead of taking the work anyway.

New door installation

What's included
  • Measured and hung true to the opening, shimmed and leveled before anything's bolted down
  • Insulated panel matched to a look that actually fits the house
  • Every hinge and roller balanced before the first full cycle
When you need it

A door that's aged out, or a garage getting the design update the rest of the house already had.

Spring & cable repair

What's included
  • Torsion spring replaced to the door's actual weight, not a stock size off the shelf
  • Cables swapped in pairs so tension stays even on both sides
  • Full balance check after, by hand and by the numbers
When you need it

A door that's suddenly heavy to lift, a loud bang on open or close, or a cable you can see fraying.

Opener install & repair

What's included
  • Belt-drive units set and tuned so the motor doesn't transmit noise into the house
  • Force and travel limits set to the door's actual weight, not a factory default
  • Old chain-drive units swapped over cleanly, mount and all
When you need it

An opener that grinds, a door that reverses for no reason, or a chain-drive that's just loud.

Annual tune-up

What's included
  • Every roller, hinge, and bolt checked and adjusted
  • Balance re-tested with the opener disconnected
  • Opener force and safety reverse verified
When you need it

Once a year, or the first time the door starts sounding different than it used to.

Off-track service

What's included
  • The door reset onto the track safely, without forcing bent hardware back into place
  • Track alignment checked at every bracket
  • An honest look at whatever knocked it off in the first place
When you need it

A door stuck open or shut after coming off the rail. These calls get priority scheduling within business hours.

Job not on the list?

Describe it on the phone. If we're not the right fit, we'll point you to what is.

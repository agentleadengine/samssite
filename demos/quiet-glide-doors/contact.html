Quick answers

How much quieter is a belt-drive opener, really?

A belt has no metal-on-metal chain contact, so what's left to hear is mostly the motor itself, and that gets isolated and tuned so it doesn't transmit into the house framing. It's a real difference, not a marketing one.

Can you match a modern design to an older house?

Yes. Panel style and finish get picked to fit the house you actually have, not swapped for whatever's closest on the truck.

What if my door comes off the track?

Call it in. We reset it onto the track safely, check alignment at every bracket, and give you a straight answer about what caused it. These calls get priority scheduling within business hours.

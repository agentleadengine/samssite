Who's actually coming to the door

Jenna Cutler ran service calls for two different HVAC outfits on Long Island before starting North Wind. Both had the same problem: the tech doing the work and the number on the invoice never seemed to agree with each other.

North Wind runs the other way. The same person who quotes the job does the work and stands behind it. We measure before we price anything, the number you get before we start is the number on the invoice after, and if a part failed, you get to see it in your hand. That isn't a slogan. It's just fewer surprises for everyone, including us.

North Wind runs out of one shop covering Nassau and western Suffolk. Keeping the service area tight is deliberate: shorter drives mean tighter arrival windows, and a no-heat call in January doesn't sit on a list for six hours.

Sample work

Six calls that show how the gauge moves from a problem to a fixed reading. Illustrative composites, not real client records or photos.

Sample project

Frozen line set, mid-July Cooling

The system had been running low on refrigerant for months, and ice built up on the line set until the coil choked entirely. We recovered what was left, pressure-tested for the leak, repaired it, and recharged to spec. The reading held steady a week later.

Sample project

Furnace short-cycling, January Heating

Furnace was firing for ninety seconds, shutting off, and firing again, all night. A cracked flame sensor was reading a false signal. New sensor, and the cycle length went from ninety seconds to a full, normal run.

Sample project

A load calc before the swap Installation

The old system had been sized off square footage alone and short-cycled every summer. We ran a full load calculation, windows, insulation, orientation, before quoting the replacement. The new system runs longer cycles at a lower speed and actually pulls the humidity down.

Sample project

Duct leak found in the attic Ductwork

Upstairs never matched the thermostat reading downstairs. A static pressure test pointed to a split seam in the attic trunk line, twenty feet from the unit, losing conditioned air into the insulation. Sealed and retested; upstairs closed the gap by several degrees.

Sample project

A mini-split for a finished attic Mini-splits

The finished attic had no ductwork to extend into and no reasonable path to add any. One outdoor condenser now feeds two indoor heads on separate thermostats, one for the office end and one for the sitting area.

Sample project

A fall tune-up catches a cracked exchanger Heating

Routine fall tune-up, nothing had been complained about yet. Combustion analysis and the camera found a hairline crack in the heat exchanger, and the CO reading confirmed it. The furnace was shut down on the spot and the part was ordered, before it became a cold-night emergency.

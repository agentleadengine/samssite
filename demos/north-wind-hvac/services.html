Everything we service

We work on the equipment already in your basement, attic or side yard, and replace it only when replacing it is genuinely the cheaper answer. Six services, each with its own gauge to watch.

Cooling

Air conditioning repair

Blowing warm, tripping the breaker, or a line set frozen solid, we check refrigerant charge, capacitor and airflow before anyone brings up the word replacement. Most warm-air calls turn out to be one of those three.

When you need it: the unit runs but the air isn't cold, it short-cycles, or it won't start at all.
Year-round

System installation

We run a load calculation before we quote a system: square footage, insulation, window load and duct condition. An oversized unit short-cycles, never pulls the humidity out of the air, and costs more every month it runs.

When you need it: your current system is past repairing, or you're finishing a space that never had one.
Heating

Furnaces & boilers

No heat on the coldest night of the year, a pilot that won't hold, a boiler short-cycling every few minutes; cleaned, tested and left running the way it should. No-heat calls jump to the front of the schedule, including after hours.

When you need it: the heat won't hold, won't light, or the utility bill jumped with no other explanation.
Year-round

Ductless mini-splits

Additions, finished attics, sunrooms, and the one bedroom that was never comfortable. One outdoor unit can feed several indoor heads, each on its own thermostat.

When you need it: a room runs hot or cold independent of the rest of the house, or there's no ductwork to extend into it.
Year-round

Ductwork & airflow

Sealing leaky trunk lines, adding a return, balancing registers room by room. A good share of "the AC is weak" calls turn out to be a duct problem, not the condenser.

When you need it: one room is always the outlier, temperature-wise, or the system runs constantly without catching up.
Year-round

Tune-ups & maintenance

Coil cleaning, condensate drain flush, safety checks, spring for cooling and fall for heat, plus a written list of what we found, including the parts that are still fine.

When you need it: once a season, before the first heat wave or the first cold snap, whichever is next.

How estimates work

Estimates are free on full replacements. Repair calls carry a diagnostic fee, credited toward the work if you go ahead.

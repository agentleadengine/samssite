About Jetstream

Jetstream is a one-crew pressure washing operation working across Long Island. The idea behind it is simple: most of what makes a house look tired isn’t the paint or the siding itself, it’s what has grown on top of it. Algae, mildew, and a season or two of pollen build up until the actual color underneath is hard to remember.

The approach stays the same on every job: figure out what the surface can handle before turning anything on. Vinyl siding and asphalt roofing get a low-pressure soft wash with a cleaning solution built for algae and mildew. Concrete, pavers, and other flatwork get the surface cleaner and higher pressure, since they can take it and siding can’t.

We bring our own water tank for houses where the spigot won’t keep up, and we protect beds, fixtures, and furniture before any water goes on. Most single-family homes are a half day, start to finish, and you don’t need to be home for it.

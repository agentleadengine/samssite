A few things people ask

Will pressure washing damage my siding or paint?

Not with the right approach. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution instead of blasting, so it’s safe on vinyl, aluminum, stucco, and painted wood. Higher pressure only goes on surfaces built for it, like concrete.

Do I need to be home for the appointment?

No. As long as we can reach an outdoor water spigot and the work area is clear, we don’t need you on site.

How far out do you book?

Usually about a week out from April through October. We keep washing through the winter too, on days the temperature allows it.

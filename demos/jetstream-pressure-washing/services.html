Right pressure for the surface

Six things we clean, each with its own pressure, chemical, and timing. Every estimate still happens the same way: describe the surface on the phone and we’ll tell you roughly what to expect, including whether it’s worth doing at all.

Residential and small commercial, across:

Houses Roofs Driveways Decks Storefronts HOA common areas

House soft wash

Low pressure and a cleaning solution that kills the algae and mildew instead of blasting it off. Safe for vinyl, aluminum, stucco, and painted wood, and it stays clean longer than a plain rinse.

What’s included

  • Low-pressure application with an algae- and mildew-killing solution
  • Plantings and fixtures protected before we start
  • Full rinse and walk-through when we’re done

When you need it

Green or black streaking on siding, or once a year to keep it from building back up.

Roof cleaning

Those black streaks on asphalt shingles are algae. We treat them with a no-pressure soft wash so the granules stay on the shingle where they belong.

What’s included

  • No-pressure soft wash treatment made for asphalt shingles
  • Plantings below covered before the rinse
  • Often bundled with a siding wash on the same visit

When you need it

Dark streaks running down the shingles, usually starting near the ridge.

Driveways & walkways

Surface cleaner on concrete and pavers for an even finish with no zebra striping. Oil spots pre-treated. Paver sand replaced and joints re-sanded on request.

What’s included

  • Surface-cleaner pass for an even finish, no striping
  • Oil spots pre-treated before washing
  • Paver sand and joint re-sanding available on request

When you need it

Before a sealcoat, before hosting, or whenever oil spots and green film build up.

Decks & fences

Wood gets cleaned at a pressure that won’t furr the grain. We can wash only, or wash and come back once it’s dry for sealing.

What’s included

  • Wood-safe pressure that cleans without raising the grain
  • Wash-only, or a return visit once dry for sealing

When you need it

Before staining season, or when the wood has gone grey and feels slick.

Patios & pool decks

Slate, bluestone, stamped concrete, and pavers cleaned before the season starts. We tape off and rinse plantings so nothing gets burned by runoff.

What’s included

  • Stone- and paver-safe cleaning for slate, bluestone, and stamped concrete
  • Nearby plantings taped off and rinsed after

When you need it

Before pool season opens, or when the stone gets a green film that’s slick barefoot.

Gutter brightening

The dark drip stripes on gutter faces don’t come off with a normal wash. We scrub them with a gutter-specific cleaner while we’re already on the house.

What’s included

  • Gutter-specific cleaner scrubbed onto the drip stripes
  • Usually added on while we’re already washing the siding

When you need it

Dark streaks on the gutter face that a normal wash won’t touch.

Not sure which one you need?

Call and describe the surface. We’ll tell you which service fits and roughly what to expect.

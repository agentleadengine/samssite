Sample work

Six situations we see a lot, drawn the same way we draw everything else on this site. Not photos of a real job, but the same surfaces, the same problems, and the same fix.

Sample project

Vinyl siding, full house soft wash

A two-story vinyl-sided house with a heavy green cast on the shaded wall. Soft washed with an algae-specific solution, no pressure on the siding itself. One visit, about three hours.

Sample project

Asphalt roof, algae streaking

Dark algae streaking down from the ridge on a fifteen-year-old roof. No-pressure soft wash; granules stayed on the shingle. Rinsed from the ground.

Sample project

Paver driveway, oil staining

Oil staining near the garage line and dulled, sandy joints. Spots pre-treated, surface cleaned, joints re-sanded after.

Sample project

Cedar deck, weathered and slick

A cedar deck gone grey and slick after a few seasons without sealing. Washed at a pressure that wouldn’t raise the grain, left to dry before a separate sealing pass.

Sample project

Bluestone patio & pool deck

Bluestone around an in-ground pool with a green film that made the edge slick barefoot. Cleaned stone by stone, coping included, plantings taped off during the rinse.

Sample project

Storefront gutter brightening

A small storefront with dark drip stripes running down white fascia below the gutters. Gutter-specific cleaner scrubbed onto the stripes during a regular storefront wash.

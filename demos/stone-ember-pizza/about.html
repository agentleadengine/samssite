About Stone & Ember

Stone & Ember started with one idea: a wood fire cooks pizza better than a thermostat ever will. No dial, no setting — just oak and maple burning down to the right heat, judged by eye and by feel, then held there for hours.

There's one oven and one line, so the menu stays short on purpose. Dough gets mixed every morning and rests before it ever sees the fire. When you call, whoever's at the counter checks what's ahead of you and gives you an honest time to come by.

Pickup runs out of the one counter. Catering trays travel across Nassau and western Suffolk — offices, job sites, team dinners, whatever needs feeding. Ask when you call and you'll get a straight answer on whether the timing works.

How we run the oven

A wood oven doesn't run itself. Someone feeds it, watches it, and holds it around 800°F for six hours straight — that's most of why the crust tastes like this.

1

Dough, mixed every morning

Flour, water, salt, a little yeast, then a long cold rest. Nothing bakes the same day it's mixed.

2

Fire built by four

Oak and maple, no gas assist. The floor holds around 800°F before the first pie ever goes in.

3

Ninety seconds, turned twice

That's the whole bake. It comes out spotted and a little uneven — that's exactly how it should look.

What we find, drawn the way we found it

Six situations that come up again and again on Long Island roofs, drawn the same way the rest of this site is drawn. Not photos of a real job. The same problems, and the same fix.

Sample project

Ceiling stain, cracked pipe boot

A bedroom ceiling stain that pointed straight up at nothing. The actual crack was in a rubber vent boot several feet up-slope, found on the attic-side walk and resealed the same visit.

Sample project

Full tear-off, architectural shingles

A colonial with the original shingles, curling on every south-facing course. Torn off to the deck, new ice and water shield at the eaves, ridge vent added.

Sample project

Chimney counter-flashing

Step flashing along a brick chimney had pulled loose after years of freeze and thaw. Reset the flashing and sealed the reglet so the seam holds through winter.

Sample project

Wind-lifted ridge course

A wind event lifted a run of shingles along the ridge and left two more cracked from a branch strike. Photographed and measured for the claim, tarped that afternoon.

Sample project

Seamless gutter run

Sectional gutters were pulling away from the fascia and overflowing at the corners. Replaced with a seamless run formed on site and re-pitched so it actually drains.

Sample project

Porch roof membrane tie-in

A porch addition where old roll roofing had started to pond water at the low corner. Re-decked and covered in membrane, tied into the house roof with new step flashing along the seam.

About Ridgeline

Ridgeline started with a simple complaint: too many roof quotes get written from the driveway. Someone looks up, guesses at what’s wrong, and prices it before anyone climbs a ladder. We wanted the estimate to come from what’s actually on the roof, so that’s the only way we do it.

Every call starts the same way. We get up there, walk the whole roof, and photograph anything that looks off, including the attic side when we can reach it. You see those photos before you see a price, and if a repair is the honest answer, that’s what we quote instead of pushing a full replacement.

Jobs run anywhere from a few hours to a couple of days depending on the size. We tarp the landscaping before anything comes off the roof, sweep for nails morning and night, and walk the finished work with you before we call it done.

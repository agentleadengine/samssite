Roof work, start to finish

Six things we get called for, most weeks. Whichever one you’re dealing with, the estimate still starts the same way: on the roof, with a camera, before anyone talks price.

Long Island, NY, homes and small commercial:

Leak repair Replacement Flashing Storm damage Gutters Flat roofs

Leak repair

We trace the water back to where it actually gets in, which is usually not where the stain is. Flashing, nail pops, cracked boots, ice dam damage. Repaired the same visit when the material is on the truck.

Most common call

What’s included

  • Full roof and attic-side inspection, photographed as we go
  • Leak traced to its actual entry point, not just the stain
  • Repair completed the same visit when parts are on the truck

When you need it

A stain spreading on the ceiling, a drip during rain, or daylight showing through the attic deck.

Roof replacement

Tear-off down to the deck, rotted plywood replaced before anything goes back down, ice and water shield at the eaves and valleys, synthetic underlayment, then architectural shingles. Ridge vent goes on every job.

Typical job: 1–2 days

What’s included

  • Full tear-off to the deck, not a layer over the old roof
  • Ice and water shield at the eaves and valleys
  • Ridge vent and architectural shingles on every job

When you need it

The roof is original to the house, shingles are curling or losing granules, or a repair would just be patching a roof that’s already done.

Flashing & chimney

Step flashing, chimney counter-flashing, skylight curbs, and pipe collars. These are the spots that leak first on a roof that otherwise still has years left in it.

Repair before replace

What’s included

  • Step flashing reset along walls and dormers
  • Chimney counter-flashing reset or replaced
  • Skylight curbs and pipe collars resealed

When you need it

The roof itself is fine, but water is getting in around a chimney, skylight, or vent pipe.

Storm damage

Wind-lifted shingles, tree strikes, and hail bruising. We document the damage with photos and measurements you can hand straight to your adjuster, and we can tarp the same day.

Emergency response

What’s included

  • Damage documented with photos and measurements for the claim
  • Emergency tarping available the same day
  • Repair or replacement quoted once the roof is dry

When you need it

Right after a storm, a tree limb strike, or when shingles start showing up in the yard.

Gutters & downspouts

Seamless aluminum gutters formed on site, hung with hidden hangers, and pitched so they actually drain. Downspouts routed away from the foundation, guards optional.

Often with a re-roof

What’s included

  • Seamless aluminum gutters formed on site to fit the house
  • Hidden hangers and a true pitch so water actually drains
  • Downspouts routed away from the foundation

When you need it

Gutters sagging, overflowing, or pulling away from the fascia, or you’re already having the roof done.

Flat & low-slope

Porch roofs, dormers, and additions where shingles don’t belong. We install rolled modified bitumen or TPO membrane and tie it into the pitched roof so the seam stays sealed.

Additions & porches

What’s included

  • Modified bitumen or TPO membrane sized to the section
  • Seam tied cleanly into the adjoining pitched roof
  • Drainage checked so water doesn’t pool

When you need it

A porch, dormer, or addition roof that’s too flat for shingles, or an existing flat section that’s ponding water.

Not sure which one you need?

Call and describe it. We’ll tell you which service fits, and if a repair is the honest answer, that’s what we’ll quote.

About Cedar & Stone

Most landscaping quotes around here arrive as a text message with a number attached and not much else. No walk-through, no explanation of what the number actually covers. Cedar & Stone started because that felt like a strange way to hand someone a few thousand dollars of work on their own property.

So we do it slower. Someone walks the property with you, measures what needs measuring, and leaves a written price before any equipment shows up. If a job isn't something we do well, we'll say so on the phone instead of taking it anyway and figuring it out later.

The same crew comes back every time, you get a text the day before a visit, and they clean up before they leave. That's most of what we're actually selling. Not surprises.

How it works

No surprise invoices, no crew you've never met walking around the side of the house.

You call

Tell us the town and what's bothering you about the property. Two minutes on the phone usually sorts out whether we're the right fit.

We walk it with you

We measure, look at drainage and access, and talk through what's worth doing now versus what can wait a season.

Written price

One page. What's included, what's not, and what it costs. Take a week with it if you want.

The crew shows up

You get a text the day before every visit. Same faces each time, and they clean up before they pull out.

Where we work, and when

We keep the routes tight so the crew spends the day working, not sitting in traffic.

Service area

Residential properties only, across Nassau County and western Suffolk, from the North Shore down to the South Shore. If you're just outside our usual route, call anyway. Sometimes it works out.

What we take care of

Six things, done properly, on a schedule you can plan around. If a job is outside what we do well, we'll tell you that on the phone instead of guessing at it in your driveway.

Weekly mowing & trimming

What's included
Cut, string-trim the edges, and blow the clippings off the walks and driveway.
When you need it
Weekly, spring through fall. Some properties step down to every other week once the heat breaks in September.
From $55 / visit

Spring & fall cleanups

What's included
Leaves out of the beds and off the lawn, perennials cut back to the right height, everything hauled away the same day.
When you need it
Two windows a year: April, and late October through November, before the ground freezes.
From $325

Garden beds & mulch

What's included
Weeding, a clean spade-cut edge, and mulch laid deep enough to actually hold moisture.
When you need it
Late spring, once the ground has warmed and before the first real heat wave sets in.
From $450

Shrubs, hedges & small trees

What's included
Foundation plantings, privacy hedges, and ornamentals pruned to shape, not just hacked back.
When you need it
Depends on the plant. Spring bloomers get pruned right after flowering, hedges hold their line best with a mid-summer trim.

Priced at the walk-through. Most of these jobs ride along with a cleanup or bed visit.

Stone walkways & patios

What's included
Bluestone, pavers, or dry-laid steps, always on a base we dig and compact ourselves.
When you need it
Any time the ground isn't frozen. Fall and spring installs give us the most flexible scheduling.
From $2,400

Lawn renovation

What's included
Core aeration, overseeding, and a topdressing pass.
When you need it
Early fall, and only early fall. That's the short window new grass actually takes hold out here.

Priced at the walk-through, based on lawn size and how much of it needs seed.

Not sure which of these your property needs?

Say what's bothering you about the yard. We'll tell you what it is on the phone.

A few things people ask

Do I need to be home for the walk-through?

No, but it helps if gates are unlocked and dogs are inside. We'll text a few photos and a price after.

What happens if it rains on our scheduled day?

Mowing moves to the next dry day that week. You'll get a text if the schedule shifts.

Do you supply the mulch and stone, or do we?

We supply everything unless you have a specific product you want matched. Either way, we'll price it before we order anything.

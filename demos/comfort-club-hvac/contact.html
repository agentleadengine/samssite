Questions We Hear Often

How fast can you get here if my heat is out?

Members get priority dispatch — we aim for same-day on no-heat calls during business hours. Non-members are scheduled in the next available slot, which depends on the season and call volume.

Do I have to be a member to call you?

No. We take service calls from anyone on Long Island. The membership is for people who want the seasonal maintenance relationship and the priority that comes with it, but you can always call for a one-time repair.

What does the membership actually include?

Two seasonal tune-ups per year (one cooling, one heating), priority dispatch, no after-hours surcharge for members, and a system history on file so any return visit picks up where the last one left off. The specifics depend on your home's equipment — we'll walk you through it.

Services

Five core services, all covered under one membership relationship. From new installations to seasonal tune-ups, every job gets the same attention — because the goal is a system you forget about.

AC Installation

What's included

  • Load calculation to match capacity to your home's actual square footage and sun exposure
  • Removal and haul-away of the old condenser and coil
  • New pad placement, refrigerant line set, and electrical connection
  • Thermostat setup and a full cooling cycle test before we leave
  • Membership registration so the new unit is on file from day one

When you need it

  • Your current system is more than 12–15 years old and repairs are adding up
  • You're adding central air to a home that never had it
  • Certain rooms never cool down even with the old unit running full blast
  • You want a quieter, more efficient unit before the next summer season

Furnace Repair

What's included

  • Full diagnostic — ignition, flame sensor, blower motor, heat exchanger, and thermostat communication
  • Transparent explanation of what failed and why, before any work begins
  • Parts replacement with components rated for your unit
  • Post-repair combustion and airflow verification
  • If a repair isn't worth it, an honest recommendation and replacement quote

When you need it

  • Heat is blowing cold or the furnace won't ignite at all
  • You hear clicking, rattling, or a boom on startup
  • The pilot keeps going out or the system short-cycles
  • Your energy bills spiked without a weather explanation

Ductless Mini-Splits

What's included

  • Single-zone or multi-zone system design based on room size and usage
  • Wall-mounted indoor unit installation with line-set concealment
  • Outdoor condenser placement on pad or bracket
  • Refrigerant charging, leak test, and performance validation
  • Remote and thermostat walkthrough

When you need it

  • A converted garage, addition, or sunroom that has no ductwork
  • One bedroom that's always too hot or too cold regardless of the main system
  • You want zone control without running new ducts through an older home
  • You need both heating and cooling in a single unit for a guest space

Seasonal Maintenance Plans

What's included

  • Spring cooling tune-up — coil cleaning, refrigerant check, capacitor test, thermostat calibration
  • Fall heating tune-up — burn chamber inspection, flame sensor cleaning, igniter test, airflow check
  • Priority scheduling ahead of extreme weather — members go first
  • System history log so any future technician knows what's been done and when
  • No after-hours surcharge when you need an off-clock visit

When you need it

  • You'd rather catch a failing capacitor in April than on the first 90-degree day
  • Your system is past warranty and you want to extend its life
  • You're tired of calling three companies every season to book a tune-up
  • You want one company that knows your equipment history end to end

Air Quality & Filtration

What's included

  • Air quality assessment — humidity, particulate, and ventilation evaluation
  • Media filter upgrade sized to your system's airflow capacity
  • Whole-home purifier or UV light installation where ductwork allows
  • Humidifier or dehumidifier integration for seasonal balance
  • Filter replacement schedule added to your membership plan

When you need it

  • You notice dust accumulating quickly on surfaces within days of cleaning
  • Someone in the household has seasonal allergies or asthma triggered indoors
  • Your home feels humid in summer or dry and static-prone in winter
  • You've upgraded to a tighter home and ventilation hasn't kept up

Sample Work

Project vignettes that show how we approach common HVAC scenarios on Long Island. Each one is a sample project — the kind of work the club handles every season.

Sample Project

Full Central Air Swap

A 15-year-old condenser replaced with a properly sized modern unit. Old pad cracked — new concrete pad poured, line set rerouted for shorter run, and thermostat upgraded to a programmable model.

Sample Project

Furnace Ignition Repair

No-heat call on a cold morning. Flame sensor had carbon buildup — cleaned and tested. Igniter was borderline, replaced preemptively. System fired up on the first cycle and the homeowner stayed warm.

Sample Project

Ductless Mini-Split for Garage Workshop

A converted garage needed both heat and cooling without running ducts. Single-zone ductless system installed — indoor unit high on the wall, outdoor condenser on a bracket beside the garage. Heating and cooling in one unit.

Sample Project

Bi-Seasonal Membership Tune-Up

A member's annual spring and fall visit. Spring: coil cleaned, refrigerant topped, capacitor tested. Fall: burn chamber inspected, flame sensor cleaned, airflow verified. Two visits, zero emergency calls that year.

Sample Project

Whole-Home Air Quality Upgrade

A homeowner with persistent allergies. Upgraded from a standard 1-inch filter to a 4-inch media filter and added a UV light in the return duct. Dust accumulation dropped noticeably within the first month.

Sample Project

Member Priority Heat Restore

Heat went out on a January night. Member called at 7 AM — technician dispatched first. Cracked heat exchanger identified, temporary heat arranged, and a new furnace was installed within 48 hours. Member went to the front of the line.

DRN Sample project

The clog that came back three times — not anymore

Kitchen drain had been slow for a week, cleared for a day or two after a store-bought snake, then backed right up again. Same story every time.

We ran the cable past where a countertop snake ever reaches, found the actual blockage below the trap, and cleared the line clean. Tested with a full sink of water, twice, before we called it done.

WHT Sample project

No hot water on a Tuesday, hot water again by Tuesday night

Old tank was well past its years and rusting at the base — not something worth patching again. Pulled it, set a properly sized replacement, hooked up and tested the same visit.

Old unit left with us, not left at the curb for somebody else to deal with.

LEAK Sample project

A leak that showed up on the ceiling, not where it started

Water bill had climbed for two months with nothing obviously wrong. Traced it electronically to one fitting well off from where the ceiling stain actually was.

Opened up exactly that spot, fixed the fitting, and pressure tested before closing it back up — not a wall's worth of guessing.

FIX Sample project

A kitchen faucet that finally shuts off all the way

Old faucet had been dripping for months and the handle barely turned. Pulled it, set a new one level and sealed, ran it at full pressure hot and cold before packing up.

SWR Sample project

A camera down the line instead of a trench across the yard

Two drains backing up at the same time usually means the main line, not just one fixture. Camera found a cracked section about a dozen feet out.

Cleared and relined from the existing access point — the lawn never got touched.

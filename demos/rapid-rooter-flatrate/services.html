What we fix, and what it includes

Every job below gets quoted the same way: over the phone, before anyone drives out, written on the ticket. Nothing here runs by the hour, because we don't bill that way.

DRN

Drain cleaning

Included: Snake and auger clearing for kitchen, bath, and laundry drains, plus main lines when that's where the clog lives. A camera check if the same clog keeps coming back, so we're fixing the actual problem instead of guessing at it again next month.

When you need it: Water backing up in a sink or tub, a drain that gurgles when another fixture runs, a toilet that's slow even right after plunging, or the third clog this year in the same spot.

WHT

Water heater repair and install

Included: Diagnosing a unit that's not heating, leaking, or making noise, and repairing what can actually be repaired. When a tank's done, we pull the old one, set the new one, and have it hooked up and tested the same visit.

When you need it: No hot water, hot water that runs out fast, a tank that's rusting or dripping at the base, or a unit old enough that parts are getting hard to find.

LEAK

Leak detection and repair

Included: Finding a leak without opening every wall to look for it, using equipment built for that job, then fixing what's actually leaking instead of just where the water showed up.

When you need it: A water bill that jumped for no clear reason, a damp spot on a ceiling or floor, a hissing sound behind a wall, or water somewhere nothing should be running.

FIX

Fixture install

Included: Faucets, sinks, toilets, tub and shower valves, and garbage disposals. Swapping out what's failing or putting in something new, set level, sealed right, and tested before we leave.

When you need it: A fixture that's cracked, a drip that won't quit, parts that aren't worth chasing on something old, or a remodel that needs the plumbing hooked up.

SWR

Sewer line service

Included: Camera inspection of the main line, clearing whatever's blocking it, and repair for a line that's cracked, bellied, or full of roots.

When you need it: Multiple drains backing up at the same time, a sewage smell in the yard or basement, or drainage that's slow across the whole house instead of just one fixture.

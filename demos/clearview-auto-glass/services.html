Every job starts with the same phone call.

Tell us the year, make, and model, and where the damage is. We'll tell you honestly whether it's a repair or a replacement, give you a price, and set a time to come to you — five things we handle, laid out below.

Windshield Replacement

The most common call we get, and the one people put off longest.

What's included
  • Old glass cut out and removed clean, pinch-weld checked for rust or damage
  • New glass set with a full bead of urethane, seated to factory reveal gaps
  • Full safe-drive-away wait before the car leaves
  • Camera or sensor recalibration if your vehicle needs it
When you need it

Cracks longer than a dollar bill, damage anywhere in the driver's direct sightline, cracks that reach the edge of the glass, or more chips than we can reasonably repair one by one. Not sure which side of that line you're on? Describe it on the phone and we'll tell you straight.

Chip Repair

The five-minute fix that skips the whole windshield.

What's included
  • Loose glass cleared and the chip dried out
  • Clear resin injected into the break and cured
  • Polished flush so it doesn't catch light or a wiper blade
When you need it

Chips and short cracks smaller than a quarter, away from the edge of the glass and out of your direct sightline. If it's spread past that, or it's been open to weather a while, we'll say so and talk replacement instead — we won't repair something that isn't going to hold.

Side & Rear Glass

Door glass, quarter glass, and the tempered back window — different glass, different break, same driveway visit.

What's included
  • Old glass and tempered fragments cleared from the door or frame
  • New glass set in the channel or frame and tested through its full range of motion
  • Weatherstripping and trim reset so it seals like it did before
When you need it

A broken door or rear window — a break-in, a backed-into branch, or a regulator that finally gave out and dropped the glass in the door. Tempered glass breaks into small rounded pieces, not shards, so it's usually safe to drive on in the meantime, just slow and careful.

ADAS Recalibration

The camera behind your mirror needs to be told the glass changed.

What's included
  • Recalibration of the forward-facing camera or sensor mounted near the mirror
  • Target-based setup or a road-based drive, whichever your vehicle calls for
  • A check that lane-keep, forward-collision, and adaptive cruise are reading straight again
When you need it

Any newer vehicle with lane-keep assist, automatic braking, or adaptive cruise that gets a new windshield. The camera's aim is set relative to the glass — swap the glass without resetting it, and those systems can read the road a few degrees off.

Mobile Service

We come to the car. You don't come to us.

What's included
  • A tech, the glass, and the tools, at your driveway, office lot, or apartment lot
  • Enough flat, clear space to work around the car safely
  • The same job, same price, whether it happens at your curb or a shop bay
When you need it

Almost every job on this page. If your car can be parked somewhere we can reach it, we'd rather come to you than have you sit in a waiting room.

Still not sure which one you need?

Describe the damage on the phone and we'll tell you straight — repair, replacement, or somewhere in between.

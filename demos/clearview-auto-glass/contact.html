Quick answers

Can a chip actually be repaired, or do I need a whole new windshield?

Usually repairable if it's smaller than a quarter, away from the edge, and outside your direct sightline. Bigger, cracked, or already spreading, and it's a replacement. We'll tell you which on the phone, not after we show up.

Do I need to be there for a mobile appointment?

No. We just need the car somewhere we can reach it and a way to reach you when it's done. A lot of people have us come while they're at work.

Does insurance cover this?

Many comprehensive policies cover glass repair or replacement, sometimes with no deductible for a repair. Check with your insurer — we're glad to write up an invoice you can submit either way.

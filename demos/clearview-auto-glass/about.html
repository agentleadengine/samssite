Why we started coming to the car instead.

ClearView started with one van and a simple idea: most people don't need a whole new windshield, they need someone to look at the chip before it turns into one. A lot of glass shops make more money the more glass they replace. We don't work that way — if a repair holds, we tell you that, even though the replacement pays more.

Every job happens where your car already is. Driveway, office lot, apartment lot — we bring the glass, the resin, the tools, and the calibration equipment for cars that need it, and do the work there instead of asking you to sit in a shop.

We're a mobile operation covering Long Island, NY. No storefront to drive to, no waiting room — just a call, an honest answer about what the glass needs, and a time we can be at your car.

How it works

Three steps, the same for a ten-minute chip or a full windshield.

  1. 1

    Call or text with the damage

    Tell us the year, make, model, and what the glass looks like. A photo over text helps.

  2. 2

    Get a straight answer and a price

    Repair or replacement, priced on the phone, before anyone drives out.

  3. 3

    We come to you

    Driveway, lot, or curb — we bring what the job needs and do it where the car is parked.

What's actually holding your windshield in.

New glass isn't just set in and left. We run a full bead of urethane around the frame, seat the glass into it, and give the adhesive the time it needs to cure before you drive. That bead isn't cosmetic — it's part of what keeps the roof from caving in a rollover and gives the passenger airbag something to push against.

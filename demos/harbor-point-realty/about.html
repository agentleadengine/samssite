One agent, one file, start to finish.

Here's how Harbor Point Realty actually works, and who's on the other end of the phone.

Harbor Point Realty started with a simple complaint. Tom Halloran had watched a neighbor get one number on the porch and a different one three weeks later, and it stuck with him. The office was built around a plain idea: write it down, or don't say it.

Every file gets one person from the first call to the closing table. That's not a slogan, it's how the calendar works. No handoffs mid-search, no new name calling you back on a Tuesday you weren't expecting. Whoever answers the phone is who shows up at the walkthrough.

The office keeps evening hours because most people are working the same hours we are. If you call after we've closed, leave a number. You'll hear back before the next showing goes on the calendar.

Four steps, in this order.

No long questionnaires before we've met, and no listing agreement until you've seen the numbers in writing.

The call

Fifteen minutes on the phone. What you own, where you're headed, and when you need to be there.

The walkthrough

We come to the house, or meet you at the first few properties on your list. Notes taken, photos on the phone.

The written plan

A price range with the recent sales it came from, a prep list, and a calendar. One page, sent by email.

Go to market

Photos, listing, showings, offers. Or the search begins. You hear from us the same day something changes.

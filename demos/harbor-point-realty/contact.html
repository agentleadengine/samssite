A few questions people ask first.

Do I need to sign anything before the first walkthrough?

No. The first call and the first walkthrough don't commit you to anything. Most people are just trying to find out where they stand.

How fast can a house actually get listed?

Once the prep list is done, usually one to two weeks for photos, copy, and the listing to go live. Faster if the house is already showing-ready.

Do you help with rentals, or just sales?

Both. Year leases, short-notice moves, and relocations get the same walkthrough-first approach as a sale.

Still have questions?

Call the office directly. Most things are easier to explain out loud.

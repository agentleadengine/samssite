Five things people call this office about.

Every file starts the same way: a phone call, then a walkthrough. What comes after depends on whether you're leaving, arriving, or just trying to find out where you stand.

Selling your home

We walk the house with you and make two lists: what's worth fixing before photos, and what to leave alone. Then a price range built from homes that recently sold nearby, listing photos and copy, a showing schedule, and a plain-English review of every offer that comes in.

When you need it: you're thinking about listing this season, or want real numbers before you decide anything.

  • Walkthrough & prep list
  • Written price range
  • Listing photos & copy
  • Showing schedule
  • Offer-by-offer review

Buying a home

Tell us the school district, the commute you can live with, and the number you're comfortable at. We line up showings around your work schedule, point out what people miss on a first visit, and sit down with you over the inspection report before you commit to anything.

When you need it: you're actively looking, or want someone lined up before you start.

  • Showings built around your schedule
  • A second set of eyes at every walkthrough
  • Inspection report review
  • No pressure to make an offer

Rentals & relocation

Year leases, short-notice moves, and transfers into the area. We confirm what's actually included, what the landlord expects at signing, and what the building rules say about pets, parking, and guests, so there are no surprises the week you move in.

When you need it: you're relocating for work, your lease is ending, or you're not ready to buy yet.

  • Lease terms confirmed upfront
  • Move-in requirements
  • Pet & parking rules checked
  • Short-notice moves

Listing preparation

A room-by-room punch list before the camera shows up: paint, lighting, clutter, and curb appeal, in the order that actually matters. Handle it yourself, or we can line up cleaners, painters, and a stager and keep the schedule moving.

When you need it: you've decided to sell and want the house ready before it's photographed.

  • Room-by-room punch list
  • Vendor referrals
  • Schedule kept on track
  • Curb appeal pass

Pricing walkthrough

About an hour at the house, then a written summary: what comparable homes nearby recently sold for, what's still sitting on the market, and what that means for the timeline you have in mind. Useful whether you list this spring or in three years.

When you need it: you just want to know where you stand. No listing agreement required.

  • One-hour walkthrough
  • Written comps summary
  • Market timeline read
  • No obligation to list

No listing agreement until you've seen the numbers in writing.

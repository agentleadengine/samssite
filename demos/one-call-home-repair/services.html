Everything that goes on the board.

Five kinds of jobs. One call puts all of them on the same visit. Here’s what’s included in each, and when you’d actually pick up the phone.

Why there’s no price list here: a loose hinge and a full punch list get called into the same line, and the board only works if the number you hear on the phone matches the number on the invoice. Tell us what’s on the list and we’ll give you one before anybody drives out.

Small Repairs

Sticking locks, running toilets, dead outlets, wobbly stair railings, a switch that quit, weatherstripping that quit keeping the weather out. The stuff that’s a five-minute fix once someone’s actually standing there with the right bit.

Most of these get bundled onto a longer list, but a single small repair is still worth a call on its own if it’s been bothering you.

When you need it

When it’s too small to justify calling around for, but you notice it every single time you walk past.

Bundle it with anything else on your list and it barely adds to the visit.

Furniture Assembly

Flat-pack dressers, bed frames, wardrobes, desks, cribs. Built with a torque driver instead of just the allen key in the box, so the joints are tight in month six the same as day one.

Doors and drawers get aligned and checked before we call it done, not left to settle on their own over the first few weeks.

When you need it

Right after delivery, while the box is still sitting in the living room.

Or the flat-pack that’s been leaning against the wall for two months. Either way, most single pieces go together in under an hour.

TV & Shelf Mounting

TVs anchored into studs, or into the right hardware when there’s no stud where you actually want the TV. Floating shelves rated for what’s going on them, not just picture frames.

Cable runs get tucked into the wall or channeled along the trim. Nothing hangs off the back or gets zip-tied to the baseboard.

When you need it

New TV, a shelf that needs to hold more weight than it looks like, or a mount someone else put up that’s started to lean.

Door & Window Fixes

Doors that stick in summer and stop latching in winter, hinges re-set and re-shimmed, storm door closers adjusted. Window sashes that won’t hold themselves up, screens popped back into the track, weatherstripping replaced where the draft gets in.

When you need it

When you’re shouldering a door open every day, or propping a window with a paint can because the balance gave out.

Punch-List Projects

The move-in list. The move-out list. The list that’s been on the fridge since spring. Every line gets read back to you before we hang up, worked in the order that actually makes sense, and checked off in front of you before we leave.

When you need it

When it isn’t one job, it’s twelve, and calling twelve different people for twelve small things is worse than just living with all of it. That’s exactly what this service is built for.

Ready to read us the list?

Call and go through it, or leave it on the contact page. Either way, it goes on the board.

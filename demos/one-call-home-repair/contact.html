A few quick answers

Can you really clear my whole list in one visit?

Most of them, yes. If something on the list needs a part we don’t carry or a permit, we’ll tell you that on the call, not after we’ve already driven out.

Do I need to know exactly what’s wrong before I call?

No. Tell us what you’re seeing, we’ll ask a couple questions, and it goes on the board. A photo texted over helps if it’s easier to show than explain.

What’s not a good fit for One Call?

Anything behind an electrical panel, on a gas line, or needing a permit. We’ll say so on the call and point you to someone licensed for it instead of taking it on anyway.

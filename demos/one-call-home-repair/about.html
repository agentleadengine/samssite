How One Call actually works.

The short version and the long version, from the guy who answers the phone.

I’m Joe. I answer the phone, I write the board, and most days I’m the one who shows up. One Call started because I got tired of watching people call five different guys for five small things, half of whom never called back.

The board is just a whiteboard, if you want the truth. Every job that gets called in goes up as its own line, and it doesn’t come off until it’s actually done. Doesn’t matter if it’s a small fix or a full punch list. It’s a line either way, and it gets worked in order.

I turn down more than people expect. Anything behind an electrical panel, on a gas line, or needing a permit gets handed to someone licensed for it, not stretched to fit. What’s left is genuinely mine to fix, and I’d rather clear twelve of those in a day than take on one job I had no business starting.

How the board actually runs

Same three words as every visit. Here’s what each one means on our end.

Called In

Nothing waits for later

Whoever answers writes down every line while you’re still on the phone. It goes on the board that day, not into a stack of sticky notes.

Dispatched

Only what’s actually happening

The board only holds jobs with a real window attached. If it isn’t scheduled, it isn’t sitting there pretending to be.

Cleared

You see it come off the list

A line doesn’t get crossed off because the visit ended. It comes off because you watched it get finished.

The difference is in the walls.

Old houses aren't just older — they're built differently. Plaster and lath instead of drywall. Knob-and-tube instead of romex. Cloth-wrapped wire instead of thermoplastic. Working inside them takes a specific kind of care.

  • We fish cable through plaster walls without gutting rooms — small access points, careful patching.

  • We recognize the difference between knob-and-tube that's safe to leave and wiring that needs to go.

  • We use device plates and trim that match the period — not modern plastic on a 1920s wall.

  • We load-check your panel before adding anything — no guessing whether it can handle a charger.

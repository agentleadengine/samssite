Services

Five things we do for older homes. Each one starts with understanding what's already in the walls.

Service 01

Panel Upgrades

If your panel still has round fuses or fewer than 100 amps of capacity, it's past time. Modern households pull more power than these systems were built to deliver — chargers, AC units, kitchen appliances all at once.

We replace fuse boxes and aging breaker panels with properly sized modern panels. That means a real load calculation first, not a guess.

  • 60-amp and 100-amp panel replacement
  • Fuse box to breaker panel conversion
  • Sub-panel installation for additions or garages
  • Whole-house surge protection
  • Grounding and bonding upgrades

When you need it: flickering lights, warm panel, tripping breakers, or adding any major appliance.

Service 02

EV Charger Installation

Charging an electric vehicle at home needs a dedicated circuit — sometimes a panel upgrade first. We figure out what your panel can handle before we install anything.

  • Level 2 (240V) charger installation
  • Dedicated 40-amp or 50-amp circuit
  • Panel capacity assessment first
  • Hardwired and plug-in charger options
  • Outdoor-rated installation for driveway charging

When you need it: you bought or leased an EV and want overnight home charging.

Service 03

Lighting Design & Install

Old ceilings weren't built for recessed lights, and old switch boxes don't fit modern dimmers. We install lighting that works with what you have — and looks like it belongs there.

  • Recessed lighting in plaster ceilings (shallow-depth fixtures)
  • Ceiling fixture and chandelier installation
  • Three-way and smart switch upgrades
  • Exterior and landscape lighting
  • Period-appropriate fixture selection guidance

When you need it: dark rooms, outdated fixtures, or you're renovating without changing the ceiling height.

Service 04

Troubleshooting & Repair

Outlet not working. Switch hot to the touch. Breaker that keeps tripping. These are symptoms, not diagnoses. We trace the actual cause — loose connection, degraded wire, overloaded circuit — and fix it at the source.

  • Dead outlet and switch diagnosis
  • Flickering light investigation
  • Breaker tripping root-cause analysis
  • Warm switch or receptacle repair
  • Aged wire assessment (knob-and-tube, cloth NMC)

When you need it: anything electrical that isn't working right or feels unsafe.

Service 05

Generator Hookups

Long Island loses power. A transfer switch and generator hookup means your fridge, heat, and lights stay on — without running extension cords through windows.

  • Manual transfer switch installation
  • Automatic transfer switch (ATS) wiring
  • Portable generator inlet boxes
  • Standby generator connection
  • Critical circuit selection (what to power first)

When you need it: you've lost power more than once or you're adding a standby unit.

Not sure which service you need?

Call and describe what's going on. We'll tell you whether it's a panel issue, a circuit issue, or something simpler.

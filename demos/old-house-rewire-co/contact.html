Questions we hear.

  • Do I need to replace all my knob-and-tube wiring?

    Not necessarily. Knob-and-tube that's intact, unmodified, and not buried in insulation can be safe to leave in place. The problem is when it's been spliced into with modern wire, covered in attic insulation, or the insulation on the conductors has cracked. We assess what you actually have and tell you what needs to go and what can stay.

  • Can you install a charger without upgrading my panel?

    It depends on your panel's capacity and what else is on it. A 100-amp panel can often support a 40-amp charger circuit if there's headroom. A 60-amp panel usually can't. We do a load calculation before recommending anything — we won't sell you a panel upgrade if a dedicated circuit is all you need.

  • Will you have to cut holes in my walls?

    Some access cuts are usually necessary to fish cable through plaster, but we keep them small and strategic — typically behind baseboards or in cabinet backs where they won't be visible. We patch what we cut. The goal is to leave your walls looking like we were never there, except for the new outlets and switches.

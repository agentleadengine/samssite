Six sample projects

Illustrated scenes built to show the standard, not photos of a real job site. Every one of these is the kind of thing that shows up on a normal list.

Sample project

Front door, rehung and planed

Dragging at the bottom corner and catching on the frame all summer. Off the hinges, planed to fit, rehung level – swings clean in any weather now.

Sample project

Baseboard, replaced clean

A water-swollen section had split at the seam. Cut out, replaced, caulked and painted to match – no gap where the wall meets the floor.

Sample project

TV mount, dead level

Off the dresser and onto the stud, checked side to side, cord run straight down to a hidden plate. Weight-tested before we packed up.

Sample project

Flat-pack shelving, squared up

Boxed since the move. Built square, checked with the level at every shelf, anchored to the wall so it can carry real weight.

Sample project

Sticking window, freed up

Painted shut and propped with a stick to stay open. Sash freed, balance reset, trim cleaned up – opens and holds on its own again.

Sample project

Full punch list, walked and cleared

Nine items handed over before closing. Taken in one visit, walked together at the end, every line checked off in front of the owner.

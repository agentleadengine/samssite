A few questions before you call

Do I need to be home for the whole visit?

Not always. If you can’t be there, a lockbox code or a key left with a neighbor works fine – just mention it on the call. You’ll get a text with photos once the list’s done.

What if something on my list needs a permit or a licensed trade?

I’ll say so on the call, before anything’s booked. Anything past small repair work – gas lines, panel work, structural changes – gets referred to someone licensed for it instead of me taking a swing at it.

How does pricing work?

You hear the rate before I start, based on what you tell me on the call or by text. Materials are at cost. If something on-site changes the scope, you hear that before I keep going – not after, on the bill.

Five things, done right

No job too small to get checked with a level, and no list too long to walk end to end. What’s included and when you actually need it – not a vague “we do it all.”

Small repairs

The stuff that’s minor until it’s been on the list for six months. Drywall dings and nail pops, loose door hinges, sticking cabinet doors, caulk lines gone gray, a trim board that finally came loose. One visit clears most of a normal list.

If a repair turns out to need a permit or a licensed trade, we’ll say so on the spot and point you to who to call, instead of taking a swing at it anyway.

What’s included

Patch and paint touch-up, hardware swaps, hinge adjustment, caulking, minor trim work.

When you need it

Before one small repair turns into three, or ahead of a showing, move-in, or inspection.

Furniture assembly

Flat-pack furniture built square, leveled, and anchored to the wall where tipping is a real risk – dressers, bookshelves, wardrobes. Every panel checked before the last screw goes in, not after.

We break down and haul the boxes when we’re done. If a piece arrives with a warped panel or missing hardware, you’ll hear it from us before assembly starts, not halfway through.

What’s included

Assembly, wall anchoring for tip-prone pieces, leveling, packaging removal.

When you need it

New furniture that’s been sitting in the box, or a piece wobbly enough you don’t trust it around kids or pets.

TV & shelf mounting

Mounted into the stud, checked level side to side, cable run tucked so the wall doesn’t look like a server room. Floating shelves get the same treatment – rated for what you’re actually putting on them.

We test the mount with real weight before we leave. If the studs don’t line up where you want the TV and the wall needs a backer board, we’ll say so and price it straight.

What’s included

Bracket mounting, in-wall or surface cable management, floating shelf install, weight testing.

When you need it

A TV that’s been leaning on the dresser since you moved in, or shelves that need to actually hold books.

Doors & windows

Doors that stick in the summer, hinges that scream, deadbolts that don’t line up, windows that won’t stay up on their own anymore. Most of it is planing, adjusting, or a hardware swap – not a full replacement.

Screens and storm windows get a look while we’re there if you want a second set of eyes, even if that wasn’t on the original list.

What’s included

Planing and rehanging, hinge and lock adjustment, deadbolt and handle swaps, sash and balance repair.

When you need it

A door that’s stuck since the weather turned, or a window that needs two hands to hold up.

Punch-list projects

The list that’s been sitting since you moved in, or the one your agent handed you before closing. We take the whole thing in one visit where we can, and tell you upfront which items need a second trip for parts.

You get a walk-through at the end. Every item gets pointed at and confirmed, not just a text that says “all set.”

What’s included

Multi-item repair lists, move-in and move-out lists, pre-listing touch-ups.

When you need it

Before a closing, a new tenant, or just the day you decide the list has won long enough.

Not sure which of these your list needs?

Call and describe it, or text a couple of photos. Most lists are a mix of two or three of these – that’s normal, and it’s still one visit.

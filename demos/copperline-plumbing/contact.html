Quick answers

Do you handle emergencies?

Copperline is built around scheduled repipe and remodel work, not a dispatch queue. If it's an active flood, call an emergency service first. If it's a leak or a job that can wait a day or two for the right fix, that's exactly what we're set up for.

Can you match fixtures to what our designer picked?

Yes — bring the spec sheet or the finish sample and we'll fit to it, not to whatever's closest on the truck.

How long does a full repipe take?

Depends on the house, but we walk it first and give you an actual schedule instead of a guess. Most homes are a matter of days, not weeks, once the plan's set.

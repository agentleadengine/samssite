The plan gets made before the truck gets loaded.

Most plumbing calls get treated the same way: someone rushes over, patches what's broken, and leaves. That works fine for a running toilet. It doesn't work for a full repipe or a bathroom that's actually being remodeled, where the pipe work is going to be looked at, lived with, and sometimes left exposed on purpose. Copperline exists for that second kind of job.

The work starts with a walkthrough, not a phone guess. We look at the pipe, the fixture, or the wall, then lay out an actual plan — what gets opened, what stays closed, and what day the crew shows up. The job gets scheduled like a remodel, because that's what it is.

If what you need is a fast patch on an active leak tonight, say so honestly — that's not the job Copperline is built around, and we'd rather tell you that upfront than take the call and make you wait on a walkthrough. For everything on a plan — repipes, remodel fixtures, a drain that keeps coming back — that's exactly the work we want.

— Tom Vance, Copperline Plumbing & Heating

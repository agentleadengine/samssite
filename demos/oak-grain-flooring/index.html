How a refinish actually runs.

An average refinish runs three to five days depending on square footage and dry time. Here's the order it happens in, so you know when to plan around it.

Day 1

Walk the floor

Thickness, cupping, gaps, and old repairs get checked in person — then an honest call on full sand versus recoat, and a written estimate the same day or the next.

Days 2–3

Prep and sand

Furniture out, doorways sealed in plastic, vents covered. Rough cut, medium, then fine, with the edger and buffer working the perimeter so nothing shows a line.

Days 3–4

Stain and seal

Samples get approved first. Stain goes on and comes off by hand, then sealer, then poly coats with a light abrasive pass in between.

Day 5+

Cure and move back

Socks on the floor after a day, furniture back after a few days, rugs last. We walk the whole floor with you at the end.

Wood floors, start to finish.

Install, sand, stain, seal, and repair. No carpet, no tile, no laminate — if it's a wood floor in a Long Island house, it's the work.

Sanding and refinishing

Screen and recoat

Flat comes before shiny.

The idea behind Oak & Grain, and how the work actually gets done.

Oak & Grain started with a simple frustration: too many refinished floors look great in photos and uneven underfoot, because the sanding got rushed and the subfloor never got checked. Flat comes before shiny. That's the whole idea.

Every job starts with a walk-through, not a guess over the phone. We check the subfloor, the thickness of what's already down, and how the house sits before we tell you what it needs. If a recoat will do, we say so — even though a full sand costs more.

We work across Long Island: occupied homes, empty houses before a move-in, and small commercial spaces after hours when the business is closed. Dust containment and a water-based finish mean most people stay in the house while we work. When that's not realistic for a job, we say that too, before you've committed to anything.

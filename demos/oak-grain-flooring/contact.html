A few things people ask first.

Recoat or full sand — how do I know which one I need?

If the color and sheen are worn but the wood underneath is still clean, a screen and recoat is usually enough — one day of work, low dust, and a lot less than a full refinish. If there's cupping, deep scratches, or old repairs, it's a full sand. Call or text a photo and we can usually tell you which one you're looking at.

Do I have to move out during a refinish?

Most of the time, no. Dust containment and a water-based, low-odor finish let most households stay in the house while we work. Some jobs genuinely call for it — we'll tell you plainly if yours is one of them, before you've committed to anything.

How long until furniture goes back?

Socks on the floor after about a day. Furniture back after a few days. Rugs last of all. You'll get the exact schedule for your finish before we start, and we walk the floor with you at the end.

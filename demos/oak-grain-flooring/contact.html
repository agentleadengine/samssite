A few common questions.

How do I know if I need a recoat or a full sand?

If the color and sheen are worn but the wood underneath is still clean, a screen and recoat is usually enough: one day of work, low dust, and a lot less than a full refinish. If there's cupping, deep scratches, or old repairs, it's a full sand. Call or text a photo and we can usually tell you which one you're looking at.

Do I have to move out during the work?

Most of the time, no. Dust containment and a water-based, low-odor finish let most households stay in the house while we work. Some jobs genuinely call for it, and we'll tell you plainly if yours is one of them before you've committed to anything.

How long does a job take?

An average refinish runs three to five days depending on square footage and dry time. New installs vary with how long the material needs to acclimate. You'll get the exact schedule at the walkthrough, not a guess over the phone.

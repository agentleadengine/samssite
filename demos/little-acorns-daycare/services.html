Six rooms, sized honestly by age

One hallway, small rooms, and a group size that shrinks the younger the room gets. Children move up when they're ready, not on their birthday, and you'll meet the next teacher weeks before the switch.

Infant Room

6 weeks - 18 months

Six babies, two teachers

  • Your feeding and nap schedule, followed exactly.
  • Every bottle, diaper, and nap written down and handed to you at pickup.
  • A lot of floor time, songs, and being held.

Good fit if you need care starting as early as 6 weeks and want a room small enough that someone can hold your baby the moment they need it.

Toddler Room

18 months - 2.5 years

Up to nine children, two teachers

  • Two outdoor blocks a day, plus water and sand play.
  • Steady help with the hard parts: sharing, waiting, using words.
  • Walking turning into climbing, and first real opinions.

Good fit if your walker is turning into a climber with opinions and needs room to test both safely.

Preschool

2.5 - 3.5 years

Up to twelve children, two teachers

  • Letters and numbers show up inside real projects: cooking, planting, building.
  • Potty training handled as a team, at your child's pace, not a calendar's.
  • Longer stretches of independent play and problem-solving.

Good fit if you want structure that doesn't feel like a worksheet yet, and a room patient with potty training.

Pre-K

3.5 - 5 years

Up to fourteen children, two teachers

  • Name writing, sounding out words, and counting past 20.
  • Practice raising a hand and sitting through a story to the end.
  • Kindergarten readiness without an all-worksheet day.

Good fit if kindergarten is a year or two away and you want your kid ready without losing the fun of being four.

Before & After Care

All ages

Mixed ages, small groups

  • Drop off from 7:00 am or stay until 6:00.
  • Quiet corners, books and puzzles, one more turn outside.
  • A real snack, not a granola bar out of a box.

Good fit if your school day doesn't line up with your work day.

Summer Weeks

June - August

Mixed ages, grouped by week

  • Themed weeks with sprinklers and water play.
  • A garden bed per room and a visiting library cart.
  • Sign up by the week if you only need a few.

Good fit if you only need coverage for part of the summer, not the whole thing.

Not sure which room fits?

Call and describe your schedule. We'll tell you honestly which room fits and when a spot opens.

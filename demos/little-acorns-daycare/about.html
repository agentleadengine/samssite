The story behind the hallway

Little Acorns started in Dana Ferro's living room, watching two kids from down the block after their regular sitter moved away. Word travels fast on a street like that. The living room turned into a rented room, then a rented building, then the hallway of six rooms it is today.

What never changed is the reason those first two families kept coming back: they could picture exactly where their kid was napping, what they ate for lunch, and who was holding them when they cried. That's still the standard here. The infant room stays small on purpose, meals come out of our own kitchen instead of a box, and a photo goes out most afternoons before pickup, not because an app told us to, but because a parent asked for it once and nobody ever stopped.

A typical week starts with a tour, twenty minutes, weekdays at 9:30 or 1:30, and you're welcome to bring your child. From there it's one flat weekly rate covering meals, supplies, and diapers, hours you can count on, and a spot on the list if your room of choice is full. We'll tell you honestly where you'd land before you decide anything.

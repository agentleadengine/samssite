Our Story

How a small storefront ended up roasting its own coffee, and why we've kept it that way.

Driftwood started because the nearest good cup of coffee was a twenty-minute drive, and that never made sense for a stretch of Long Island with this many people walking past every morning. We found a small storefront, put a roaster in the back, and started small.

We buy green coffee in small amounts and roast it ourselves three mornings a week, in batches you could carry under one arm. Whatever's freshest goes on the board. When something better comes along, we switch to that instead.

The counter runs the same way. Espresso gets pulled to order, pour-over gets brewed by hand, and nobody's rushing you out for nursing one coffee through a whole afternoon.

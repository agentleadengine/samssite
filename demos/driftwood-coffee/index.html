Three mornings, one drum.

Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, in batches of about twelve pounds. Nothing about it is automated — someone stands at the drum, listens for the first crack, and pulls it when it's ready, not when a timer says so. You can usually smell it from the sidewalk before you see it.

Why it tastes different

Nothing here is complicated. We buy green coffee in small amounts, roast it ourselves, and use it while it still tastes like something.

  • We buy small Green coffee comes in by the sack, not the pallet, so we can switch to something better the moment it shows up.
  • Dated, not guessed Every bag gets the day it was roasted stamped on the back — not a best-by guess pulled from a chart.
  • Pulled twice if it's off Espresso gets dialed in every morning and checked again after lunch. If a shot's wrong, we pull it again — you don't pay twice.

Need something more than a cup?

Call for large orders, catering carafes, or wholesale bags for the office. Someone behind the counter picks up — not a call tree.

Quick questions

Do I need to order ahead?

No, we're a walk-in counter. Call ahead only if you need a large order, catering carafes, or a standing wholesale account.

Can I bring my own cup?

Yes — bring a clean cup or growler and take fifty cents off any drink.

Do you sell beans to take home?

Yes. 12 oz retail bags are on the board, ground for free if you ask. Offices and restaurants can call about a standing wholesale order.

How we work

A few things we do differently, because they matter more to the dogs than they do to the schedule.

  • One dog on the grooming table at a time.
  • No cage dryers, ever. Everything is hand-dried and brushed out.
  • Oatmeal and fragrance-free shampoo on hand for itchy or sensitive skin.
  • Anxious and senior dogs can be split into two shorter appointments.
  • Coat is matted? We call and talk through the options before anything gets shaved.
  • Every dog goes home the same day, every time.

Ready to get your dog on the schedule?

Call and tell us your dog's breed, coat, and what you want off. Most calls end with a price and an open time in about two minutes.

Bath & Brush

Between full grooms, before a family visit, or any time your dog just needs to smell better and feel better.

Everything in the bath, plus the cut you want: breed trim, puppy cut, teddy face, or short and tidy for summer. Scissored finish on the face and feet.

Bath, hand-dry, breed or custom cut, scissor-finished face and feet, nails, ears.

How a visit goes

No mystery, no all-day stay. Here's the whole thing start to finish.

  1. You call and tell us about your dog

    Breed, coat, last groom, and anything that makes them nervous: dryers, clippers, having their feet held.

  2. Drop off at your appointment time

    We book with room between dogs, so nobody sits around waiting for their turn.

  3. Bath, dry, and cut

    Hand-dried and hand-finished, with a break whenever your dog needs one. Senior dogs get a slower pace.

  4. We text you when they're ready

    Usually two to three hours later. At pickup we'll tell you what we saw on the skin, ears, and nails.

Final price depends on size, coat type, and how much work the coat needs. Call and describe your dog and we'll give you the number before you book.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe your dog's breed and coat on the phone and we'll point you to the right groom, or the right combination.

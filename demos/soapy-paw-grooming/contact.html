A few common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Baths and full grooms are by appointment. Nail trims are walk-in, Tuesday through Friday before 11:00 am, no booking needed.

What if my dog's coat is matted?

We call you before anything gets shaved and talk through the options together. We won't shave a mat without asking first.

How long does a groom take?

Usually two to three hours, depending on size and coat. We text the moment your dog is ready for pickup.

Still have a question?

Call or text and ask. Most calls end with a price and an open time in about two minutes.

A few things people ask

Q.Do you work with my contractor, or do I need to find one?

Either. Most clients already have someone in mind by the time the drawings are done; if not, we can point you toward a few we've worked well with before.

Q.How long does it take to get through permitting?

Depends on the town, but a clean, complete set moves faster than one that gets sent back with questions. That's most of what the drawings are for.

Q.Can you tell me if an addition is even allowed before I pay for a full design?

That's exactly what a feasibility study is for — a short, flat-fee read on setbacks and zoning before you commit to anything bigger.

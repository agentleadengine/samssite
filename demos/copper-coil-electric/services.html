What we get called for

Six things cover most of it. If your problem isn't on this list, call anyway. We'll tell you straight whether it's ours to fix.

Read it top to bottom, the way it's taped inside your own panel door: odd numbers down the left leg.

01 MAIN‑200

Panel upgrades & service changes

What's included. Old fuse box, or a 100-amp panel with no open slots left? We swap in a 200-amp service, land every circuit properly, and leave a typed directory taped inside the door so you know what each breaker actually controls.

When you need it. The lights dim as soon as the AC or a big appliance kicks on, you're out of room for a new circuit, or the panel is older than everything else running through it.

03 EV‑CHG

EV charger installation

What's included. A Level 2 charger on its own 240-volt circuit, run from the panel to the garage or the driveway side of the house.

When you need it. We check the load on your existing service first. If there's room, it's a single new circuit. If there isn't, we'll tell you honestly before any work starts, so the charger isn't fighting the dryer at 7pm.

05 TRBL‑DX

Troubleshooting & repair

What's included. Breakers that trip, outlets that went dead, lights that flicker when the AC kicks on, a switch that buzzes. We trace the fault back to the cause instead of swapping parts and hoping it sticks.

When you need it. Anything that's stopped working, or that only acts up sometimes. The visit starts at $95, credited toward the repair if you hire us to fix it.

07 LIGHT‑CIR

Lighting

What's included. Recessed cans, under-cabinet strips, dimmers matched to the bulbs so they don't hum, exterior and motion lighting, and ceiling fans hung off a fan-rated box instead of a plastic one.

When you need it. A new room, a kitchen remodel, a dimmer that buzzes, or a fan that wobbles because it was never hung right in the first place.

09 OUT‑GFCI

Outlets & circuits

What's included. GFCI and AFCI protection, dedicated 20-amp kitchen and laundry circuits, weatherproof receptacles outside, and a real outlet in the spot where the phone chargers pile up on the counter.

When you need it. An older home without GFCI protection near water, a kitchen that trips the same breaker every time the microwave and toaster run together, or an outdoor space with no outlet at all.

11 GEN‑SURGE

Generator hookups & surge

What's included. Interlock kits and transfer switches so a portable generator feeds the circuits you picked in advance, plus whole-home surge protection at the panel for everything with a circuit board in it.

When you need it. Before the next outage, not during it. Surge protection is a same-visit add to most panel work.

How the price works

Diagnostic visits start at $95, and that amount comes off the job if you hire us to do the repair. Bigger work, like a panel upgrade or an EV charger, gets one flat number in writing before we begin. Nothing changes once the cover's off unless we call you first.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the problem on the phone and we'll tell you which of these it is, honestly, before anyone drives over.

A few jobs, told straight

No client names and no addresses. Just six jobs that look like what actually comes through the phone, and how each one got fixed. Every scene below is drawn in the shop's own style, not photographed.

Sample project

100-amp fuse box to a 200-amp service

A fuse box with two open circuits left, feeding a house that had since added central air and a finished basement. We swapped in a 200-amp panel, moved every circuit over one at a time so nothing sat dead overnight, and taped a typed directory inside the door when it was done.

Sample project

Level 2 charger, garage side

The service had room once we checked the load against the dryer and the range. One new 240-volt circuit, run through the attic to a garage wall, on its own breaker so it's never competing with anything else in the house.

Sample project

A breaker that only tripped at night

Intermittent faults are the hard ones. This one only tripped once the outside lights and a window AC unit ran together. Traced it to a loose neutral in an outdoor junction box, not the breaker itself, and fixed the connection instead of just swapping the breaker and hoping.

Sample project

Recessed lighting, kitchen remodel

Eight cans on two dimmer-controlled zones, matched to the LED bulbs going in so the dimmers wouldn't hum or flicker at low settings. A fan-rated box went in over the island for a fixture that used to hang off a plain plastic one.

Sample project

GFCI protection, whole kitchen

A 1970s kitchen with two ungrounded outlets near the sink and no GFCI anywhere. We added GFCI protection at the panel for the kitchen and laundry circuits, plus a weatherproof outlet on the deck that never had power at all.

Sample project

Interlock kit and surge protection

A portable generator that used to run extension cords through a cracked window. We installed an interlock kit and a manual transfer switch so it feeds the fridge, the sump pump, and a few lights straight from the panel, plus whole-home surge protection added the same visit.

Something like this at your place?

Call and describe it. Most of the time we can name the likely cause before anyone leaves for your house.

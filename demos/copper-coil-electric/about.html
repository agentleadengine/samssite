The person who shows up is the person who quoted it

Copper Coil Electric is a small Long Island outfit built around one idea: you shouldn't have to guess what's happening behind your own walls.

Dennis Cole started Copper Coil Electric after one too many callbacks to fix somebody else's rushed work. Most of those jobs weren't bad wiring so much as work that got hurried to hit a number that was never realistic in the first place.

The fix was simple: quote the real job, in writing, before anything gets opened up. If something unexpected turns up once the cover's off, the answer is a phone call, not a bigger number at the end.

Drop cloths go down before the work starts and the vacuum comes out before anyone leaves, so a service call doesn't turn into a mess you're still finding weeks later. Every panel gets labeled before the van pulls away, because a directory that only makes sense to the electrician who wrote it isn't much of a directory. Small things. They're also what people mention when they call back for the next job.

Service area

Copper Coil works across Long Island, NY: houses, condos, and small storefronts. Not sure you're in range? Call and ask. It takes ten seconds, and you'll get a straight answer.

What comes out of the oven

Small batches, all day. If something sells out early, we won't fake it with yesterday's. We'll just tell you what's still good.

Daily breads

Country sourdough with an overnight ferment, seeded rye, soft potato rolls, and baguettes that come out twice a day.

When you need it: every day we're open

Challah every Friday, out by late morning. Call ahead if you need two, it moves fast.

Morning pastry

Butter croissants, almond croissants, cinnamon knots, and a fruit danish that changes with what's actually in season.

When you need it: best odds before nine

The case is fullest first thing and thins out from there through the morning.

Cakes to order

Birthdays, showers, retirements, the last day of school. Vanilla, chocolate, or lemon, buttercream or ganache, and yes, we'll write on it.

When you need it: 3 days' notice, most sizes

A week's notice if it's tiered. Call and we'll tell you if a shorter window is possible.

Pies & seasonal

Apple and blueberry through the summer, pumpkin and pecan once the weather turns. Whole pies to take home or a slice at the counter with coffee.

When you need it: most days, in season

For a holiday table, call a few days out so we know how many to set aside.

Coffee & the counter

Espresso, drip, and a couple of seats by the window.

When you need it: anytime we're open

No line for coffee alone. Bring the paper, sit down with a knot, let the morning go slowly for once.

Office trays & catering

Pastry platters, sliced bread and roll boxes, and cookie trays for meetings and open houses.

When you need it: 1 day's notice

Tell us the headcount when you order. We bake with nuts, wheat, and dairy in one small kitchen, so mention allergies, though we can't promise anything is free of them.

Ready to order?

Cakes and trays are handled by phone, not online.

A few questions people ask

How far ahead do I need to order a cake?

Three days for most sizes, a week if it's tiered. Call and we'll tell you if a shorter window is possible.

Can you make something nut-free or dairy-free?

We can't promise it. Everything comes out of one small kitchen that handles nuts, wheat, and dairy every day, so we can't guarantee anything is free of them, even if the recipe itself doesn't call for one. Tell us when you order and we'll talk it through.

Do you deliver?

No, pickup only, at the counter. We'll have your order ready at the time we agree on when you call.

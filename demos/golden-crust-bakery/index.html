What four in the morning gets you

Nothing here is complicated. It's flour, water, salt, butter, and time, handled by people who show up while it's still dark out.

4:00 AM

Dough that started the night before comes out, gets shaped, and goes into the oven in the day's first wave.

6:30 AM

Doors open Tuesday through Friday, 7:00 on weekends. Sourdough, baguettes, and rolls are already out; croissants and danish are close behind.

Late morning

A second bake finishes what the case needs to make it through the afternoon. Fridays, that's when the challah comes out.

At close

Whatever's left doesn't go back out tomorrow. We'd rather tell you it's gone than sell you yesterday's.

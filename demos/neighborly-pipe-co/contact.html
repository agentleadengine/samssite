Quick answers

Is the price you quote on the phone the final price?

Yes — that's the whole idea. We ask enough questions on the phone to size the job right, and that number goes on the ticket. If something changes once we're inside a wall, we stop and confirm the new number with you before doing anything else.

Do you handle after-hours emergencies?

Not as a dispatch service. We run scheduled visits, Monday through Saturday, so the person who quotes the job is the one who shows up to do it. If you've got an active flood, shut the water off at the main and call an emergency line first — for anything that can hold until the next opening, that's exactly what we're built for.

What if the job turns out bigger than expected once you're inside?

We stop, explain what we're actually looking at, and give you the new number before doing another thing. You're never signing up for a blank check.

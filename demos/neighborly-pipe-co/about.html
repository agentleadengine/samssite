A shop built around one promise.

Not a franchise, not a dispatch center — a small plumbing shop that decided the price should never be a surprise, and built everything else around holding to that.

How it started

Danny Reyes started The Neighborly Pipe Co. on the idea that a plumbing bill shouldn't be a surprise. Say the price out loud on the phone, write it on the ticket, and stick to it once the truck's in the driveway. Everything else about how the shop runs grew out of that one rule.

Call and you're usually talking to whoever ends up doing the work. We ask what's going on, quote the flat rate on that same call, and set a time that works for your house — not a four-hour window you have to guard all day. What's quoted on the phone is what's on the ticket when the job's done.

The shop covers Long Island, NY, one street and one ticket at a time. If a job turns out to be bigger than a phone call can size up, we say so before opening a wall, not after.

How a call becomes a job

  1. Call or text and describe what's going on.
  2. Get a flat price before anything's scheduled.
  3. We show up, do the work, and the ticket matches the quote.

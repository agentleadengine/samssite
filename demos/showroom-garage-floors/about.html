Started because half-finished garages kept showing up.

Dave started Showroom Garage Floors after one too many Saturdays fixing other people's half-finished garage projects — paint peeling at the tire line, DIY epoxy kits gone gummy in the corners. The floor was always the part people tried to shortcut, and the part that showed it first.

The idea behind the name is simple: a garage floor should look like it belongs in a showroom, not like an afterthought poured the same day as the driveway. That means grinding the concrete properly, running a real two-part epoxy system, and picking a flake blend that actually looks right under your own garage lighting — not just under a spotlight at a home show booth.

Every job is walked start to finish by the same crew, on a schedule you know before work begins. No mystery add-on days, and no flake blend picked off a tiny paper swatch.

How a project actually runs.

Same order, every time. It's the reason the finish holds up after the first winter, not just on install day.

First, we look at the slab

Every job starts with an honest look at the concrete — cracks, old coatings, moisture, whatever's actually there. That decides the prep, not a guess made over the phone.

Then prep, coat, and broadcast

Grinding, repair, base coat, flake, topcoat — in that order, on a schedule we tell you up front. No surprise extra days once we've started.

Last, the walk-through

We walk the finished floor with you under your own garage light before we call it done. Anything off gets fixed before we leave, not after you notice it later.

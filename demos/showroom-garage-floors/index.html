This isn't paint on a slab.

A real epoxy floor is a system, not a color. Skip a layer and it shows up later as peeling, hot-tire marks, or flake that never really locked in.

  • Grinding opens the concrete so the coat actually bonds, instead of sitting on top of it.
  • The base coat carries the color and gives the flake something to grab onto.
  • Flake goes down broadcast, full coverage — not sprinkled on and hoped for.
  • The topcoat takes the abuse, so the finish underneath doesn't have to.

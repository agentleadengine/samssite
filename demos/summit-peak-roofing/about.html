The story behind the ridge.

Summit Peak Roofing exists because roof replacement is the largest home maintenance decision most people will make — and most of them are making it with bad information.

Why this business

I started Summit Peak Roofing after years of watching homeowners get sold roofs they did not need, or get sold the wrong roof for their house. The roofing business has a reputation problem, and most of it is earned — high-pressure sales, subcontracted crews, and quotes that do not match the final invoice.

The idea behind Summit Peak is simple: fly a drone over the roof before quoting, show the homeowner what we found, and give an honest recommendation. Sometimes that recommendation is a repair, not a replacement. Sometimes it is ventilation work, not new shingles. The right answer is not always the expensive one.

We do our own work. No subcontracted crews, no day-labor pickups. The people who show up to quote the job are the people who will be on the roof doing it.

How a project works

Every job starts with a drone inspection. We fly the roof, capture high-resolution images, and walk you through what we see — on a tablet, at your kitchen table. If the roof needs replacement, we explain why. If it does not, we tell you that too.

On replacement day, we arrive early. The crew sets up tarps to protect landscaping, positions the dumpster, and begins the full teardown to the deck. We inspect every sheet of plywood or plank decking, replace what is soft, and build the layers back in order — ice and water shield, underlayment, drip edge, flashing, shingles. At the end of the day, we do a magnetic nail sweep and haul the dumpster away. Your property looks like we were never there, except for the new roof.

After installation, we fly the drone again. You see the finished roof up close without climbing a ladder, and you get a written record of the work.

Service area

Summit Peak Roofing serves homeowners across Long Island, NY — from the Nassau-Queens border east through Suffolk. We work on homes of every age and style, from post-war ranches to new construction, steep-pitch Capes to flat-roof additions.

If you are on Long Island and your roof is on your mind, call us. The drone inspection is the first step.

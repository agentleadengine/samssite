Roofing questions, answered

The only honest answer is: we need to look. Curling, cracking, or missing shingles across a large area usually point toward replacement. A single leak in an otherwise sound roof usually points toward repair. We fly the drone, show you what we find, and recommend the option that makes sense — including telling you when a repair is enough.

We fly a small drone over your roof and capture high-resolution photos of every slope, valley, flashing, and penetration. The whole flyover takes about 20 minutes. We then sit down with you — at your kitchen table or over the phone — and walk through the images together so you can see exactly what we see.

We provide the documentation you need — drone imagery, a written report, and scope of work. You submit that to your insurance company and handle the claim directly. We do not represent your insurer or act as a claims adjuster. We give you honest information about the condition of your roof; what your policy covers is between you and your insurance company.

The inspection is the first step.

Roofing services built to last.

Five core services. No upsells, no subcontracted surprises. Every job starts with an inspection and ends with a clean site.

Full Roof Replacement

The complete system: old roof comes off down to the deck, damaged wood is replaced, and the layers go back in the right order — ice and water shield in the valleys, synthetic underlayment across the field, drip edge at every eave, new flashing where the roof meets walls and chimneys, and architectural shingles on top.

  • Complete teardown to the plywood or plank deck
  • Rotted decking inspection and replacement
  • Ice and water shield in valleys, eaves, and penetrations
  • Synthetic underlayment across the full field
  • New drip edge, step flashing, and counter-flashing
  • Architectural or designer shingles installed to manufacturer spec

When you need it

Your shingles are curling, cracking, or losing granules into the gutters. You are finding soft spots in the deck. The roof is past its useful life and repairs are becoming frequent. A replacement is cheaper than the next three emergency leak calls.

Leak Repair

Water on the ceiling is a symptom, not the diagnosis. We trace the leak back to its source — which is rarely directly above the stain. Roof leaks travel along rafters and underlayment, so the entry point and the visible damage are often feet apart.

  • Drone and attic inspection to trace the entry point
  • Shingle replacement at the source area
  • Flashing repair around chimneys, vents, and skylights
  • Valley repair and resealing
  • Ice dam damage assessment and fix
  • Interior stain does not always mean the roof is the cause — we check

When you need it

You see water stains on a ceiling, dampness in an attic after rain, or active dripping. A repair is the right call when the rest of the roof is still sound — the shingles are in good shape and the leak is isolated to one area.

Storm Damage Inspection

After wind, hail, or a fallen limb, the damage is not always visible from the ground. We fly a drone over the roof, capture high-resolution imagery, and document every compromised shingle, dent, or puncture — so you have a clear record before deciding what to do next.

  • Drone flyover with high-resolution photo documentation
  • Hail impact assessment — shingle bruising and granule loss
  • Wind lift inspection — lifted, creased, or missing shingles
  • Fallen limb and debris damage assessment
  • Flashing and vent boot inspection
  • Written report with imagery for your records

When you need it

A storm just passed through. Neighbors are getting roofs inspected. You heard limbs hitting the house or saw shingles in the yard. Even if nothing looks wrong from the ground, an inspection is the only way to catch damage before the next rain turns it into a leak.

Gutter Integration

A new roof with old gutters is a half-finished job. Water management is part of the roof system. We install seamless aluminum gutters sized to your roof area, with downspouts that move water away from the foundation — not just off the edge of the roof.

  • Seamless aluminum gutters, cut on-site to exact length
  • 5-inch and 6-inch profiles based on roof area
  • Downspout placement for proper drainage away from foundation
  • Gutter guard compatibility assessment
  • Drip edge integration with gutter system
  • Fascia and soffit inspection during installation

When you need it

Your gutters are pulling away, sagging, overflowing in normal rain, or rusting through. You are replacing the roof and the gutters are old enough that putting new shingles around them does not make sense. Water is pooling near the foundation after rain.

Flat Roof Systems

Flat and low-slope roofs need a different approach than pitched roofs. Water does not run off — it sits, pools, and finds every seam. We install modified bitumen and EPDM membrane systems designed for low-slope conditions, with proper drainage planning and seam sealing.

  • Modified bitumen torch-down and self-adhered systems
  • EPDM rubber membrane installation
  • TPO membrane for commercial and residential flat sections
  • Drainage planning and scupper installation
  • Seam and penetration flashing
  • Ponding water assessment and correction

When you need it

You have a low-slope section — a porch, an addition, a dormer, or a full flat roof. The existing membrane is bubbling, cracking, or pooling water. Seam failures are showing up as interior leaks. Flat roofs have shorter lifespans than pitched roofs, and waiting usually means interior damage.

Every roof has layers. We engineer every one of them.

Most roofers see shingles. We see a system — shingles, underlayment, ice and water shield, drip edge, decking. Each layer has a job, and each layer is where a shortcut becomes a leak three winters from now.

That is why every Summit Peak replacement starts with a full teardown to the deck. We inspect the wood underneath, replace what is soft, and build the layers back in the right order — not just what is visible from the street.

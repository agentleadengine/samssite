Sample projects from the ridge.

Each project below is a sample — a representative roofing scenario showing how Summit Peak approaches the work. No real client names, no before-and-after photos. Just the engineering.

Sample project

Full Replacement — Two-Story Colonial

A 2,200-square-foot colonial with a 30-year-old roof showing curling shingles and granule loss. Full teardown revealed two sheets of soft decking near the chimney, which were replaced before the new system went on.

  • Architectural shingles in charcoal
  • Ice and water shield in both valleys and around chimney
  • New step flashing and counter-flashing at chimney
  • Two sheets of decking replaced
  • Drone inspection before and after
Sample project

Flat Roof — EPDM Membrane Over Porch Addition

A 400-square-foot low-slope porch roof with ponding water and failing asphalt roll roofing. The old material was removed, the slope was corrected with tapered insulation, and a new EPDM membrane was installed with proper drainage to a scupper.

  • Tapered insulation for drainage correction
  • 60-mil EPDM membrane, fully adhered
  • New scupper and downspout connection
  • Seam and penetration flashing
  • Ponding water resolved
Sample project

Storm Damage — Wind and Fallen Limb

After a summer storm with 60 mph wind gusts, a limb fell across the roof slope, puncturing several shingles. The drone flyover found an additional area of wind-lifted shingles on the opposite slope that was not visible from the ground.

  • Drone inspection with photo documentation
  • 12 shingles replaced in the impact zone
  • 8 wind-lifted shingles replaced on opposite slope
  • Decking inspected — no puncture through to the wood
  • Flashing checked at chimney and valley
Sample project

Full Replacement — Steep-Pitch Cape with Dormer

A 1,600-square-foot Cape with a 12/12 pitch and a single shed dormer. The steep slope required harness setup and toe boards. The dormer had flashing failure causing a long-term slow leak into the upstairs bedroom.

  • Architectural shingles in slate gray
  • Dormer sidewall and roof flashing replaced
  • Ice and water shield extended up the dormer cheek walls
  • Steep-slope safety setup — harnesses and toe boards
  • Seamless gutter replacement with 6-inch profile
Sample project

Full Replacement — Ranch with Ice Dam History

A 1,400-square-foot ranch with recurring ice dams on the north slope. The previous roof had minimal ice and water shield. The replacement extended ice and water shield 6 feet up from the eaves on both slopes, with a focus on the north-facing side.

  • Ice and water shield extended 6 feet up from all eaves
  • Full synthetic underlayment across both slopes
  • Improved attic ventilation assessment
  • Architectural shingles in weathered wood
  • New drip edge with gutter integration

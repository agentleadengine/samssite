About the shop.

Black Anchor started with a simple complaint: too many shops treat the drawing like an afterthought and the sitting like a rush job. Dana opened the shop to do it the other way around, with the drawing first, on paper, before a machine ever gets picked up.

The shop books one appointment at a time. No stacking three clients across one afternoon, no drawing your piece the morning you walk in. If the book is full, it is full, and you get an honest date instead of a rushed one.

It is still just a place to get tattooed properly: a chair, a wall of flash, a stencil that goes on your skin before it goes anywhere near a needle. If it does not sit right in the stencil, it does not go any further until it does.

Where we are

A private studio serving Long Island, NY. Consultations happen at the shop or over the phone, whichever is easier for you. Call for directions and parking before you head over.

What to expect

  • You must be 18 or older with a valid government photo ID. No exceptions, and we do not tattoo minors even with a parent present.
  • Deposits are taken on every booking and come off the final price. They are non-refundable, but they move once with 48 hours' notice.
  • Eat a full meal before your session and bring a drink. Long sittings are easier on a full stomach.
  • Cash and card are both fine. Card payments carry a small processing fee that we will tell you about up front.
  • One guest per client, please. The shop is small and a crowded station slows the work down.
  • Every client leaves with written aftercare instructions and a number to call if anything looks off.

Questions before
you book?

Call the shop directly. If we are with a client, leave your name, the idea, and the best time to reach you, and we will call back between appointments.

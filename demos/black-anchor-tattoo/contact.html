A few common
questions.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments first, always. Walk-ins get taken when the book allows it, which changes day to day, so call ahead before you drive over.

How does the deposit work?

Every booking takes a deposit that comes straight off your final price. It is non-refundable, but it moves once if you give us 48 hours' notice.

What should I do before my session?

Eat a real meal, bring a drink, and get some sleep the night before. Long sittings go a lot easier on a full stomach than an empty one.

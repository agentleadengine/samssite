Six kinds
of appointment.

Every appointment starts the same way: you tell us the idea, we tell you honestly what it takes. Here is what we book, what is included, and when each one is the right call.

Custom design

What's included

Bring a reference, a photo, or a half-formed idea. We talk through size, placement and budget, then draw the piece from scratch and share the concept before your date. One round of changes is built into the price.

When you need it

Right call when nothing on the flash wall matches what you actually want, or the piece has to fit one specific spot on your body exactly.

American traditional

What's included

Bold outline, solid black, a tight color palette. Anchors, roses, panthers, script banners: the stuff that still reads clean from across the room in twenty years.

When you need it

Right call for flash straight off the wall, or a custom piece you want built in that same bold, durable style.

Black & grey

What's included

Smooth shading with no color: ornamental filler, statues and objects, wildlife, sleeve backgrounds. Larger pieces get mapped out across multiple sittings so the flow works before we commit.

When you need it

Right call for detailed or ornamental work where color would compete with the shading instead of adding to it.

Fine line & lettering

What's included

Single-needle script, small symbols, dates, coordinates, initials. We set the size and spacing on your skin first, because lettering that looks right on paper does not always sit right on a forearm.

When you need it

Right call for small, personal pieces where the wording, or the line itself, is the whole point.

Cover-ups & rework

What's included

Old work darkened, reshaped, or folded into something bigger. Text a clear photo taken in daylight and we will tell you straight whether a cover-up will work, or whether the piece needs a different plan.

When you need it

Right call when a piece has faded badly, never healed right, or just does not fit who you are anymore.

Touch-ups

What's included

Line and color touch-ups on work done in this shop, booked as a short sitting. Bring the piece in so we can look at it in the light before we put you on the calendar.

When you need it

Right call a year or two after a piece heals, once you can see where the line or the color has softened.

What it runs

Shop minimum starts at $120 and covers setup, needles and the smallest pieces. Anything larger is quoted by the sitting once we have seen the placement and the drawing. We will give you the number before you commit, not after.

Four steps,
no surprises.

Step 01

Talk it through

Call the shop and describe the idea. We ask about size, placement, color, and what you want to spend, then tell you roughly how many hours it takes.

Step 02

Deposit & date

A deposit holds the chair and comes straight off your final total. It moves once with at least 48 hours' notice, so life happening is not a problem.

Step 03

Drawing & stencil

The piece is drawn before you walk in. On the day, we place the stencil, move it as many times as it takes, and only start when you are happy with where it sits.

Step 04

Session & aftercare

We work at a steady pace with breaks when you need them. You leave wrapped, with a written aftercare card and a phone number to call with questions.

Not sure which
one fits?

Call the shop and describe what you have in mind. We will point you to the right kind of appointment before you book anything.

A few common questions.

Do I need a survey before we start?

Not on day one. For a feasibility check or early concept sketches we can often work from what's on file with the town. Once we move into permit drawings, a current survey makes the setback and grading work accurate, and we'll tell you exactly when to order one.

How long does a permit drawing set take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly concept decisions get made, but a typical addition or renovation set takes a few weeks once the floor plan is settled. New homes and anything with a zoning question upfront take longer. You'll get a real timeline at the first site walk, not a guess over the phone.

Can you design to my actual budget instead of past it?

That's the point of the feasibility and early concept work. We'd rather tell you a room needs to shrink in month one than let you fall in love with drawings that don't match what it costs to build. If the numbers don't work, we say so before you've spent more on drawings.

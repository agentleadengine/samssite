How a first lesson actually goes

Every student starts in the same quiet place, no matter how nervous the first phone call sounds.

Stop 1: The quiet lot

Steering, braking, mirrors

We start somewhere with nobody else around. Cones mark a box, and the first goal is small: steer straight, brake smooth, know where your mirrors actually show you.

Stop 2: Residential streets

Stop signs, turns, judgment

Once the basics feel automatic, we move to the streets students actually drive on: stop signs, parked cars, the timing of a left turn with traffic coming.

Stop 3: The parkway, when you're ready

Merging, real speed

Highways and parkways come last, and only when the student says so. Merging is a specific skill, and we drill it in short stretches instead of throwing anyone into rush hour.

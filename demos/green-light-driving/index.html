What a first lesson actually looks like

We start in a quiet lot: steering, braking, mirrors, the feel of the car before anyone asks you to think about traffic. No clipboard, no grading, just getting your hands used to it.

Every car has a second brake on the instructor’s side, so there’s always a real backstop, not just a reassuring voice. Once the lot feels boring, we move to residential streets. You decide when boring happens. We don’t rush it.

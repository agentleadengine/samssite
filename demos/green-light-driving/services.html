What we teach

Call and tell us where you are: never driven, halfway there, or just need the road test handled. Six ways in, one phone call to start.

90 minutes

Beginner lessons

First time behind the wheel. We start in a quiet lot with steering, braking, and mirrors, then work up to residential streets once it feels automatic instead of forced.

  • Quiet-lot basics: steering, braking, mirrors
  • A residential-street stretch once you're ready
  • Direct feedback after every drive, nothing saved up

When you need it: you've never driven, or only driven a little with a family member and want to start clean.

One session

5-hour pre-licensing class

The classroom course New York requires before your road test. Held on weekends and weekday evenings, with the completion certificate handed to you at the end.

  • The full state-required classroom hours, one sitting
  • A completion certificate handed to you the same day
  • Weekend and weekday-evening sessions to fit school or work

When you need it: before your road test. New York requires the certificate to schedule one.

2 lessons + test

Road test prep & car use

We drill the maneuvers examiners look for: parallel parking, three-point turns, signaling, and you can use our dual-control car for the test appointment itself.

  • Focused drills on the maneuvers examiners actually score
  • Use of our dual-control car for the test itself
  • A walkthrough of the test route beforehand, no surprises

When you need it: once the basics are solid and you've got a test date on the calendar.

60 or 90 minutes

Nervous & returning drivers

For adults who have a license but stopped driving, or who freeze up on highways and merges. Quiet routes first, then bridges and parkways at your pace, not ours.

  • Quiet routes first, no highway on day one
  • You set the pace, out loud, before each lesson
  • Bridges and parkways only when you say you're ready

When you need it: you have a license but stopped driving, or a specific thing (a bridge, a merge) makes you freeze.

Included with lessons

Permit & paperwork help

We walk you through the written permit test material and what to bring to the DMV, so nothing sends you home for a missing document.

  • A plain-language walkthrough of the permit material
  • A checklist of exactly what to bring to the DMV
  • Answers to whatever's actually confusing you about it

When you need it: before you've even started, if the permit process itself feels like the hard part.

Ask by phone

Lesson packages

Three, five, or ten lessons booked together at a set rate, with the road test scheduling handled for you. Ask what fits your timeline when you call.

  • Three, five, or ten lessons booked at once, one set rate
  • Road test scheduling handled for you, not left to chase
  • A plan that fits your actual timeline, built on the call

When you need it: you already know you'll need more than a lesson or two and want it mapped out.

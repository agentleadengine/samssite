What we teach

Call and tell us where you are: never driven, halfway there, or just need the road test handled. Every lesson below can be booked on its own, or by phone as a package.

Beginner lessons

90 minutes

Start in a quiet lot with steering, braking, and mirrors, then work up to residential streets when you feel ready.

When you need it You’ve never driven before, or it’s been so long it might as well be never.

5-hour pre-licensing class

One session

The classroom course New York requires before your road test. Held on weekends and weekday evenings, with the completion certificate handed to you at the end.

When you need it Before you can schedule your road test: it’s a state requirement, not optional.

Road test prep & car use

2 lessons + test

We drill the maneuvers examiners look for: parallel parking, three-point turns, signaling. Our dual-control car is yours to use for the test itself.

When you need it Your test is booked and you want the last mile to feel rehearsed, not risky.

Nervous & returning drivers

60 or 90 minutes

For adults who have a license but stopped driving, or who freeze up on highways and merges. Quiet routes first, then bridges and parkways at your pace.

When you need it You already know how to drive, technically. You just don’t trust yourself out there yet.

Permit & paperwork help

Included with lessons

We walk you through the written permit test material and what to bring to the DMV, so nothing sends you home for a missing document.

When you need it Before lesson one, so a missing document doesn’t cost you the appointment.

Lesson packages

Ask by phone

Three, five, or ten lessons booked together at a set rate, with the road test scheduling handled for you.

When you need it You already know you’ll need more than one or two lessons and want it planned out, not booked call by call.

Not sure which one fits?

Tell us where you are on the phone and we’ll figure out the right starting point together.

A few things people ask

Do I need a permit before my first lesson?

No. If you don't have one yet, we'll walk you through the permit paperwork first, before anything else.

I've genuinely never driven. Is that a problem?

No, that's most of our beginner lessons. We start in an empty lot and build up from there, one step at a time.

Can you pick me up?

Yes. Home, school, or work, anywhere in our Nassau or Suffolk service area, and we'll drop you back after.

A few common questions

Do I need a permit before my first lesson?
No. If you don’t have one yet, we’ll walk you through the written test material and what to bring to the DMV before your first lesson in the car.
Can I take my road test in your car?
Yes. Our dual-control car is available for the road test itself once you’ve finished test prep with us.
What if I’m not a total beginner, just rusty?
That’s exactly what the nervous and returning driver lessons are for: quiet routes first, then bridges and parkways at your pace.

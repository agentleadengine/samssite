A few questions before you call

Do you only work Saturdays?

Saturday’s the name because that’s the feeling – the list that actually gets shorter. Morning bookings run Monday through Saturday; call and we’ll find one that fits.

What should I have ready before you show up?

Just the list – written down, texted over, or run through with us at the door. If it’s more than fits in one morning, we’ll say so before we start and help you pick what matters most today.

What if something on the list turns out to be bigger than expected?

We stop and tell you before it becomes part of the morning. Some things – electrical panels, gas lines, structural work – we’ll point you to someone licensed for instead of guessing.

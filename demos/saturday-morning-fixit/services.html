What we fix in a morning

Five kinds of jobs, one visit each. If it’s not on here, call and ask – if it’s not ours, we’ll say so straight.

Small repairs

The stuff that’s minor until it’s been on the list for two months – a hinge that catches, a drawer that sticks, a crack in the drywall from the last time a door swung too hard. None of it’s a big job on its own. Together, it’s usually most of a morning.

If something turns out to need a permit or a licensed trade, we say so on the spot and point you toward who to call, instead of taking a swing at it anyway.

What’s included

Doors, hinges & handles that stick or sag

Drywall dings, nail holes & small cracks

Loose trim, baseboards & caulk lines

Squeaky, sticking & misaligned drawers

When you need this

When you’ve got a running list of little stuff that never feels worth its own service call.

Furniture assembly

Flat-pack furniture built square, level, and anchored to the wall where tipping is a real risk – dressers, bookshelves, bed frames, wardrobes. We follow the real instructions, not the ones that came in the box, and check every joint before the last screw goes in.

Boxes and packaging leave with us. If a piece shows up with a warped panel or missing hardware, you’ll hear it before assembly starts, not halfway through.

What’s included

Flat-pack furniture, any brand

Bed frames, dressers & bookshelves

Wall anchoring for tip-prone pieces

Packaging hauled out with us

When you need this

Same morning, straight out of the box – or after it’s sat in the garage a while. Either way.

TV & shelf mounting

Mounted into the stud, set level with an actual level, and the cords run somewhere you don’t have to look at. Floating shelves get the same treatment – rated for what you’re actually putting on them, not just a candle.

We test every mount with real weight before we leave. If the studs don’t line up where you want the TV, we’ll say so and price a backer board straight, before anything’s on the wall.

What’s included

TV mounting, any size, into the stud

Floating & bracket shelving

Cord concealment & cable management

Curtain rods, mirrors & wall art

When you need this

Before it goes another weekend propped against the wall.

Door & window fixes

A door that catches on the frame, a window that won’t stay up on its own, a lock that’s started fighting the deadbolt – usually a hinge, a strike plate, or a track that’s out of line, not a whole new door or window.

Screens and weatherstripping get a look while we’re there too, if you want a second set of eyes on them, even if that wasn’t on the original list.

What’s included

Doors that stick, sag, or won’t latch

Windows that stick or won’t stay open

Weatherstripping & draft fixes

Lock & handle adjustment

When you need this

Once you’ve stopped noticing it’s broken and started just living with it.

Punch-list projects

The list you’ve been adding to since spring. Send it over, we walk it with you when we arrive, and we work down it in order. Whatever fits in the morning gets done; whatever’s bigger than a morning, we say so before we start.

You get a walkthrough at the end – every item pointed at and confirmed, not just a text that says “all set.”

What’s included

One list, worked top to bottom

A walkthrough before anything starts

Honest word on what’s bigger than a morning

A finished-list walkthrough before we leave

When you need this

When the list is longer than any one thing on it.

Ready to put a date on it?

Call with the list, or send it through the contact page – either way you’ll get an actual morning, not a maybe.

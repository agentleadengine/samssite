Why Saturday morning, specifically

The idea started small: Joe Marchetti was the guy on the block who’d spend a Saturday morning helping a neighbor hang a shelf or fix a sticking door, then head home in time for lunch. Enough neighbors asked enough times that it turned into an actual schedule – still mornings, still one house at a time, just with a truck and a real list of tools instead of whatever was in the garage.

That’s still the whole model. We book one home per morning, walk the list in person before anything starts, and work down it in order. If it fits in the morning, it gets done. If something’s bigger than that – a permit, a licensed trade, a job that needs two visits – we say so up front instead of letting the clock make that decision for us.

“Saturday Morning” is the name because that’s the feeling we’re after: the good kind of to-do list, the one that actually gets shorter. The bookings themselves run Monday through Saturday – more on that on the contact page.

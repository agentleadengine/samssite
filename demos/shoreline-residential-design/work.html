What actually gets drawn

A handful of projects from this season, shown the way we'd walk you through them at the site visit. Every scene here is a sample, not a specific address.

Sample project

Elevated Bay House, Concept to Framing

A new house on a narrow bay-front lot, set on piers well above the flood line with a wide deck facing the water. Shown here mid-framing, past the point where the section drawing became a real floor.

Sample project

Screened Porch Addition, Opened to the Water

A 1960s bungalow's back wall came out and a screened porch went in its place, turning a room that faced the driveway into one that faces the bay.

Sample project

Raised Bungalow, Flood Compliance

The original footprint stayed put. Everything above it went up on new piers to clear the updated flood map, with a new stair and storage tucked underneath.

Sample project

Primary Suite Addition with Roof Deck

A second story went on above an existing one-story wing, adding a primary suite and a small roof deck angled toward the water instead of the road.

Sample project

Site Feasibility Sketch, Dune-Line Lot

Before any design work started, this one needed the setbacks, the dune line, and the flood zone pulled and drawn against the lot, to see what size house was actually possible.

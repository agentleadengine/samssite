A few things people ask

Do you only do new construction, or renovations too?

Both, though most of what we're doing right now is raising and reworking older beach houses rather than starting from an empty lot.

What if I'm not sure my lot can support what I want?

That's exactly what the site feasibility study is for — a straight answer before you spend on a full design.

Do you handle the permit and zoning process, or just the design?

Both. We prepare the construction drawings for the building department, and the additional drawings a zoning variance hearing needs if your setback isn't standard.

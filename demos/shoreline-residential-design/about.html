About Shoreline Residential Design

Most of the beach houses out here were built decades before anyone drew a flood map for this stretch of coast. They face the wrong direction, they sit a few feet lower than they should, and half of them have a room nobody uses because it never got the view it was supposed to have. Shoreline exists to fix that on paper, before it gets fixed with a contractor's best guess.

That starts with a real site visit, not a phone estimate. We pull the flood zone and the setbacks, walk the lot, and talk through what the house is actually allowed to become before anyone draws a floor plan. A lot of that conversation is about what won't work, which saves everyone a redesign later.

The result is usually simpler than people expect. Not a bigger house. A house turned to face what it's sitting next to.

How it works

No design fee spent before you know what the lot can actually support.

You call

Tell us about the property and what isn't working — the view, the layout, the height. A few minutes on the phone usually sorts out what kind of project this is.

Concept & drawings

Permit set

A full construction document set for the building department, and the zoning drawings too if your setback needs a variance.

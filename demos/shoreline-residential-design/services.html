What we draw

Five ways we get involved, from a first feasibility look to a full permit set. If your project needs something else, say so on the phone.

Custom Home Design

What's included
Concept sketches, a full construction drawing set, and 3D views so you can see the rooms before they're framed.
When you need it
An empty lot, a teardown, or a house too far gone to save. Anything starting from a blank site plan.

Design fees are set as a percentage of the construction budget, worked out after the feasibility study.

Additions & Renovations

What's included
Opening up a closed-off layout, adding a primary suite, raising a house to clear the flood line, or extending the deck toward the water.
When you need it
The bones of the house are fine, but the view, the elevation, or the square footage isn't working anymore.

Fee depends on scope — a bump-out and a full second-story addition aren't priced the same way. Set after we've walked the house.

Permit Drawings

What's included
A full, submission-ready construction document set for the building department, plus the drawings a zoning variance hearing needs if your setback isn't standard.
When you need it
You already have a design, or a contractor who needs a stamped-ready set to actually pull the permit.
From $4,200

3D Concept Visualization

What's included
A walkthrough view of the addition or new house from the angles that matter — the new great room, the deck, the water view from the kitchen.
When you need it
Before you commit to a full design, or before you sit down with a contractor to talk numbers.
From $1,100

Site Feasibility Studies

What's included
A pull of the flood zone map, setbacks, and dune line for your lot, plus an honest read on what size and shape of house it can actually support.
When you need it
Before you buy a lot, before you fall in love with a floor plan, or before you spend on a full design.
From $1,450

Say what's going on with the property. We'll tell you which of these you actually need.

About the truck

Dana runs the truck. The idea was simple: keep the menu short enough that everything can be made fresh, in front of you, on a flat top hot enough to give a smash burger real edges.

That's still the whole plan. A short board, a hot griddle, and enough notice from a phone call to have the right amount of meat thawed. No freezer-aisle shortcuts, no menu so long that half of it sits untouched.

Some days that means a quiet office park lunch. Other days it's a graduation party with eighty people and a folding table of sides. Either way, the questions are the same: where are you parked, how long is the wait, and can you fit us in. Call and we'll give you a straight answer.

How the window works

1

Find the truck

Call the number and we'll tell you exactly where we're parked that day and how long the wait is running.

2

Order at the window

Tell us what you want and how you want it. Card, tap, or cash. We call out your name, not a number.

3

Eat it hot

Most orders come off the griddle in about eight minutes. Big orders take longer, and we'll tell you straight up if they do.

The Ember Smash

Two thin patties, pressed hard on the flat top for the crispy edges. American cheese on both, pickles, griddled onion, and our house sauce on a toasted potato bun.

Get this if you want the classic done right — no substitutions, no surprises.

Bacon & Hot Honey

Single or double, thick-cut bacon cooked right on the griddle, sharp cheddar, and a hot honey drizzle that hits sweet first and catches up with you after.

Mushroom Melt (veg)

Black bean and mushroom patty, smashed the same way as the beef, with Swiss, caramelized onion, and garlic aioli. Cooked on a clean section of the griddle.

Crinkle Fries, Loaded or Not

Salted crinkle cuts, fried twice. Load them with cheese sauce, griddled onion, and pickled jalapeño for $8, or keep it plain and dip them in whatever you like.

Order the loaded size if more than two people are eating out of the same box.

Private & Corporate Bookings

Birthdays, graduations, job sites, brewery nights, and office lunches. Truck bookings start at $1,200 for two hours of service. We bring the truck, the griddle, the crew, and a menu cut down so the line stays quick.

Call once you know roughly how many people are eating and whether there's power on site. We'll work out the rest with you.

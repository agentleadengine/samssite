A few common questions.

Do you take toothache appointments the same day?

Call in the morning and tell the front desk what hurts and when it started. We do our best to fit you in that day.

Can you see my kids at the same visit as me?

Yes. Tell us when you call and we'll book your family back to back instead of on separate days.

Will I know the cost before you start any work?

Yes. Beyond a routine cleaning, you'll get a plain-language explanation and a written number before anything happens.

Prefer to just call?

The front desk answers during office hours and can usually find a routine cleaning within a couple of weeks.

The office, and how it runs.

Dr. Naomi Park started Bright Smile Family Dental with one idea: an appointment should start when it's supposed to, and end with you understanding exactly what happened and what it costs. Long Island has plenty of dental offices. Not all of them treat your calendar like it matters as much as your teeth.

In practice, that means early mornings before school and work, two evenings that run past five, and a Saturday morning for whoever can't make a weekday. Families get booked back to back, so one trip to the office covers everyone instead of three separate ones spread across the month.

Every visit ends with a plain-language walk-through of what was found, and a written number for anything beyond a routine cleaning, before any of it happens. If something can wait, you'll hear that too. Nobody leaves the chair confused about what just happened or what it costs.

How a first visit goes.

Four steps, in this order, every time.

  1. You call the office

    Tell us what you need, a cleaning, a specific tooth, or a visit for the kids, and we'll pick the right length of appointment.

  2. Paperwork before you arrive

    We email the new-patient forms so you can fill them out at home instead of on a clipboard in the waiting room.

  3. Exam and plan

    The dentist walks through what they see in plain language, and writes down what's recommended and what each part costs.

  4. Next appointment before you leave

    If anything needs a follow-up, we book it at the front desk while you're there and send a reminder the week before.

Everyday dentistry, in our own office.

Call and tell us what's going on. We'll book the visit length that actually fits, not a generic slot.

Cleanings & Checkups

A hygienist cleans and polishes, we take x-rays when they're due, and the dentist looks over your teeth and gums and talks through what they see.

About 45-60 minutes

Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings, usually finished in a single visit. We go over what needs to be done, and what it costs, before any work starts.

About 30-60 minutes

Crowns & Bridges

An impression, a temporary while the lab work is made, then a fitting appointment once it comes back. We'll tell you the timeline up front.

Two visits, about 2-3 weeks apart

Kids' Visits

Short, friendly appointments for children: a ride in the chair, a count of the teeth, a cleaning, and time for questions from parents.

About 30 minutes

Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening or custom take-home trays. We'll show you both options, and what each one involves, before you decide.

One visit or take-home

Toothache Appointments

Call in the morning and tell the front desk what hurts and when it started. We do our best to see you the same day.

Same-day when the schedule allows

Not sure which visit you need?

Call the front desk and describe what's going on. We'll match it to the right appointment.

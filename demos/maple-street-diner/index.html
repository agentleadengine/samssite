The grill goes on before the sun's up. The pot gets dumped and made fresh every half hour whether it's empty or not, so nobody's drinking yesterday's coffee by accident. By eleven the blue plate special is chalked up on the board — one dish, cooked in a big batch, gone when it's gone.

Three ways to eat

Same kitchen, same prices, no service fee for calling ahead.

  • At the counter Fourteen stools and a dozen booths. If the counter's full, give your name at the register and grab a coffee while you wait.
  • Call ahead, take it home Tell us what you want and roughly when you're coming. Most orders are ready in about fifteen minutes.
  • Trays for the office Sandwich platters, breakfast trays with a box of coffee, or a full pan of the day's special. Give us a day's notice and a head count.

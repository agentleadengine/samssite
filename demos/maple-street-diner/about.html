About the Diner

How the counter runs, day to day.

Maple Street Diner is a counter with fourteen stools and a dozen booths, plus a kitchen you can watch from most of the seats. Nothing about it is fancy — a flat-top griddle, a coffee station that never really cools down, and a pie case by the register that gets refilled straight through the day.

Breakfast runs all day because there's no good reason it shouldn't. The blue plate special is cooked in one big batch each morning and goes up on the board at eleven; when the pan's empty, that's it until tomorrow. Everything else — the burgers, the melts, the dinner plates after four — comes off the same griddle and out of the same kitchen, whether you're at a booth or waiting on a call-ahead order.

Takeout works the same as sitting down: same menu, same prices, no extra charge for calling ahead. Tell us roughly when you're coming and we'll have it bagged and hot. Office trays need a day's notice and a head count, but otherwise it's first-come, first-served — put your name in at the register if the counter's full and grab a coffee while you wait.

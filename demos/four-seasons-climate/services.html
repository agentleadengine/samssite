Services

Full heating and air conditioning service for Long Island homes. Here is what each one includes and when you might need it.

AC Installation

A new air conditioner is not just a box that goes on the wall. It is a system that needs to be sized to your home, matched to your ductwork, and installed so it runs quietly and efficiently for years. We handle the full job: load calculation, equipment selection, installation, and cleanup.

  • System sizing and load calculation
  • Equipment selection and placement
  • Indoor and outdoor unit installation
  • Ductwork modification if needed
  • Refrigerant line set and electrical connection
  • System testing and walkthrough

When you need it

Your current AC is more than 12-15 years old, needs frequent repairs, or cannot keep up on hot days. Or you are adding cooling to a home that never had it.

Furnace Repair

When the heat stops, you need someone who can find the problem fast and fix it right. We diagnose ignition failures, blower motor issues, thermocouple problems, and everything in between. If a repair makes sense, we do it. If the furnace is at the end of its life, we tell you straight.

  • Full diagnostic and safety inspection
  • Ignition system and pilot light repair
  • Blower motor and belt service
  • Thermocouple and flame sensor replacement
  • Gas valve and pressure switch testing
  • Honest repair-vs-replace recommendation

When you need it

Your furnace is short-cycling, blowing cold air, making unusual noises, or has stopped entirely. Do not wait — call the same day.

Ductless Mini-Splits

Some rooms do not need the whole house heated or cooled — they need their own climate. Ductless mini-splits deliver targeted comfort without installing or modifying ductwork. Perfect for additions, converted garages, sunrooms, and homes where one zone is always too hot or too cold.

  • Single-zone and multi-zone systems
  • Indoor wall unit placement and mounting
  • Outdoor condenser installation
  • Line set routing and concealment
  • Electrical connection and testing
  • Remote and thermostat setup

When you need it

A specific room is always uncomfortable, you are finishing a basement or addition, or you want zone control without running new ductwork.

Seasonal Maintenance Plans

The best time to fix a heating or cooling problem is before it happens. Our seasonal maintenance plan covers a spring cooling check and a fall heating check, so your systems are ready before the weather turns. You get priority scheduling and a record of what was inspected.

  • Spring AC tune-up: coil cleaning, refrigerant check, electrical test
  • Fall furnace tune-up: heat exchanger inspection, burner check, filter change
  • Priority scheduling during peak season
  • Discounted repair rates for plan members
  • Written record of each visit

When you need it

You want to avoid mid-season breakdowns, extend the life of your equipment, and have a go-to company when something does go wrong.

Air Quality and Filtration

The air inside your home can be two to five times more polluted than the air outside. We install and service whole-home filtration systems, UV light purifiers, and humidifiers that integrate with your HVAC system. If allergies, dry air, or dust are a problem, this is the upgrade that changes daily comfort.

  • Whole-home air filtration systems
  • UV light air purifier installation
  • Whole-home humidifier installation
  • Filter upgrade and replacement schedule
  • Duct inspection and sealing

When you need it

Someone in the house has allergies or asthma, the air feels dry in winter or damp in summer, or you are constantly dusting. A filtration upgrade makes a noticeable difference within days.

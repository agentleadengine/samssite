Questions we hear a lot

How fast can you come out?

For heating emergencies in winter and AC failures in a heat wave, we aim for same-day. For installations and non-emergency repairs, usually within 1-2 days. Call and we will give you a real time estimate.

Should I repair or replace my old AC?

If your AC is over 12-15 years old and needs a major repair, replacement usually makes more sense — new systems are significantly more efficient and you avoid paying for a fix that buys you one more season. We will give you both numbers and let you decide.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. Our seasonal plan includes a spring cooling check and a fall heating check, priority scheduling, and discounted repair rates. It pays for itself if you ever need a mid-season visit. See the maintenance plan page for details.

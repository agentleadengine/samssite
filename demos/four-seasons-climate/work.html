Sample projects

Illustrated examples of the kind of work we do. These are representative scenarios, not specific client records.

Sample project

Full AC replacement

A 1970s ranch with a 15-year-old condenser that could not hold refrigerant. We removed the old unit, sized a new 2.5-ton system, replaced the line set, and had the house cooling again the same day.

Sample project

Mid-winter furnace repair

A family called on a 12-degree night — the furnace was running but blowing cold air. We found a cracked flame sensor and a clogged pressure switch port. Replaced the sensor, cleaned the port, and had heat restored within two hours.

Sample project

Ductless mini-split for sunroom

A homeowner added a sunroom that turned into an oven every afternoon. We installed a single-zone ductless mini-split with the indoor unit on the far wall and the condenser tucked outside. The room is now usable year-round.

Sample project

Seasonal maintenance plan

A homeowner tired of forgetting to change filters and scheduling tune-ups at the last minute. We set up a twice-yearly plan — spring cooling check and fall heating check — with automatic scheduling and priority service if anything breaks between visits.

Sample project

Whole-home air filtration

A household where everyone was coughing through winter. We installed a media filtration system at the return duct and a UV light purifier at the coil. The difference was obvious within a week — less dust, clearer mornings.

Sample project

Two-zone HVAC system

A two-story home where the upstairs was always hot and the downstairs always cold. We installed motorized dampers and a zoning controller so each floor gets its own thermostat. Both zones are now comfortable at the same time.

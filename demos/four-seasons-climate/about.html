About Four Seasons Climate Co.

Four Seasons Climate Co. started with a simple observation: most HVAC companies pick a season. They are heating companies that also do AC, or cooling companies that also fix furnaces. We decided to be both — equally good at both — because that is what a home actually needs.

The work is straightforward. We show up when we say we will. We figure out what is wrong or what is needed. We explain it in plain language, do the job right, and clean up after ourselves. If a repair will get you through the season, we say so. If it will not, we tell you that too — before you spend money on something that will not last.

We work on homes across Long Island, from the South Shore to the North Fork. In the summer we are installing and repairing air conditioning. In the winter we are keeping furnaces running. In between, we are doing maintenance visits that keep both systems running when the weather turns. That is the whole idea — one company, both seasons, no uncomfortable days.

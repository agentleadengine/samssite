About

A roofing company on Long Island that thinks about materials before it thinks about weather — because the material is what fails first.

The story

Cedar & Slate Roofworks started with a simple observation: most roof failures aren't caused by storms. They're caused by the wrong material installed the wrong way — underlayment that wasn't rated for ice, flashing that wasn't folded correctly, shingles nailed too high to hold in wind. The storm just exposes what was already wrong.

So the approach is straightforward. Read the roof before recommending anything. Write down what's there, what's failing, and what isn't. Then do the work the way the manufacturer says to do it — not the shortcut, not the "close enough," not the version that saves a day and costs five years.

The name comes from the two materials that represent opposite ends of roofing craft: cedar shake, which weathers and breathes and ages like wood, and slate, which is stone and holds for a century if it's installed right. Copper is what ties them together — the flashings, the valleys, the drip edges. A roof is an assembly, not a product.

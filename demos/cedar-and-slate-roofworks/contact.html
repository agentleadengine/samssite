Roofing questions

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

If the leaks are isolated and the decking is solid, a repair is usually enough. If there are multiple leaks in different areas, shingles are curling or missing across a large section, or the decking is soft underfoot, replacement is likely the better investment. We'll tell you which one after we inspect it — we don't push replacements when a repair will hold.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements take two to three days, weather permitting. Cedar shake and slate take longer because the material is slower to install. Flat roof membrane systems can often be done in one day. We'll give you a timeline in the written estimate before we start.

Do you handle the insurance inspection after a storm?

We do a storm damage inspection and write a condition report with documented findings. That report is yours to use however you need it. We don't represent you in insurance negotiations, but the report gives you a clear, independent assessment of what the storm did.

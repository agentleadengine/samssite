Services

Five roofing services, each approached the same way: inspect, document, then recommend. No upselling, no panic quotes.

Full Roof Replacement

When repairs have stacked up past the point of diminishing returns, or when the decking itself is compromised, a full replacement is the honest answer. We handle the tear-off, deck inspection, underlayment, and new installation — whether the material is cedar shake, natural slate, architectural asphalt, or a flat membrane system.

What's included

  • Complete tear-off and disposal of existing roofing
  • Deck inspection and repair of rotted or soft sections
  • Ice and water shield at eaves, valleys, and penetrations
  • New underlayment and flashing installation
  • Material installation per manufacturer specifications
  • Site cleanup with magnetic nail sweep

When you need it

Multiple leaks in different areas, curling or missing shingles across more than 30% of the roof, or decking that gives underfoot. If only one slope is failing, we'll tell you — a partial replacement is sometimes the right call.

Leak Repair

A leak is a symptom, not a diagnosis. We trace it to the entry point — which is rarely where the water appears inside — and repair the specific failure. That might be a cracked flashing, a lifted shingle, a failed seal around a vent pipe, or ice damage along an eave.

What's included

  • Exterior and attic inspection to trace the water path
  • Targeted repair of the identified failure point
  • Flashing replacement or resealing as needed
  • Shake, slate, or shingle replacement matching existing material
  • Written summary of what was found and what was done

When you need it

Water staining on a ceiling, drips during rain, or a damp smell in the attic after a storm. The sooner the source is found, the less decking and insulation you'll replace alongside it.

Storm Damage Inspection

After a storm — wind, hail, or fallen limbs — the damage isn't always visible from the ground. We do a full roof inspection and document what we find in a written condition report with photographs. That report is yours to keep, whether or not you hire us to do the repair.

What's included

  • Full roof surface inspection from the eaves to the ridge
  • Flashing, vent, and chimney check
  • Gutter and downspout assessment
  • Written condition report with documented findings
  • Repair recommendations if damage is found

When you need it

After any storm that dropped limbs on or near the roof, produced hail, or sustained winds over 50 mph. Also worth doing if you're buying a house and want an independent roof assessment before closing.

Gutter Integration

A roof without functioning gutters is a foundation problem waiting to happen. We install and integrate gutter systems that tie cleanly into the roof edge — proper drip edge flashing, correct pitch, and downspout placement that moves water away from the foundation.

What's included

  • Gutter sizing and layout assessment
  • Seamless aluminum or copper gutter installation
  • Drip edge and fascia integration with the roof system
  • Downspout routing and extension
  • Gutter guard options for debris management

When you need it

Overflowing gutters, water pooling near the foundation, ice damming in winter, or gutters pulling away from the fascia. Also worth pairing with a roof replacement so the whole system is coordinated.

Flat Roof Systems

Flat and low-slope roofs need a different approach — membrane systems, proper drainage planning, and seam work that holds. We install TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen systems for residential flat sections, additions, porches, and commercial buildings.

What's included

  • Substrate inspection and preparation
  • Membrane installation (TPO, EPDM, or modified bitumen)
  • Seam welding and edge flashing detail
  • Drainage planning and scupper installation
  • Penetration flashing for vents, HVAC, and skylights

When you need it

Ponding water on a flat section, membrane cracks or bubbles, or a flat roof over 15 years old. Flat roof problems tend to escalate quickly once the membrane fails, so early inspection saves the insulation and decking below.

Not sure which service you need?

That's what the inspection is for. We'll come look and tell you straight.

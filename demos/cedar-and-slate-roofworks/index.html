How we work.

Materials first

We start with what's on the roof and what should be. Cedar, slate, membrane, metal — each has a correct installation, and we install it correctly.

Documented inspection

Every roof gets a written condition report before we recommend anything. You see what we see, including the parts that are fine.

Clean site, clean install

Magnetic nail sweeps, tarps over landscaping, and a dumpster hauled away when the job is done. Your property looks like we were never there — except the roof is new.

We'll come look at it, write down what we find, and tell you honestly whether it needs replacing or just a repair.

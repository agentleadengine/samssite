The idea behind Brightside.

A short version of why the crew works the way it does.

Marisol Vega started Brightside around one idea: a house should be cleaned by people who already know it. Not a rotating roster, not a different name at the door every few weeks. The same two-person crew, working from a checklist built for that specific home.

That checklist is the whole system. Before a first visit, someone walks the house, asks what actually matters (the floors, the pets, the spot by the door that always collects mail), and writes it down. The crew works from that list every time after. If something needs to change, a text is enough. Nobody has to explain the house from scratch again.

Brightside works across Long Island, on a schedule that stays the same week to week. No contracts, no long sign-up process, and pricing that's flat once a walkthrough happens, so the invoice never surprises anyone.

How the business works.

Three parts, in order, and then it just repeats.

  1. A walkthrough, not a sales pitch

    Fifteen minutes, no pressure. We look at square footage, floors, pets, and the spots that bother you most, then quote a flat rate per visit.

  2. A checklist made for your house

    Not a generic template. Your home gets its own written list, and you can add or drop items any time by text.

  3. The same crew, the same slot

    You keep the same two cleaners and the same day on the calendar. If your regular crew is out, we call before sending anyone else.

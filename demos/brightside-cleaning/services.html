What we clean.

Pick the cleaning that fits the week you're having. Every visit runs off a written checklist made for your home.

Recurring house cleaning

Made for households that want the baseline maintained without thinking about it. Weekly, every other week, or monthly visits: kitchen and bathrooms top to bottom, floors vacuumed and washed, beds made, trash out, surfaces dusted down to the baseboards, all run off the checklist built for your home.

Starting at $140 a visit

Deep clean

Booked before recurring visits start, or any time the house needs more than a surface pass. Inside the oven and fridge, cabinet fronts degreased, window sills and tracks, shower grout scrubbed, light fixtures and vents wiped down, behind and under what normally doesn't move.

Starting at $340

Move-in / move-out

Timed for closings and lease turnovers, before keys change hands. Empty-house cleaning: cabinets and drawers inside and out, appliances, closets, and floors, finished so a final walkthrough goes smoothly.

Starting at $395

One-time & occasion cleans

Booked before the family lands, after the party, or the week the in-laws visit. Tell us which rooms matter most and the crew spends the hours there instead of spreading them thin across the whole house.

Starting at $185

Small offices & studios

For storefronts, salons, and small offices that need an after-hours reset. Restrooms, glass, trash, and floors, on a schedule that stays out of customers' way. Quoted after a short walkthrough of the space.

Quoted by square footage

Add-ons, no surprises

Added to any visit when there's a little extra to cover. Interior windows, laundry folded, inside the fridge, a garage sweep. Each has a flat price approved before the crew starts, so the invoice matches the estimate.

$25–$80 each

Not sure which one fits?

Call and describe the house. We'll tell you straight which visit type makes sense, no pressure to book the bigger one.

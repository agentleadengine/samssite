Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Most clients give us entry instructions and a lockbox or code. If you'd rather be home the first time to walk through the checklist together, that's fine too.

What if I need to skip or move a visit?

Text the number above. As long as we hear from you before the crew heads out, there's no fee for skipping or rescheduling.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, every visit. If you'd rather we use a product you already have under the sink, leave a note and we'll use it instead.

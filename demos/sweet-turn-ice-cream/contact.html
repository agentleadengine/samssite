Quick questions

Three things people usually ask before they call.

How much notice do you need for a cake?

Two days is plenty for most sizes. If you need something bigger, or it's a busy weekend, call as early as you can and we'll tell you straight whether we can make it happen in time.

Do you have anything dairy-free?

Yes. Coconut-milk bases and fruit sorbets, churned on their own machine so there's no cross-contact with the dairy flavors. Ask at the counter for what's on the board that day.

Can party trays be delivered?

Pickup only. We pack pints and sundae bars cold with dry ice so they survive the ride, and we'll walk you through how much to order once we know your headcount.

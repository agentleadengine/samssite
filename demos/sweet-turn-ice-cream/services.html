Everything that comes out of the case

Six things we make regularly. The flavor board changes with whatever got churned that morning, but this list holds all year.

Scoops & cones

Single, double, or a kiddie size in a cup if cones aren't happening today. Cake cone, sugar cone, or a waffle cone we press in the front window all afternoon — you'll smell it before you see it.

Most days: no notice needed, just walk up

Sundaes

Hot fudge, salted caramel, or fresh strawberry over three scoops in a real glass dish — not a paper cup. Whipped cream, nuts and a cherry unless you say otherwise.

Walk up: ready in a couple of minutes

Shakes, malts & floats

Any flavor on the board, blended thick enough that the straw stands up on its own. Root beer floats and egg creams too, if that's more your speed.

Made to order right at the counter

Ice cream cakes

Two layers of the flavors you pick with a crunch layer in the middle. Six, nine or twelve slices, iced and lettered by hand — tell us what to write on top.

Two days' notice for most sizes

Good to know: we can split flavors down the middle, and hold a finished cake in the freezer until closing.

Party trays & catering

Pre-scooped pints, a sundae bar with six toppings, cups, spoons and napkins — packed cold for birthdays, block parties and team nights.

Pickup only: tell us your headcount and time

Packed in dry ice to stay frozen for the ride. We'll walk you through how much to order for your group.

Dairy-free & sorbet

Coconut-milk bases and fruit sorbets churned on their own machine, so there's no cross-contact with the dairy flavors. Ask at the counter and we'll walk you through what's in each one.

Ask at the counter: flavors turn with the season

Ready to order?

Call and tell us the flavors, or the headcount.

Four steps, no shortcuts

Nothing here arrives on a truck already frozen. This is the whole routine, most mornings before the doors open.

Base cooked first

Cream, sugar and egg yolks cooked in one pot, then chilled overnight so the texture sets up right.

Fruit cut by hand

Strawberries hulled, peaches sliced, bananas mashed that morning. It goes in whole, not as syrup.

Small batches only

Four quarts at a time in the machine out back. When a flavor runs out, we churn more or move on.

Cones pressed daily

Waffle batter goes on the iron all afternoon, which is why the shop smells the way it does.

A few questions we hear

Do you handle small repairs, or only whole-home jobs?

Both. Most whole-home clients started with something smaller — a dead outlet, a breaker that wouldn't stop tripping. Describe what's going on and we'll tell you where it fits.

Do you pull permits?

For panel changes and service work, yes — that's part of the job, not an add-on. Smaller repairs usually don't need one, and we'll tell you either way before anything's scheduled.

How far out are you scheduling?

It depends on the season, but nothing gets scheduled without a plan first. Call and you'll get a real timeline, not a guess.

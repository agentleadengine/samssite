A few jobs, start to finish.

Fictional projects built to show how a job actually goes here: plan first, then the work, then a test before we call it finished. No client names, no real addresses.

Sample project

100A to 200A, whole-house

The old panel had rust on the bus and slots for barely half of what an addition would need. New 200A panel, permitted and inspected, every circuit tested before the cover went back on.

Sample project

Level 2 charger, detached garage

The garage subpanel had room on paper but not enough real headroom once the load calc was done. Ran a dedicated circuit from the main panel instead, and the charger tests at full rate.

Sample project

Kitchen and living room relight

Recessed cans over the counters, a separate switch for the island pendant, dimmers on the living room circuit. Laid it out on paper first so the switching made sense before any wire got pulled.

Sample project

Breaker tripping, no clear cause

A breaker that tripped maybe once a week, never on a pattern anyone could pin down. Traced it to a shared neutral from an older addition. One afternoon, and the panel's been quiet since.

Sample project

Manual transfer switch, whole panel

A generator that had been living in the garage, run in through an extension cord during the last two outages. Wired a transfer switch to the panel so it powers the house directly. No cords through a window.

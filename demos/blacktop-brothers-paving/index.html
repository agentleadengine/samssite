How a paving job actually runs

Three visits at most, and you'll know the schedule before anything gets booked.

First

We walk the driveway

Call and we'll come look at what's actually under the cracks — not guess square footage over the phone. You get a real quote before anything's scheduled.

Then

We prep, then pave

Old asphalt cut out or cleaned, base repaired if it needs it, then laid and rolled while it's still hot. Edges cut clean against the garage and the walk.

Last

You drive on it again

We tell you the real cure time before we leave — not an optimistic one — and walk the finished driveway with you.

What doesn't change job to job

We show you the base before we cover it

If it needs more prep than a phone quote assumed, you hear that before we pour, not after.

One crew runs the whole job

The people who quote it are the people who pave it — no labor swapped in partway through.

We call off bad-weather days

Asphalt poured too cold or too wet doesn't cure right. We'd rather reschedule than hand you a bad driveway.

Edges get cut, not just rolled to a fade

Where the asphalt meets the garage, the walk or the lawn is a clean line, not a soft taper.

Tired of patching the same crack?

Call and we'll come look at what you've got — no obligation, and no guess made from the truck window.

A few questions people ask first

How long before I can drive on it?

New asphalt usually takes a few days before regular traffic and closer to a year before it's fully cured — we'll give you the real window for your job, not an optimistic one. Fresh sealcoat is normally drivable the next day.

Do you fill cracks before sealcoating, or is that separate?

Cracks get routed and filled first, then the sealcoat goes over the whole surface. If the driveway isn't too far gone, both usually happen in one visit.

What if it rains on the day you're scheduled?

We reschedule rather than pave or seal in the wrong weather — asphalt poured cold or wet doesn't cure right. You'll hear from us ahead of time, not find out from an empty driveway.

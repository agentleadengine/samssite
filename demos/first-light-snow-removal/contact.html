A few things people ask

How deep does it have to be before you plow?

Most contracts trigger around two inches. Tell us if you want a lower trigger for stairs or a steep driveway; it can be set per property.

What if it snows overnight and I need to leave early?

Storm routes run through the night on purpose, so an early morning isn't the exception. If yours is unusual, text the time and we'll see what we can do.

Do you handle ice without new snow?

Yes. A refreeze or black ice call doesn't need a fresh storm behind it, just call or text and we'll get salt down.

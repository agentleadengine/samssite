What's on the truck

Five services, one crew. Driveways and walkways during the storm, salt right after, and a seasonal contract if you'd rather not think about any of it again.

Storm service

Driveway Plowing

Curb to curb, one clean pass, snow banked to the sides instead of blocking the mailbox or the sidewalk. Single driveways, shared driveways, small lots. We come back mid-storm if it's still coming down hard enough to bury the first pass.

When you need it: any storm that leaves enough on the ground to need a blade, roughly two inches and up.
Storm service

Walkway Clearing

Front walk, steps, and the path to the side door, shoveled or blown clear by hand where the plow can't reach. Done in the same visit as the driveway, not scheduled separately and not left for later.

When you need it: included with driveway service, or on its own for walkways, stoops and short paths.
Storm service

Salting & Ice Control

Ice melt down on the driveway and walkway once the plowing's done, so the surface doesn't refreeze overnight into something worse than the snow was. A pet-conscious blend is available on request.

When you need it: right after plowing, and again on its own for a refreeze or black ice with no new snow.
Whole season

Seasonal Contracts

One agreement for the season covers every storm that meets the trigger depth, driveway and walkway both. You stop checking the forecast and start checking your phone for the text that says we've already been by.

When you need it: sign up any time before the first storm; contracts run through the season.
One-time

Storm Callouts

For the property that just needs this one storm handled, no contract required. Same crew, same equipment, priced for the single visit. Good for a one-off deep freeze, a house you're selling, or trying us out before you sign on for a season.

When you need it: anytime, call or text once the snow starts sticking.

How pricing works

Seasonal contracts are priced per property before the season starts. Storm callouts are priced per visit when you call, based on the driveway and how hard it's coming down. No per-inch surcharge sprung on you mid-storm.

Notion AI vs ChatGPT

Choose between permission-aware workspace search and a general assistant with projects and research tools.

My verdict: Pick Notion AI when the source of truth already lives in Notion and connected work apps. Pick ChatGPT when the work spans files, web research, memory, and reusable assistants outside one workspace.

Notion AI honors existing workspace permissions. Its connectors only return content the user can access in the connected app.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 01

Third-party Notion AI connectors require Business or Enterprise, except the personal Gmail connector. Connector availability can change.

Changing28 Sep 2026Source 02

Notion says its LLM providers use zero data retention for Enterprise workspaces. Other plans use 30 days or fewer by default, with documented exceptions.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 03

Notion can export workspace pages as HTML, Markdown, or CSV. Pages the exporter cannot access are excluded.

Checked28 Sep 2026Source 04

ChatGPT plans are Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Go is $8 monthly in the US, Plus is $20, and Pro has $100 and $200 tiers with current availability conditions.

Changing28 Sep 2026Source 05

ChatGPT Business and Enterprise data is not used for training by default. Personal plans use the Data Controls setting.

Run a permissions test

Create two pages with different access groups. Put a harmless code word on each page. Ask both an authorized and unauthorized account for each code word. Repeat the test after removing access. For ChatGPT, run the equivalent test with the connected source or uploaded project files you plan to use.

Notion connectors can take time to ingest and permission changes can take time to appear. ChatGPT features and limits vary by plan. Neither product removes the need to review sensitive-source access.

Do this today

  1. Choose three real source types your team uses.
  2. Run the two-page permissions test.
  3. Export one workspace or project and inspect what is missing.
  4. Record retention, training, and admin settings before rollout.

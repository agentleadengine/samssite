Claude vs GPT

The file is still called claude-vs-gpt4 because that is what people search. The real matchup today is Anthropic's Claude 5 family against OpenAI's GPT-6 line.

Both vendors now ship the same shape: a top model, a workhorse, and a cheap fast model, with context windows around a million tokens. Capability is close enough that I choose by ecosystem, effective price on a real workload, and how each model behaves on my own tasks. Last checked 28 Sep 2026.

When to pick Claude

I pick Claude for agentic work, and I should disclose that this site is maintained with Claude Code, so I am not neutral. The reason I keep choosing it is the harness: Claude Code, the Agent SDK, skills, and MCP make it straightforward to give a model tools, memory, and long-running tasks without building the scaffolding myself. Sonnet 5 at $2 / $10 is the price-performance point most everyday work fits, and Opus 5.5 is there when a job is long and hard.

Claude is also my default for writing that has to sound like a person. That is a subjective call, and exactly the kind of thing you should test on your own material rather than take from a comparison page.

When to pick GPT

Pick OpenAI when distribution and ecosystem breadth matter more than any single benchmark. ChatGPT is where many of your clients and employees already are, and the OpenAI SDK is the default in a lot of codebases.

The price spread at the bottom is also wide. Luna at $0.10 in and $0.50 out is a tenth of Haiku 4.5 for high-volume simple jobs like classification and extraction. If your workload is mostly that kind of volume, OpenAI prices it better today.

The models are close enough that the durable difference is the agent ecosystem, not a benchmark. Swapping a model name in an API call is cheap. Swapping an agent harness is not, and that is where the real lock-in lives.

What most comparisons miss

Most pages with this title compare models that no longer matter. GPT-4 versus Claude 3 benchmarks still rank in search results years after both were replaced. Check the date on any comparison before trusting a number, this page included.

Headline per-token prices mislead too. Prompt caching, batch discounts, and routing easy jobs to a cheaper tier change your real cost by multiples. A bill projection needs your actual traffic, not the pricing table.

Both vendors change prices and model names often. Everything in the table above was true on the date shown and should be re-checked before you commit a budget.

My verdict

I do most of my agent work on Claude because the tooling fits how I work. If I were standardizing a company that already lives in ChatGPT, or running a high-volume pipeline where Luna's price wins, I would pick OpenAI and feel fine about it. Run a short test on your own tasks and let that decide, not this page.

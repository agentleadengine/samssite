FAQ


Which CRMs does QUEVA work with?
Send your CRM name with your demo request. I check it before setup and confirm what QUEVA can do in your account.


What should I bring to the demo?
Your CRM name, the first task you'd hand off and a blank copy of any template you want filled. For a cash summary, bring the column names from your weekly CSV.


How does it work with my assistant?
Your assistant keeps the work they already do well. QUEVA covers the checks and admin you still handle yourself, and you choose which tasks it takes.
